Does GTA 6 Have a Performance Mode? Frame Rate on Every Console

Published Nov 11, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

No. Rockstar hasn’t announced a GTA 6 performance mode on any console. In its November 10 Newswire post, Rockstar said the game was built for current consoles and is targeting 30 frames per second on both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That covers the PS5 Pro too. The Pro gets visual extras, not a higher frame rate.

If you were hoping to flip a switch for 60, here’s what is actually known, what isn’t, and what you can do about it.

What Rockstar said about GTA 6 fps

Rockstar’s line, as quoted by Push Square, was that the game is "targeting 30 frames per second while pushing both platforms to their absolute limits." So the official GTA 6 fps target at launch is the same everywhere:

  • PS5: 30fps target.
  • PS5 Pro: 30fps target.
  • Xbox Series X: 30fps target.
  • Xbox Series S: covered by the same "both platforms" statement. Rockstar didn’t break it out separately.

Rockstar didn’t list a 40fps or 60fps option for any of them. It also didn’t publish resolution numbers for any console, so if you see a site quoting an exact resolution, ask where it came from.

GTA 6 performance on PS5 and PS5 Pro

The PlayStation versions got the longest feature list in Rockstar’s post. On a standard PS5, Rockstar listed:

  • Haptic feedback on the DualSense controller, adjustable in the accessibility settings.
  • Spatial audio using the PS5’s Tempest 3D Audio.
  • Improved load times.

The PS5 Pro adds two things that Rockstar said are only available on that console:

  • PSSR, Sony’s PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution upscaling, for a sharper image.
  • Ray-traced shadows.

That’s the whole list. Clearer picture, better shadows, same 30fps target. If you bought a Pro specifically for a 60fps GTA, that’s not what Rockstar announced.

On Xbox, the extra Rockstar mentioned was Xbox Cloud Gaming: if you own the game, you can stream it to an Xbox One or Series X|S at Series S graphics settings.

Will GTA 6 have a performance mode later?

Rockstar hasn’t announced one as of November 16, 2026. Here’s the honest state of the rumors:

  • Rob Nelson’s comment. Rockstar North’s co-studio head said after the Extended Look that 30fps was the target on all consoles, and outlets reported him saying a 60fps mode for the PS5 Pro was still being discussed internally. That’s a hint at most, not a promise.
  • The "60fps build" quote. An interview that went live early on TheGamer and was then pulled had Rockstar saying it once had a PS5 Pro build at 60fps, but only by cutting cars and most of the NPCs’ heads. Notebookcheck read it as a joke, and so do we.
  • Leaked store metadata. A reported 120Hz listing for PS5 Pro has been floating around. Rockstar hasn’t confirmed it, and a listing isn’t a feature.

So the answer to "will GTA 6 have performance mode" is: nobody outside Rockstar knows. If one arrives, it will be in a patch, and Rockstar’s patch notes are where you’ll see it first. Don’t trust a YouTube thumbnail with "60FPS CONFIRMED" in red letters.

Why 30fps, anyway?

Rockstar’s framing is that it’s pushing both consoles to their limits, and the world it has shown is dense: crowds, traffic and detail everywhere in Vice City. Open-world games this busy lean hard on the console’s processor, not just the graphics chip, and a faster graphics chip alone doesn’t fix that. That’s why the Pro’s extras are about image quality rather than frame rate.

Red Dead Redemption 2 launched at 30fps on consoles too, and people still played it for a few hundred hours.

How to get the smoothest 30fps

You can’t change the target, but you can make 30 feel better:

  1. Turn on your TV’s game mode. It cuts input lag, which matters more at 30fps than people think.
  2. Turn off motion smoothing. TV "motion" settings add lag and odd artifacts.
  3. Check the in-game options. Rockstar games usually have motion blur and camera settings worth tweaking.
  4. Keep the console ventilated. A hot console in a closed cabinet is never a good idea.

The short version for each console

If you’re choosing a console for GTA 6, frame rate isn’t the tiebreaker, since the target is the same. The Pro looks a bit sharper. The Series S has the smallest drive.
