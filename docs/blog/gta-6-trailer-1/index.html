GTA 6 Trailer 1 Explained: What Rockstar Showed in 2023

Published Nov 12, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

GTA 6 Trailer 1 came out on December 4, 2023, a day earlier than Rockstar planned, because it leaked. It ran about a minute and a half, introduced Lucia, showed off a modern Vice City, and pointed to a 2025 release on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It also broke YouTube records within a day. The game itself arrives almost three years later, on November 19, 2026, but that first trailer is still where most people’s picture of GTA 6 started.

Why the first trailer dropped a day early

Rockstar had scheduled the GTA 6 first trailer for the morning of Tuesday, December 5, 2023. On Monday, December 4, a copy leaked on X. Rather than let a low-quality copy do the rounds, Rockstar put the real thing up on YouTube right away and posted, in short, that the trailer had leaked and fans should watch the real one on YouTube.

It was a very Rockstar moment: years of near silence, then a leak forcing their hand by less than a day. The studio had already been through a much bigger leak in September 2022, when development footage was stolen and posted online. We don’t cover that footage, but it explains why everyone was jumpy.

The GTA 6 trailer 1 views and records

The numbers were big enough that Guinness World Records got involved. Per Guinness, as reported by Push Square:

  • 90,421,491 views in 24 hours. That made it the most-viewed video game reveal on YouTube in its first day.
  • Most-viewed non-music YouTube video in 24 hours. It passed MrBeast’s previous record of 59.4 million.
  • Most-liked video game trailer in 24 hours, with about 8.9 million likes.

Music videos are a separate category. BTS’s "Butter" still held the overall 24-hour record. TechCrunch noted Rockstar’s own post about the leak was seen more than 80 million times on X, which says something about how many people were refreshing their feeds that night.

The view count kept climbing for months after. Trailer 2, released in May 2025, didn’t beat Trailer 1’s first-day record.

What the GTA VI trailer showed

A short trailer, but dense. The main beats:

  • Lucia in prison. It opens with Lucia in an orange jumpsuit talking to a prison officer. Rockstar’s later character page says she landed in the Leonida Penitentiary for fighting for her family, and got out by luck.
  • A robbery with a partner. Lucia and a masked man rob a convenience store. That partner turned out to be Jason Duval, the other lead, though Rockstar didn’t name him in this trailer.
  • Vice City, modern day. Beaches, nightclubs, boats, traffic, crowds. Not the 1980s neon of the 2002 game, but a modern city with phones everywhere.
  • Social media clips. A big chunk of the trailer is fake vertical videos and livestreams of strange behavior around the state, a clear wink at real-life "Florida Man" internet culture.
  • Leonida. Swamps, the Keys, small towns and wildlife made it obvious the map went well beyond one city.
  • The music. The trailer was set to Tom Petty’s "Love Is a Long Road" from his 1989 album Full Moon Fever. Rockstar trailers often use songs that aren’t in the game, so this was never a soundtrack confirmation. We won’t quote the lyrics, but you know the one.
  • The end card. "Coming 2025," with PS5 and Xbox Series X|S logos.

What changed after Trailer 1

The 2025 date didn’t hold. In May 2025, Rockstar moved the game to May 26, 2026. In November 2025, it moved again, to November 19, 2026, saying the extra months would let the team finish with the polish players expect. Trailer 2 in May 2025 named Jason properly and came with official character profiles. Then came the Extended Look gameplay video on Netflix in August 2026.

The platforms stayed the same the whole time. It was PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on the first end card, and it is still those two for launch. There’s no PC version announced; see our PC answer.

Is Trailer 1 still worth watching?

Yes, mostly as a time capsule. It sets the tone Rockstar’s later footage kept: two partners, a sunny state with a dark side, and an internet-brained version of Florida.
