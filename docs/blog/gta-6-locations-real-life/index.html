GTA 6 Locations vs Real Florida, Including Jason’s House

Published Nov 13, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

Jason’s house in GTA 6 doesn’t have one real-life address. Rockstar says Jason lives rent-free in a property owned by Brian, an old smuggler in the Keys, and fans who matched the trailers against Florida place it in the Leonida Keys, a stand-in for the real Florida Keys. That’s the pattern for the whole map: Leonida is Rockstar’s Florida, built from real places but rearranged, renamed and squeezed to fit a game. Here’s how the pieces line up, and which matches are official versus fan work.

Leonida is Florida, officially

Rockstar is not shy about this one. The game is set in the state of Leonida, and Rockstar’s own copy calls it the sunshine state and pitches the story as the dark side of America’s sunniest state. Nobody needs a detective board for that.

Rockstar also did its homework. Rob Nelson, Rockstar’s head of development, told Dazed the team spent years in Miami and the surrounding area, meeting former criminals, police, club promoters and gun dealers, among others. So the places feel lived in, even when the names are made up.

Vice City vs Miami

Vice City has been Rockstar’s Miami since 2002, and GTA 6 keeps it that way. The neon, the beachfront hotels, the causeways and the downtown towers in the trailers are Miami in spirit. Rockstar’s Leonida page talks about the neon-soaked streets of Vice City, and Boobie Ike’s business empire (real estate, a strip club and a recording studio) is based there.

What you won’t find is a street-for-street copy. Rockstar compresses cities. Landmarks get moved closer together, neighborhoods get merged, and the names change. Fans have spent two years matching shots from the trailers to real buildings, and plenty of those matches are convincing, but none of them are Rockstar-confirmed.

The Keys, and Jason’s house

The Leonida Keys are Rockstar’s version of the Florida Keys, and this is Jason’s world. According to Rockstar:

  • Jason grew up around crooks, served in the Army, then worked for drug runners in the Keys.
  • He lives rent-free in one of Brian Heder’s properties, in exchange for helping with local shakedowns.
  • Brian is an older drug runner who still smuggles through his boat yard with his wife, Lori.

So what about the GTA 6 Jason house in real life? Fans building the "State of Leonida" map place it in an area they call Key Lento, near the southern edge of the map. The name and setting have been compared to Key Largo, the biggest island in the real Keys. That’s fan mapping, not a Rockstar statement, and Rockstar redesigns real places rather than copying them. The house in the game is a composite, not a real person’s home.

Which brings up the obvious point. If a video claims to have found "Jason’s real house" with a street address, don’t go there. It’s somebody’s actual home, and Rockstar never said it modeled the house on any specific property.

The Everglades and Grassrivers

Grassrivers is the region most people link to the Everglades: swamp, sawgrass, airboats and wildlife. The match comes from fan mappers and guides, and the trailers’ marsh footage makes the case on its own. Rockstar itself hasn’t published a "this is the Everglades" statement. It just named the place Grassrivers and let the alligators do the talking.

Small towns and the rest of the state

The site’s fact base lists six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. The last three are harder to pin down:

  • Port Gellhorn is described in fan guides as a worn-down coastal town. Fans have floated Gulf Coast and Panhandle comparisons, but there’s no confirmed real-world twin.
  • Ambrosia reads as the rural interior. Again, no official match.
  • Mount Kalaga National Park is the odd one out. Florida doesn’t have real mountains, so this is Rockstar stretching the map beyond what Florida can supply.

Rockstar’s Leonida page links each of these to characters but doesn’t describe them in detail, so be wary of anyone claiming a confirmed one-to-one match.

Comparing GTA 6 to real life yourself

Rockstar said it would post a free printable Leonida map on its website on release day, giving you an official look at the whole state. If you want to do your own GTA 6 vs real life comparison, put that next to a real Florida map once it’s up and look at the shape of the coastline and the Keys. You’ll see what Rockstar kept and what it rearranged.
