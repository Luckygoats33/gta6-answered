GTA 6 vs The Witcher 4: The Two Biggest Open Worlds Ahead

Published Nov 16, 2026.

GTA 6 vs The Witcher 4 isn’t a fair fight yet, because only one of them has a release date. GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The Witcher 4 has no release date: CD Projekt Red said in August 2026 that it is targeting 2028. What you can compare is what each studio has shown and said, and they’re aiming at very different kinds of open world.

Where each game stands

GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026. Rockstar announced it with a trailer in December 2023, delayed it twice (from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, then to November 19, 2026) and is launching it as a single-player game. GTA 6 Online hasn’t been announced.

The Witcher 4 was revealed at The Game Awards in December 2024 with a nearly six-minute cinematic trailer and no gameplay. Ciri is the lead this time, taking over from Geralt. CD Projekt said through 2025 that the game wouldn’t arrive before 2027, and in August 2026 joint CEO Michał Nowakowski said on CD Projekt Red’s X account that the target is 2028. A target isn’t a date, and CD Projekt has slipped before.

Platforms compared

GTA 6’s platforms are settled:

  • PlayStation 5 and PS5 Pro.
  • Xbox Series X and Series S.
  • No Xbox One version, but owners will be able to stream it to an Xbox One through Xbox Cloud Gaming, at Series S settings.
  • No PC version announced.

The Witcher 4’s platforms haven’t been announced. The one hardware detail CD Projekt has shared came from its Unreal Engine 5 tech demo in June 2025, which ran on a base PS5 at 60 frames per second with ray tracing on. CD Projekt was careful about it: the studio said it was a showcase of tools, not actual gameplay, and that it was too early to confirm specs for every platform. CD Projekt’s past games launched on PC, so a PC version seems likely, but the studio hasn’t said so.

That’s one area where the two differ sharply. GTA 6 is skipping PC at launch. The Witcher series has always been at home on PC.

Different engines, different approaches

Rockstar builds its games on its own in-house technology. CD Projekt dropped its own REDengine for The Witcher 4 and moved to Unreal Engine 5, working directly with Epic Games. The reveal trailer was rendered in a custom build of Unreal Engine 5, and the June 2025 tech demo showed off Epic’s tools for dense forests, horse movement and town crowds.

The frame rate story is different too. Rockstar confirmed GTA 6 targets 30 fps on every console, including the PS5 Pro. The Witcher 4’s tech demo ran at 60 fps on a base PS5, but that demo wasn’t the game. Until CD Projekt shows real gameplay and confirms targets, comparing frame rates means comparing a confirmed target with a tech demo.

Open world scale

Rockstar says Leonida is the biggest map it has built and roughly twice the size of GTA V’s. It’s a modern state based on Florida, with Vice City at its center, six regions, more than 170 animal species and a weather system with hurricanes.

The Witcher 4’s world is still mostly unknown. The tech demo took place in Kovir, a northern region the games had never visited before, including the snowy capital, Lan Exeter, and a town called Valdrest. CD Projekt hasn’t said how big the map is, how many regions there are, or whether it’s one open world or several large areas like The Witcher 3.

So "which is bigger" doesn’t have an answer yet. Even when it does, size matters less than density. GTA 6 is built around one connected, busy modern state. The Witcher games are built around quests and story in a fantasy world. Different jobs.

What each studio has shown

  • Rockstar (GTA 6): two trailers, a 27-minute Extended Look of in-game PS5 footage on Netflix in August 2026, a Launch Trailer in October 2026, with the full game following on November 19, 2026.
  • CD Projekt Red (The Witcher 4): a cinematic reveal trailer in December 2024, and an Unreal Engine 5 tech demo in June 2025 that the studio said isn’t meant to represent the final game.

That gap is the real comparison. One game is about to move from promises to something you can play. The other is still at the "here’s what our tools can do" stage.

Should you wait for The Witcher 4?

No reason to. They’re different kinds of games, and The Witcher 4 is at least a year away by CD Projekt’s own target. If you want GTA 6, it’s out on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
