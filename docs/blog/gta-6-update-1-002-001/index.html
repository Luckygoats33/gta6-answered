GTA 6 Update 1.002.001: Second Pre-Launch Patch, Size and Rollout

Published Nov 17, 2026.

GTA 6 update 1.002.001 started rolling out on November 16, 2026, three days before the game unlocks. It is the second update pushed to pre-loaded copies, it is small, and Rockstar has not published patch notes for it. If your console already has the pre-load, this is one more download to let finish before midnight on November 19.

Here is what multiple outlets have reported, what is still unknown, and how to make sure your console has it.

How big is the update

The numbers are consistent across the reports we checked:

  • PS5: about 602.5 MB.
  • Xbox Series X|S: about 1.59 GB to 1.6 GB, depending on which outlet you read.

So the Xbox file is roughly 1 GB larger than the PS5 one. Nobody outside Rockstar has explained why. Sportskeeda says plainly that there is no information on the reason for the difference, and that is where it stands.

Either way, this is tiny next to the game itself. Rockstar put the full pre-load at up to 212 GB, depending on the console. If you had room for that, you have room for this. If you are still clearing space, our file size page walks through checking storage on both consoles.

What the version number means

On PS5 the update shows as version 01.002.001. Techtroduce reports that Xbox lists the same build as 1.0.2.1. They are the same update with different labels, so don’t worry if your friend on the other console reads you a number that doesn’t match yours.

Why you might not have it yet

On PS5, the update is going out in stages instead of hitting every console at once. One report puts the first wave at around a third of PS5 owners. That means two people who pre-loaded on the same day can see different things in their download queue right now, and neither console is broken.

If yours hasn’t shown up:

  1. Give it time. Staggered rollouts usually reach everyone within a day or so.
  2. Check by hand. On PS5, highlight GTA 6, press Options, and choose Check for Update. On Xbox, open My games and apps, highlight GTA 6, press the Menu button, choose Manage game and add-ons, then Updates.
  3. Turn on automatic updates so you don’t have to think about it. Our post on the GTA 6 day one patch and updates has the exact menu path for both consoles.

What does the update change

This is the part nobody can tell you for sure. Rockstar has not released patch notes for 1.002.001. No official list of fixes exists, and Rockstar’s Newswire and support pages are where one would appear if it did.

You will see confident claims on social media about what the patch contains. Some dataminers say it changes existing game files and doesn’t add new content. We couldn’t confirm that against anything Rockstar has published, so treat it as a claim, not a fact. The same goes for anyone saying this update "unlocks" the game early or adds a performance mode. Nothing Rockstar has said supports either idea. The 30 fps target Rockstar confirmed on November 10 for PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S still stands.

What a small update three days before launch usually means is simple housekeeping: the studio is still polishing, and it would rather you download fixes now than on launch night. That is our read, not Rockstar’s statement.

Do you need it to play

Plan on it. Consoles normally ask you to install the latest update before a game will start, and at least one outlet describes this one as mandatory. Since the game doesn’t unlock until midnight local time on November 19, there is no downside to installing it now and every reason not to leave it for the last minute.

The practical checklist for the next three days:

  • Make sure the base pre-load has finished. The pre-load page covers how to confirm that.
  • Install update 1.002.001 when it reaches your console.
  • Leave automatic updates on in case Rockstar pushes another one before launch. This is the second pre-launch update, so a third would not be a surprise.
  • Check your unlock time on the release time page. It is midnight in your own time zone, not one global moment.

Will there be a day one patch too

Possibly, but Rockstar hasn’t announced one. A pre-launch update and a day one patch are the same kind of thing with different timing: one arrives before the unlock, the other on or after it. Two updates in the week before launch tell you Rockstar is still shipping fixes, which makes another update around November 19 plausible. Plausible is not confirmed. Any size you see quoted for a "day one patch" right now is a guess.

What we’ll update

We’ll revise this post if any of the following happens:

  • Rockstar publishes patch notes for 1.002.001.
  • The download sizes change as the rollout finishes.
  • A third pre-launch update appears.
  • Rockstar announces a day one patch with a size.

Until then, the short version: the update is real, it is about 602 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox, it is rolling out in waves, and nobody outside Rockstar knows exactly what is in it. Download it, leave auto-updates on, and you are ready for midnight.
