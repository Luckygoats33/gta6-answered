Every Song in the GTA 6 Trailers, From Tom Petty to Justice

Published Nov 12, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

Each GTA 6 trailer song is a different flavor of Florida. The first trailer used Tom Petty’s "Love Is a Long Road," the second used The Pointer Sisters’ "Hot Together," and the Launch Trailer debuted a new Justice single, "Flower in a Hurricane." The long Extended Look gameplay video was wall to wall licensed music too, though Rockstar never published an official track list for it. Here’s each one, in order, with what’s actually confirmed.

Trailer 1: Tom Petty, "Love Is a Long Road"

Rockstar posted the first trailer on December 4, 2023, ahead of schedule because it had leaked online. The song is "Love Is a Long Road" by Tom Petty, from Full Moon Fever, his 1989 solo album.

It was a smart pick. It’s not one of Petty’s big radio hits, which made it feel like a deep cut you’d hear on a car stereo in the Keys. Petty grew up in Gainesville, Florida, which fans were quick to point out, though Rockstar never said that was the reason.

Trailer 2: The Pointer Sisters, "Hot Together"

The second trailer arrived on May 6, 2025, and its main track is "Hot Together" by The Pointer Sisters, a mid-1980s song. Where Petty’s track was moody and wide open, this one is slinky and glossy, which suits a trailer built around Jason and Lucia as a couple. Rockstar said at the end of the trailer that the footage was captured on PS5.

The Launch Trailer: Justice, "Flower in a Hurricane"

The third trailer, which Rockstar calls the Launch Trailer, came out on October 15, 2026. Rockstar’s own page credits the music as the debut of "Flower in a Hurricane" by Justice, featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard.

That’s a big change in approach. The first two trailers used older licensed songs. This one premiered a brand new track, and Justice, the French electronic duo, is also the composer of the game’s original score. So if you’re searching for the "GTA 6 theme song," the closest official answer is Justice’s work. Rockstar hasn’t named a single track as the game’s theme.

Two related releases are worth knowing:

  • Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album. "Flower in a Hurricane" is on it. Vice reported the album has 34 original tracks inspired by Vice City and Leonida.
  • "Pusher." Justice released a second single, featuring Gesaffelstein, on October 29, 2026, tied to the game’s original score. It wasn’t the music for a trailer.

The Extended Look: a whole radio dial

The Extended Look premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026, and Netflix lists it at 27 minutes. It’s the closest thing to hearing GTA 6’s radio before launch, because cars in the footage are playing music the whole time.

Rockstar didn’t release an official song list for it. Rolling Stone Canada identified a dozen tracks by ear, including:

  • Phil Collins, "Against All Odds (Take a Look at Me Now)"
  • Depeche Mode, "People Are People"
  • Def Leppard, "Love Bites"
  • Cliff Richard, "Devil Woman"
  • Birdman and Lil Wayne, "Pop Bottles"
  • Kodak Black, "Skrilla"
  • Sexyy Red and Tay Keith, "Pound Town"
  • Prefab Sprout, "Cars and Girls"

The mix runs from 1970s soft rock to current Florida rap, which is about as Vice City as it gets. Treat these as identified songs, not a confirmed in-game tracklist. A song showing up in a preview doesn’t promise it’s on a particular station in the final game.

The intro song question

People also search for the "GTA 6 intro song." Rockstar hasn’t revealed what plays over the game’s opening, and we don’t describe story content here. Once the game is out on November 19, the official sources for the music that plays in game will be the in-game radio stations and Rockstar’s own soundtrack releases.

Listening without getting scammed

All of these songs are on the normal streaming services. Rockstar’s Justice releases went out on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music and YouTube. You don’t need a "GTA 6 soundtrack download" site, and those are a good way to pick up malware.

For the trailers themselves, Rockstar’s YouTube channel and website are the source. Our news page collects official announcements, and the facts page keeps the confirmed details in one place. If you’re more interested in the Netflix video than the music, the Netflix page explains what it was and what it wasn’t.

One house rule: we don’t print lyrics. Go listen to the songs instead. They’re better with the music anyway.
