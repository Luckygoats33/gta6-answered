GTA 6 Not Downloading or Won’t Start? Fixes for PS5 and Xbox

Published Nov 10, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

If GTA 6 is not downloading or won’t start, the cause is almost always one of five boring things: not enough free space, a paused or stuck download queue, a license the console can’t check, a boxed code that was never redeemed, or a console that just needs a proper restart. GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and every launch of a game this size produces the same handful of problems. Work through the list below in order. Most people are fixed by step two.

Step 1: check your storage first

GTA 6 is big. Rockstar said the download needs up to 212 GB depending on your console, and the PlayStation Store page asks for at least 212 GB free on PS5. If you’re even a little short, the download will refuse to start or stall near the end. The full breakdown is on our file size page.

  • PS5: Settings, Storage, Console Storage. Delete or move games until you clear the space with some room to spare.
  • Xbox Series X|S: Settings, System, Storage devices. The Series S has the smallest drive, so it hits this wall first.

Patches add to the total over time, so leave a cushion rather than squeezing in at exactly 212 GB.

Step 2: GTA 6 stuck in the download queue

A download that sits at 0% or freezes partway is the most common "gta 6 stuck" complaint.

  • PS5: Open the Downloads list from the Control Center. Pause the GTA 6 item, wait a few seconds, then resume it. If it still won’t move, cancel it, delete what was downloaded, and start again from your Game Library.
  • Xbox: Go to My games & apps, then Manage, then Queue. Make sure GTA 6 isn’t paused and isn’t stuck behind other downloads. Xbox Support also says to check that you’re signed in and that you have enough space.

On either console, a wired connection is faster and steadier than Wi-Fi for a download this size. Pause other downloads and streaming in the house while it runs.

Step 3: the game won’t start (licenses)

If GTA 6 finished downloading but shows a padlock or says you can’t play it, the console can’t confirm you own it.

  • PS5: Make sure you’re signed in to the account that bought the game. Then go to Settings, Users and Accounts, Other, Restore Licenses. PlayStation Support notes this doesn’t touch your saved data. You can also select the game, press Options and choose Restore License for just that one title.
  • PS5, part two: Turn on Console Sharing and Offline Play for your main console. PlayStation’s support page suggests this if license problems keep coming back.
  • Xbox: Sign in with the account that bought the game and check your Home Xbox setting. Your home Xbox is the console where your digital games can be played without checking your license online each time, so set it to the console you actually use.

If it says the game isn’t available yet, check the clock. The game goes live at midnight in your own time zone on November 19, so if your region hasn’t reached midnight, nothing is wrong. Our launch times page lists when each region unlocks.

Step 4: boxed copy owners, redeem the code

The boxed Standard Edition has no disc. It holds a download code, so a "gta 6 install xbox" or PS5 problem is often just an unredeemed code. See our disc page for the details.

  • PS5: Open PlayStation Store, press Triangle, pick the More menu, then Redeem Code, and type the 12-digit code. You can also do it in a browser at store.playstation.com.
  • Xbox: Use the Redeem option in the Microsoft Store on your console, then install from your library.

On PlayStation, the code has to match the region of your account. If redeeming fails, check the small printed region flag and your account country before calling support.

Step 5: restart properly, then update

A full restart clears a lot of launch-night strangeness.

  • PS5: Use the power menu and pick Restart, not Rest Mode.
  • Xbox: Hold the power button on the front for about 10 seconds until the console shuts off, then turn it back on. Microsoft says this doesn’t erase games or saves.

After that, check for a system software update on both consoles, and let any GTA 6 update finish before launching. A half-installed patch is a classic "gta 6 not working" cause.

Pre-order error and other account problems

If you see a "gta 6 pre order error" or the game never showed up in your library, confirm the purchase went through in your PlayStation or Microsoft account’s transaction history. If you bought from a retailer or Rockstar’s own store, that seller handles refunds and missing codes, not Sony or Microsoft.

When it’s not you

Sometimes the problem is on the server side. Check the PlayStation Network or Xbox status pages before you start deleting things. If a service is down, waiting is the fix. For any GTA 6 problem that survives all of the above, check Rockstar’s support site for known issues and patch notes rather than random videos promising a magic fix.

One last warning: nobody needs your password to fix a download. Ignore anyone offering "GTA 6 unlock tools" or account recovery help in DMs. Those are scams.
