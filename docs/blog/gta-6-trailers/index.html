Every GTA 6 Trailer So Far, Explained

Published Nov 12, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

Rockstar has put out two numbered trailers for GTA 6 ahead of launch: Trailer 1 in December 2023 and Trailer 2 in May 2025. After that came a 27-minute gameplay video called An Extended Look, which premiered on Netflix in August 2026, and a Launch Trailer in October 2026. There has never been an official "GTA 6 Trailer 3." Every one of them is on Rockstar’s YouTube channel, which is the only place you need to watch them.

Trailer 1: December 2023

Rockstar planned to premiere the first trailer on Tuesday, December 5, 2023. It didn’t get the chance. A low-quality copy leaked online on the evening of Monday, December 4, and Rockstar responded by posting the real thing on YouTube hours early, telling fans to watch the official version instead.

What it confirmed:

  • The game is set in the state of Leonida, with Vice City, the series’ take on Miami, as its big city.
  • Two protagonists, one of them a woman, a first for the series. Rockstar later confirmed her name: Lucia.
  • A 2025 release window. That window didn’t hold. Rockstar later moved the game to May 26, 2026, and then to November 19, 2026.

TechRadar reported that Trailer 1 drew 93 million YouTube views in its first day.

Trailer 2: May 2025

The second trailer arrived on May 6, 2025, shortly after Rockstar announced the first delay. It gave both leads a proper introduction, with Rockstar naming them Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, and showed the two as partners in crime.

Rockstar told The Hollywood Reporter that Trailer 2 passed 475 million views across platforms in its first 24 hours, which it called the biggest video launch ever. Keep in mind that number counts every platform. On YouTube alone, Trailer 1 still held the first-day lead.

Trailer 2 also showed far more of Leonida beyond Vice City, from the Keys to the swamps.

The Extended Look on Netflix: August 2026

The longest official look at the game wasn’t called a trailer at all. "Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look" premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026. It runs about 27 minutes, and Netflix says it was captured entirely in-game on PlayStation 5. It went up on Rockstar’s YouTube channel later the same evening.

Netflix describes the story as an easy score that goes wrong and drags Jason and Lucia into a conspiracy. It was also the first time most people saw the game actually being played rather than cut together like a movie: cover shooting, slow-motion aiming, police chases, a six-star wanted level, shop robberies, fan boats, clubs and base-jumping. No, you can’t play GTA 6 on Netflix. That’s a common question, and our Netflix page answers it.

The Launch Trailer: October 2026

Rockstar posted the Launch Trailer on October 15, 2026, without warning, about five weeks before release. It’s short, around a minute and a half, and focuses on Jason and Lucia as a couple, with new music from Justice, who Rockstar named as composers of the game’s original score. It ends on the November 19 date with PS5 and Xbox Series X|S logos.

PlayStation’s DualSense trailer

On November 10, 2026, the day pre-load opened, PlayStation released its own GTA 6 trailer to show off PS5 controller features: weapon recoil through the triggers, extra pressure during weightlifting, and police radio from the controller speaker. There’s no equivalent Xbox-only trailer. It’s a marketing video for PS5, not a new story trailer.

If you’re looking for a "GTA 6 release video," the Launch Trailer is the closest thing.

Is there a Trailer 3?

No. Rockstar has never released anything labeled Trailer 3. For most of 2026 fans counted down to one, and plenty of fake countdown sites and fan-made "Trailer 3" uploads filled the gap. The Extended Look and the Launch Trailer took its place.

If you see a "new GTA 6 trailer" now, check that it comes from Rockstar’s own YouTube channel or the Rockstar Newswire. Fan edits and AI-made "leaks" pick up millions of views on the strength of a thumbnail.

What each one revealed

A quick recap, in order:

  1. Trailer 1 (December 4, 2023): Leonida, Vice City, Lucia, a 2025 target.
  2. Trailer 2 (May 6, 2025): Jason and Lucia together, a wider look at the map.
  3. An Extended Look (August 27, 2026): about 27 minutes of in-game footage on PS5, the first real look at gameplay.
  4. Launch Trailer (October 15, 2026): the final pre-release trailer, with the November 19 date.

The game comes out on November 19, 2026. For the full list of what Rockstar has confirmed ahead of launch, see what to expect in GTA 6 and our facts page.
