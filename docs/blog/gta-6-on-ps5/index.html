GTA 6 on PS5: Launch Time, Size, Editions and PS5 Pro

Published Nov 11, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

GTA 6 runs on every PS5 model, and almost every bit of GTA 6 gameplay Rockstar has shown before launch was captured on PS5, including the Netflix Extended Look. The game comes out on November 19, 2026, unlocking at midnight local time on the PlayStation Store. If you’re picking it up on a PS5, here’s what you’re getting into: a very big download, two editions, and a 30 fps target on every model, PS5 Pro included.

When it unlocks on PS5

Rockstar’s rule is the same everywhere: midnight local time on November 19. Players in New Zealand start first and the US West Coast is among the last. If you buy it after launch, none of that matters anymore. It will be live in every region, and it plays as soon as the download finishes. The release time page has the launch schedule.

How big the PS5 download is

Rockstar put the download at up to 212 GB, depending on the console. On PS5 that’s enough to matter:

  • The original 825 GB PS5 leaves roughly 667 GB for games, so GTA 6 takes close to a third of it.
  • Updates usually make games bigger, not smaller, so leave extra room.

To check space: Settings, Storage. You can move other PS5 games to a USB drive to make room, but you can’t play PS5 games from USB. GTA 6 has to sit on the internal drive or a compatible M.2 SSD.

Pre-load, and buying after launch

Pre-load on PS5 opened at midnight local time on November 10, nine days before release, and it’s still open. If you buy it now, the game downloads ahead of time and unlocks at midnight on November 19. If you buy it after launch, the game simply downloads and you can start once the "playable" portion is in. If you bought a boxed copy, remember there’s no disc in it. The box holds a download code, which you redeem on the PS Store and then download like any digital purchase. The disc page explains why.

Standard vs Ultimate on the PlayStation Store

Two editions are on the PS Store:

  • Standard Edition, $79.99 in the US, as confirmed by Take-Two and Rockstar. Also sold boxed, with a code.
  • Ultimate Edition, $99.99. Digital only.

Ultimate adds extra vehicles, weapons, clothing, shops and locations inside the single-player game. If you start with Standard, you can buy the upgrade later from the store. On PlayStation, the upgrade price shows once you own the Standard Edition. The upgrade page has the details, and the comparison of what’s in each edition is on the Standard vs Ultimate page.

PS5 Pro: what Rockstar confirmed

The PlayStation Store listing carries a PS5 Pro Enhanced label, and on November 10 Rockstar said what that means. Rockstar’s target is 30 fps on every console, PS5 Pro included. The Pro adds PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows. Rockstar hasn’t disclosed rendering resolution, and no 60 fps or performance mode has been announced. A 40 fps mode you may see mentioned is only a datamine rumor.

That lines up with what Rob Nelson, Rockstar’s head of development, said in an earlier interview: the consoles were running the game at 30 frames per second "for now," and a 60 fps option was a question for the technical team. The Extended Look footage was 30 fps. So if you bought a Pro expecting 60 fps GTA 6, nothing Rockstar has announced promises that. Once the game is out, check its own settings menu and Rockstar’s patch notes for anything newer.

What GTA 6 gameplay on PS5 looks like before launch

The best official look is still the Extended Look, which premiered on Netflix in August and was captured entirely on PS5. It showed both protagonists, Jason and Lucia, switching between them on the fly, a six-star wanted level, and a long list of side activities. Rockstar also said the second trailer was captured on PS5.

On the controller side, Sony announced two limited-edition GTA 6 DualSense controllers for November 19, priced at $84.99 in the US. They’re cosmetic. Any DualSense works fine.

Things you don’t need

  • PS Plus. Buying guides report that the single-player game doesn’t need a subscription. GTA 6 launches single-player, and Rockstar hasn’t dated GTA 6 Online.
  • A PS5 Pro. The game runs on the standard and Slim models.
  • A second purchase for a different region. Your copy works on your account.

If you run into trouble with downloads or updates, our earlier post on the GTA 6 day one patch and updates walks through checking for updates and turning on auto-install on PS5.
