How to Buy or Upgrade to the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition

Published Nov 14, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

The GTA 6 Ultimate Edition pre-order window is still open, and it closes when the game comes out on November 19, 2026. Ultimate is easy to get either way. You can pre-order the Ultimate Edition outright from the PlayStation Store or the Microsoft Store, or, if you already own the Standard Edition, buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade as an add-on. Both stores list Ultimate at $99.99 in the US, and Xbox lists the upgrade at $20. The one thing you can’t do is buy Ultimate in a box. It’s digital only.

Buying the Ultimate Edition outright

If you don’t own GTA 6 yet, this is the simplest path.

GTA 6 Ultimate Edition on PS5:

  1. Open the PlayStation Store on your PS5, or go to the store in a web browser.
  2. Search for Grand Theft Auto VI and open the game page.
  3. Choose the Ultimate Edition from the edition list and buy it.
  4. Download it from your Game Library. Make sure you have at least 212 GB free first.

GTA 6 Ultimate Edition on Xbox:

  1. Open the Microsoft Store on your Xbox Series X|S, or use xbox.com.
  2. Find Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition.
  3. Buy it, then install from My games & apps.

The Xbox listing shows the Ultimate Edition as a bundle: the base game, the Ultimate Edition Upgrade, and the Vintage Vice City Pack. PlayStation describes it the same way: everything in Standard plus the upgrade. Our Standard vs Ultimate page covers whether the extra money is worth it.

Upgrading from Standard

Already have Standard? You don’t need to buy the game again. Rockstar confirmed that Standard owners can buy the upgrade at any time.

  • On Xbox, the Ultimate Edition Upgrade is listed as a separate add-on for $20.
  • On PlayStation, the upgrade is listed as an add-on too. Its price only shows once you own the Standard Edition, so check the game’s add-ons page from your own account.
  • Find it on the game’s store page, in the add-ons section.

Xbox’s listing says the extras unlock across the story, so you’re not locked out of them by starting with Standard. The full answer on timing is on our upgrade page.

What you get

The PlayStation Store lists the Ultimate contents in a few groups. In short:

  • Vehicles and garages, including a ’95 Grotti Cheetah, extra vehicles for Jason’s safehouse, and the Paradise Garage in Watson Bay.
  • Weapons, including two revolvers and personalized pistols for Jason and Lucia.
  • Looks, with Vice City style outfits, hairstyles, tattoos, and Goodtime Gear apparel.
  • Commissions and customs, including a classic car collection.
  • Ultimate-only shops and services, such as Rideout Customs and Electric Fang Tattoo, which has tattoos designed by FAILE.

All of it is single-player content. It doesn’t give you anything online, since GTA 6 doesn’t have an online mode yet.

Price on Xbox vs PlayStation

The US prices match on both stores:

  • Standard Edition: $79.99
  • Ultimate Edition: $99.99
  • Ultimate Upgrade: $20 on Xbox. PlayStation’s price for the upgrade isn’t public yet.

Prices differ by country, and store sales can change them. Check the store on your own account before you buy. Our price page has the main numbers.

Boxed copies can’t be Ultimate

The boxed Standard Edition has a download code, not a disc, and there’s no boxed Ultimate. If you bought a box, redeem the code first, then buy the upgrade. That route ends up at the same place: the full Ultimate content on your account.

Does the pre-order bonus still apply?

The pre-order bonus is time-limited, and the cutoffs are close:

  • Xbox says purchases before 23:59:59 on November 19, 2026 get the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+.
  • PlayStation says the GTA+ month needs a digital pre-order placed before November 20, 2026, and has to be claimed by March 31, 2027.

So a purchase after those dates won’t include the GTA+ month. Whether the Vintage Vice City Pack is still bundled with purchases after launch is something to check on the current store listing before you buy, not something to assume. If it isn’t listed on the edition you’re buying, it isn’t included.

One last tip: buy from the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store or a real retailer. "Cheap Ultimate upgrade" codes from random sites are a common way to lose $20 or your account.
