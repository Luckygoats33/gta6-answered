What to Expect in GTA 6: Everything Confirmed Before Launch

Published Nov 10, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

Here is what to expect in GTA 6: it comes out November 19 at midnight your local time, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, as a single-player game starring Jason and Lucia. It costs $79.99 for Standard or $99.99 for Ultimate in the US, the download is up to 212 GB, and the setting is Leonida, Rockstar’s version of Florida. That covers most of the GTA 6 questions people are asking in the last days before launch. The rest of this post fills in the details and flags what still isn’t known.

When can you play GTA 6

The rule is simple: midnight in your own time zone on November 19. New Zealand reaches midnight first, so players there start hours ahead of Europe and the Americas. There’s no early access and no official way around the clock. The full city-by-city list is on our launch times page, and the short version lives at what time GTA 6 comes out.

If you bought digitally, the game should already be sitting on your console. Pre-load opened November 10. On PS5 it opened at midnight local time. On Xbox it opened at midnight GMT, which was the evening of November 9 in the US.

Which consoles it runs on

Rockstar lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That includes the Series S and the PS5 Pro. A few platform details came out on November 10:

  • Every console targets 30 frames per second. Rockstar hasn’t given resolution targets.
  • The PS5 Pro adds PSSR upscaling for a cleaner image and ray-traced shadows that no other console gets.
  • On PS5, the DualSense handles haptics, trigger feedback and police radio through the controller speaker.
  • Xbox One can’t run the game itself, but Rockstar confirmed you can stream it there through Xbox Cloud Gaming, using Series S graphics settings. You still need to own the game.
  • Rockstar says Remote Play is supported on PlayStation and Xbox.

There’s no PC version, no PS4 version and nothing on phones. Anything promising a "GTA 6 mobile download" is a scam.

Editions and price

Take-Two confirmed the US prices in June: $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. Ultimate adds 16 items for the single-player game, including a ’95 Grotti Cheetah, extra outfits and tattoos, and several shops that only Ultimate owners can use. The full breakdown is on our Standard vs Ultimate page.

A few things worth knowing before you pay:

  • Digital purchases made before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store) include the Vintage Vice City Pack.
  • The boxed Standard copy, in stores since November 12, holds a download code, not a disc.
  • Ultimate is digital only. If you buy Standard, you can upgrade later.

Download size and storage

Rockstar says the download is up to 212 GB, depending on your console. That’s a lot for any drive and a real squeeze on the Series S, which has about 364 GB of usable space to start with. Patches after launch usually add more. Check your free space before launch night, not after the countdown hits zero.

The map and the setting

GTA 6 takes place in Leonida, a fictional state modeled on Florida, with Vice City as its Miami. Rockstar’s Aaron Garbut told Game Informer it is the biggest map the studio has built, and Rockstar has described it as roughly twice the size of GTA V’s. Coverage of Rockstar’s previews lists six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.

Rockstar has also talked up a new weather system with hurricanes and strong winds, and more than 170 animal species. Rockstar’s narrative lead said the story won’t walk you through every corner of the map, so some of it is left for you to find. Rockstar plans to post a free printable map of Leonida on its website on release day.

If you want to see it moving, the 27-minute Extended Look that premiered on Netflix in August is the longest look at real gameplay so far. It was captured on a standard PS5.

What isn’t confirmed yet

Plenty of questions don’t have answers, and it’s better to know which ones:

  • GTA 6 Online. The game launches as a single-player experience. Rockstar hasn’t announced a date for multiplayer. "2027" comes from Bloomberg reporting, not from Rockstar.
  • PC. Rockstar hasn’t announced a PC version or a date for one.
  • Game Pass. It’s reported as not coming to Game Pass at launch. You buy it outright.
  • Resolution and extra modes. Rockstar has said 30 fps. It hasn’t said anything about resolution, a 60 fps mode or a 40 fps mode.
  • Story DLC. Nothing announced.

Anyone who tells you otherwise is guessing. Our rumors page tracks the claims that keep circulating and where each one actually comes from.

The short version: clear your storage, pre-load if you haven’t, and plan to start at your own midnight on November 19.
