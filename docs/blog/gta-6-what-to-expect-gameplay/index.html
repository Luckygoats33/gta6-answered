What’s in GTA 6: How It Looks and Plays, Per Rockstar

Published Nov 13, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

If you’re wondering what will be in GTA 6, the most reliable answer is what Rockstar itself has shown before release: two playable leads, Jason and Lucia, a Florida-style state called Leonida, a six-star wanted system, and a long list of side activities, all captured on PS5. The game comes out on November 19, 2026. This is a guide to what Rockstar has put on the record before launch, not a review, so you know what has been promised and what’s still an open question.

Graphics from the PS5 footage

The best look is the Extended Look, a gameplay video that premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026. Netflix lists it at 27 minutes and says it was captured entirely from in-game footage on PS5. That matters, because everything you see in it is console footage, not a high-end PC render.

A few things stood out in the official material:

  • Dense crowds. Rockstar told Dazed it built new tools to create its most varied population of characters yet.
  • Real street art. More than 50 street artists designed murals for the game.
  • A lived-in Miami. Rockstar said its team spent years researching Miami and the area around it.

Frame rate is the catch. Rockstar confirmed on November 10, 2026 that its target is 30 fps on every console, PS5 Pro included. The Pro adds PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows, not extra frames. Rob Nelson, Rockstar’s head of development, had said earlier that a 60 fps option was something the technical team would have to weigh in on, but no 60 fps or performance mode has been announced. If you’re coming from 60 fps shooters, expect the cinematic pace of Red Dead Redemption 2 rather than a twitchy shooter.

The open world

The state of Leonida is Rockstar’s version of Florida. The site’s fact base lists six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Rockstar has reportedly put the map at about twice the size of GTA V. Our map size page covers exactly what was said.

The Extended Look took in Vice City, the bayous and forests outside it, a downtown rooftop bar and an outdoor gun range. Rockstar also said player actions ripple outward: walk down a street carrying a rifle and people react.

Two protagonists

This is the biggest structural change. GTA 6 is built around a couple:

  • Jason Duval grew up around crooks, served in the Army and then worked for drug runners in the Keys.
  • Lucia Caminos has just come out of Leonida Penitentiary and wants a better life.

Nelson told Dazed the story is styled after Bonnie and Clyde, with their relationship at the center. You can swap between them freely in the open world and in some missions, though Rockstar has said some missions switch for you or lock you to one character. On the way to a job, you can switch between driving and the passenger seat, where you can use your phone. And you can play as the pair or go solo and cause chaos on your own.

Rockstar also said exercise visibly builds muscle, food affects weight, and long stretches without sleep, police chases or multi-day benders take a toll on the characters.

What else the footage showed

From the Extended Look and the coverage around it:

  • A six-star wanted level, one more than GTA V, plus a Criminal Profile system that responds to what you do.
  • New carjacking. A slim-jim style minigame for older cars. Cars with electronic locks need a key cloner.
  • Combat tweaks. Swapping between two guns without the weapon wheel, easier weapon pickups, and some one-handed weapons.
  • Talking your way out. Dialogue options let you taunt people or try to defuse a situation.
  • Things to do. Basketball, dirt bike races, scuba diving, target practice, stock car racing, kayaking, fishing, a bench press and backyard wrestling.
  • A social feed. A livestream-style feed in the spirit of GTA’s old Lifeinvader parody.

What’s still unknown

Some of the biggest questions haven’t been answered before launch. As of November 16, 2026, Rockstar hasn’t announced:

  • A date for GTA 6 Online. The game launches single-player. Late 2027 comes from a Bloomberg report, not from Rockstar. See the online page.
  • A PC version. It’s PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. The PC page tracks this.
  • Resolution. Rockstar hasn’t disclosed rendering resolution for any console, the PS5 Pro included.
  • Story expansions. Nothing announced.

Once the game is out on November 19, you’ll find out a lot of this by playing. Just be careful with what you read online in the meantime. Clips claiming "hidden features" are often fan edits or rumors, and nothing beats Rockstar’s own patch notes for what’s officially changed.
