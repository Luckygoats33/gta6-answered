Is GTA 6 the Biggest Rockstar Map Ever?

Published Nov 13, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

Yes, GTA 6 is huge, and by Rockstar’s own account it’s the biggest map the studio has made. Aaron Garbut, Rockstar’s art director, told Game Informer that Leonida is its largest world "by some degree," and Rockstar has described it as about twice the size of GTA V’s map. What Rockstar hasn’t done is publish an official area in square miles, so every exact figure you see online is someone’s estimate.

Rockstar’s comparison to GTA V

The cleanest claim is the one Rockstar repeated in both the Netflix Extended Look material and Game Informer’s September 2026 cover story: Leonida is roughly double the size of GTA V’s Los Santos and Blaine County. GTA V’s map was already large for its day, so doubling it is a big step.

Rockstar’s narrative lead, Rupert Humphries, added a detail that tells you more than any number. He told Game Informer the story’s job is to take you around the map, but the team couldn’t take you everywhere. In other words, the main missions won’t show you all of it. Some of Leonida is left for you to find on your own.

How it stacks up against GTA IV, GTA V and RDR2

Two other comparisons have circulated, and they come from different places:

  • Three times Red Dead Redemption 2. This came from YouTuber TGG, who visited Rockstar North before the Extended Look premiere and relayed what head of development Rob Nelson told him: roughly twice GTA V and about three times RDR2. That’s Rockstar’s word, passed on secondhand.
  • Close to GTA IV, GTA V and RDR2 combined. This one traces back to Rockstar comments in an Edge magazine interview, and outlets have worded it differently, from "nearly as large" to "bigger than." Treat it as a rough comparison, not a measurement.

Put together, the message is consistent. Leonida is much bigger than anything Rockstar has shipped. The exact multiplier depends on who’s doing the math and whether water counts.

Size vs density

A map can be big and empty. Rockstar’s pitch for GTA 6 is that it’s big and full. That’s harder to measure, but the official footage gives some idea:

  • Vice City is the dense core, with nightlife, beaches, apartment blocks and a downtown.
  • The rest of Leonida stretches into the Keys, swamps, at least one national park and a port town. Coverage of Rockstar’s previews names six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.
  • Wildlife and weather fill the gaps. Rockstar says Leonida has more than 170 animal species and a weather system with hurricanes and strong winds.

Garbut also told Game Informer the team was obsessed with every part of the world and wanted it to feel like one connected place rather than a set of zones. That’s a goal, not a measurement, but it’s the right goal. GTA V’s countryside had long, quiet drives. Whether Leonida’s open stretches feel alive is the kind of thing you only learn by playing.

Is GTA 6 the biggest open world ever?

Probably not, and that’s fine. Some games have far larger maps on paper, often because they’re procedurally generated or made mostly of empty terrain. Comparing GTA 6 to those by square miles alone misses what Rockstar is going for, which is a world built by hand, block by block.

The fair comparison is with other hand-built open worlds, and there Rockstar is claiming the top spot for itself. Without an official area figure, nobody can settle the ranking. If Rockstar ever publishes one, our map size page will have it.

How to see the whole map

Rockstar has said it will post a printable map of Leonida on its website on release day, November 19, 2026, as a free download, alongside a printable cover poster. A double-sided physical poster is also slated for the Rockstar Store at $0.99, or free with any order.

That’s the official route. Fan-made interactive maps are fine for planning a route, but check them against the game once you can, since early ones are often built from trailer screenshots.
