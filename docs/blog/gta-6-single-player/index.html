Is GTA 6 Single-Player Only? What Launches on Day One

Published Nov 13, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

Yes. GTA 6 is single player only for now. When it comes out on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, it launches as a story game about Jason and Lucia with no online multiplayer mode. The PlayStation Store lists it as one player with no multiplayer modes and offline play enabled, and the Xbox Store calls it a single-player experience. GTA 6 Online isn’t part of the launch, and Rockstar hasn’t announced when it will be.

If you came from GTA V, where GTA Online took over everyone’s life for a decade, that can feel strange. It shouldn’t. GTA V did almost the same thing.

Story mode at launch

What you get on day one is the full single-player game:

  • Two playable leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, with a story that runs across the state of Leonida.
  • The open world, from Vice City to the Keys and the wetlands. Our earlier Leonida guide walks through the regions.
  • Edition extras, if you buy them. The Ultimate Edition’s vehicles, weapons, outfits and locations are single-player items, unlocked as you go through the story.

There is no lobby, no free mode with strangers and no co-op with a friend. Everything happens on your console, in your save.

No Online date yet

Rockstar hasn’t announced a date or even a window for GTA 6 Online as of November 16, 2026. What exists is press reporting:

  • Bloomberg reported that multiplayer is expected in 2027. Vice’s summary of that reporting reads it as late 2027, about a year after launch. That’s a report, not a Rockstar statement.
  • Take-Two’s CEO, Strauss Zelnick, said on a May earnings call that it was too early to talk about the online business model.
  • Rockstar hasn’t officially announced a GTA 6 multiplayer mode at all.

History helps here. GTA V came out in September 2013, and GTA Online followed a couple of weeks later as a separate launch. Red Dead Redemption 2 launched in October 2018, and Red Dead Online arrived as a beta the next month. So a gap between the story and the online mode is normal for Rockstar. A gap this long would be new, but nothing about it means Online is cancelled. Our online page tracks any updates.

What that means for crossplay

With no multiplayer, there’s nothing for PS5 and Xbox players to share, so crossplay isn’t missing so much as not applicable. Our post on GTA 6 crossplay covers what GTA Online did in the past (PlayStation and Xbox never shared sessions) and what to look for when Online is announced.

No subscription needed for single-player

You don’t need PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass to play the GTA 6 story. Neither store page lists a membership as a requirement for the single-player game, and PlayStation lists offline play as supported. You’ll still want an internet connection for the download, updates and some optional features, which the store pages mention may need a Rockstar Games account.

Two things that confuse people:

  • GTA 6 isn’t on Game Pass. You buy the game outright. See our Game Pass page.
  • The GTA+ month isn’t GTA 6 Online. Digital pre-orders come with one month of GTA+, Rockstar’s paid membership. It’s a pre-order perk, not a sign that GTA 6 has an online mode. On PlayStation the month has to be claimed by March 31, 2027, and it renews as a paid subscription unless you cancel.

Is GTA 6 single player worth it on its own?

That’s your call, but the math is simple. If you bought GTA 5 for the story and the world, GTA 6 at launch is that, bigger. If you bought GTA 5 for Online with friends, you’re waiting, and nobody outside Rockstar knows for how long.

What’s next

Watch for three things from Rockstar, in roughly this order:

  1. Story updates and patches, which show up in Rockstar’s patch notes.
  2. An official GTA 6 Online announcement with a date and platforms.
  3. Details on whether Online needs PS Plus or Game Pass, and whether it supports crossplay.

Until Rockstar says otherwise, the answer stays the same: GTA 6 is a single-player game, and a full one. Enjoy the quiet while it lasts.
