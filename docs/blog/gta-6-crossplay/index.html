Does GTA 6 Have Crossplay? PS5 and Xbox Explained

Published Nov 11, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

No, GTA 6 crossplay isn’t a thing, and at launch it can’t be. GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026, as a single-player game on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, so there will be no online mode for PlayStation and Xbox players to share. The PlayStation Store lists it as one player with no multiplayer modes, and the Xbox Store calls it "a single-player experience." Crossplay only matters when people play together, and at launch nobody in GTA 6 will be playing together.

That answers today’s question. The better question is what happens when GTA 6 Online shows up, and for that the series’ history is the best guide we have.

Is GTA 6 cross platform at all?

Not in any way that connects the two consoles. Here’s how the pieces fit:

  • The story mode is the whole game for now. You play Jason and Lucia’s story alone, on your own console.
  • No GTA 6 Online yet. Rockstar hasn’t announced a date for it as of November 16, 2026. Our online page tracks what’s known.
  • No announced cross-save. Rockstar hasn’t said your PS5 story save can move to Xbox or the other way around. Treat your purchase and your progress as tied to one platform.
  • No PC version. Rockstar hasn’t announced one, so there’s no PC side to connect to either. See our PC page.

One small wrinkle: Microsoft said people who own GTA 6 on Xbox can play it through Xbox Cloud Gaming at launch, including streaming it to an Xbox One. That’s the same Xbox version running on Microsoft’s servers, not crossplay with PlayStation.

What GTA Online did: no crossplay between PlayStation and Xbox

If you’re wondering "will GTA 6 be crossplay" once Online arrives, look at the last 13 years. GTA Online never let PlayStation and Xbox players into the same sessions.

  • Console families stayed apart. PlayStation players played with PlayStation players. Xbox players played with Xbox players. PC was its own world.
  • Even generations split. When the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions of GTA Online arrived in March 2022, they couldn’t play with the PS4 and Xbox One versions. GTABase notes that the only way to mix generations was running the older backward-compatible version on a new console.
  • Progress moved, people didn’t. Rockstar offered one-time character transfers to newer platforms at various points. That’s moving your stuff, not playing across platforms.

So the honest track record is: Rockstar has never shipped GTA crossplay between PS and Xbox. That doesn’t mean it never will. Plenty of big online games added it over the last few years. But nothing Rockstar has said points either way for GTA 6.

GTA 6 crossplay Xbox PS5: what the reports say

The only real reporting on GTA 6 multiplayer is about timing, not features. Vice summarized Bloomberg reporting that multiplayer is expected in 2027, possibly about a year after launch. That’s a press report, not a Rockstar announcement, and Rockstar hasn’t officially announced the multiplayer mode at all. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said on a May earnings call that it was too early to talk about the online business model.

Nobody credible has reported whether GTA 6 Online will support crossplay. If you see a video claiming it’s "confirmed," check whether it links to Rockstar. It won’t.

What this means for buying

If you’re picking a console based on where your friends play, think about it this way:

  • For the story, it doesn’t matter. Your friends can’t join you anyway.
  • For a future Online, history says you’d want the same console family as your crew. If you buy on PS5 and your friends are on Xbox, assume you won’t share sessions unless Rockstar says otherwise.
  • For saves, don’t plan on switching platforms later and keeping your progress. Nothing announced supports that.

The consoles page covers which machines run the game natively.

What to watch for

The crossplay answer will come with the GTA 6 Online announcement, whenever Rockstar makes it. Watch Rockstar’s Newswire and the Rockstar Support site, not leak accounts. The things worth checking on that day:

  1. Which platforms Online launches on.
  2. Whether PS5 and Xbox share sessions.
  3. Whether your character or progress can move between platforms.
  4. Whether you need a PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass membership to play online, which has been the rule for most console multiplayer.

Until then, the short version holds: GTA 6 is a one-player game, so crossplay isn’t missing. It just isn’t relevant yet.
