GTA 6 Trailer 2 Explained: Jason, Lucia and Leonida

Published Nov 12, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

GTA 6 Trailer 2 is the one that turned Jason and Lucia from two faces in a car into actual characters. Rockstar dropped it without warning on Tuesday, May 6, 2025, captured on PS5, and followed it the same day with a big update to the game’s website: character bios, locations and a pile of screenshots. GTA 6 itself comes out on November 19, 2026, but if you want to know where the story’s setup was first laid out, it’s here.

When the second trailer dropped, and why the timing mattered

Four days before Trailer 2, on May 2, 2025, Rockstar had announced the game’s first delay, from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026. The trailer landed in the middle of that disappointment, and it worked. Rockstar said it pulled more than 475 million views across all platforms in its first day and called it the biggest video launch of all time. That’s Rockstar’s own count, not an independent one, but even YouTube’s public number alone was huge.

(The May 2026 date didn’t hold either. Rockstar moved the game again in November 2025, to November 19, 2026, the date it launches on.)

How it was captured

The end of the trailer says the footage was captured on PlayStation 5. Rockstar described it as roughly equal parts gameplay and cutscenes. That’s worth remembering when people argue about whether a shot was "real gameplay": Rockstar never claimed all of it was.

The music is "Hot Together" by The Pointer Sisters, and Spotify saw a huge jump in streams of it after the drop. If you’re tracking the songs across every trailer, our trailer song post has the list.

Jason and Lucia

The trailer splits its time between the two leads, and Rockstar’s website filled in the rest.

  • Jason Duval grew up around grifters and crooks, did a stint in the army, and ended up in the Keys working for local drug runners.
  • Lucia Caminos is introduced fresh out of Leonida Penitentiary. Her backstory runs through Liberty City, where she grew up, and her mother, and her fights (she’s a kickboxer) are part of how she ended up inside.

The point of the trailer is the two of them together: two leads who work as a pair, a different shape from GTA V’s three separate protagonists.

The supporting cast from the GTA 6 second trailer

The website update put names to faces that flash past in the trailer. Going by Rockstar’s bios as reported at the time:

  • Cal Hampton, Jason’s friend, who likes his criminal schemes from the comfort of his couch
  • Boobie Ike, a former petty criminal turned businessman with real estate, a strip club and a recording studio
  • Dre’Quan Priest, a dealer trying to make it in music, who runs the Only Raw label
  • Real Dimez, a rap duo signed to Dre’Quan, chasing a second shot at success
  • Raul Bautista, a seasoned and reckless bank robber always looking for the next score
  • Brian Heder, a boatyard owner and veteran smuggler who has Jason handle shakedowns and lets him live rent-free in one of his properties

How big each of these roles is in the finished game, Rockstar hasn’t said, and this post won’t guess.

What it showed of Leonida

Trailer 2 is a tour of the state. It names Vice City, the Keys where Jason worked, and the penitentiary Lucia walks out of, and Rockstar sells Leonida as the sunniest place in America with a darker side. The game’s six regions, as Rockstar later described them, are Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Our map size answer has the scale Rockstar has given.

GTA 6 trailer 2 reaction, then and now

The reaction was mostly about two things: how good it looked on a base PS5, and how much more story it gave away than Trailer 1. Some viewers took the graphics as a promise about frame rate or resolution. It wasn’t one. Rockstar’s actual answer came on November 10, 2026: its target is 30 fps on every console, PS5 Pro included, with rendering resolution not disclosed.

Looking back with launch days away, Trailer 2 holds up best as a character introduction. It tells you who Jason and Lucia are, who they owe, and what part of the map they come from. If you skipped it, it’s still worth a watch before you play on November 19, but go in knowing it’s a trailer, edited to sell, not a slice of a mission.
