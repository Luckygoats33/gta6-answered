GTA 6 by the Numbers: Price, Size, Map and Launch Time

Published Nov 16, 2026.

The two GTA 6 numbers most people need are price and size: $79.99 for Standard or $99.99 for Ultimate in the US, and a download of up to 212 GB depending on your console. Everything else on this page is the supporting cast. Each number comes with its status, because "Rockstar said" and "someone online said" are not the same thing.

$79.99 and $99.99

  • $79.99: Standard Edition, US list price. Confirmed by Take-Two and Rockstar.
  • $99.99: Ultimate Edition, digital only, also confirmed.
  • $20: the gap between them, and the listed price of the Ultimate upgrade on Xbox. On PlayStation the upgrade price shows only to Standard owners. Standard owners can upgrade any time.
  • £69.99 and £89.99: the UK prices. €79.99 and €99.99 in the eurozone, as reported at pre-order launch.
  • $84.99: Sony’s recommended price for each of the two limited-edition GTA VI DualSense controllers.

Our price by country post has the rest of the world.

Up to 212 GB

  • 212 GB: Rockstar’s maximum download size, "depending on your device." Patches usually add to it over time, so leave room.
  • About 364 GB: usable space on a 512 GB Xbox Series S, so GTA 6 takes more than half.
  • 0 discs: the boxed Standard copy holds a download code, not a disc.

The file size answer shows how to check your free space.

About twice GTA V’s map

  • 2x: Rockstar’s Rob Nelson told YouTuber TGG the world is about twice the size of GTA V’s.
  • 3x: and about three times Red Dead Redemption 2’s.
  • 2x again: Vice City alone, he said, is about twice the size of GTA V’s Los Santos.
  • 0 official square miles. Rockstar hasn’t published an area figure, so any exact number you see is a fan estimate.

Six regions

6 regions make up Leonida, Rockstar’s version of Florida: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. The map size answer covers them.

Midnight local, November 19

  • November 19, 2026: launch day, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
  • 12:00 am: the unlock time, in your own time zone.
  • 18 hours: how far ahead New Zealand (11:00 UTC on November 18) is of New York (05:00 UTC on November 19).
  • November 10: pre-load opened. November 12: boxed copies hit stores.
  • 2: platforms. No PC version announced, no PS4 and no phones. There’s no Xbox One version either, but if you own GTA 6 you can stream it to an Xbox One through Xbox Cloud Gaming.

16 Ultimate extras

16 items come with Ultimate, all for the single-player game. They include the ’95 Grotti Cheetah, the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, engraved pistols for Jason and Lucia, a ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, a Shitzu Squalo boat, five Ultimate-only shops and the Classic Car Collection side activity. Electric Fang Tattoo alone has 50+ tattoos by the artists FAILE. The Standard vs Ultimate answer lays out the split: Standard vs Ultimate.

A few more numbers

  • 30fps: Rockstar’s frame-rate target on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. The Pro adds PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows. No 60 fps or performance mode has been announced.
  • 2: playable leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.
  • 90,421,491: Trailer 1’s YouTube views in its first 24 hours in December 2023, a Guinness record for a non-music video.
  • 2015: the year development started, per Rockstar North’s Rob Nelson. Most of the team joined after 2018.
  • 13 years: from GTA V in 2013 to GTA 6 in 2026.
  • 2 delays: from the original 2025 window to May 26, 2026, then to November 19, 2026.
  • 0 dates for GTA 6 Online. Rockstar hasn’t announced one as of November 16, 2026.

Numbers we don’t have

Some numbers get quoted everywhere without a source. As of November 16, 2026, Rockstar hasn’t published any of these, so treat them with suspicion:

  • Sales figures. Take-Two reports sales in its earnings, not Rockstar’s Newswire. Wait for the official report rather than a viral screenshot.
  • Budget. You’ll see billion-dollar figures. Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed one.
  • Hours to finish. Estimates of story length are guesses until players log them.
  • Resolution. Rockstar gave a frame-rate target, not pixel counts, for any console.
  • A PC date. Not announced.
