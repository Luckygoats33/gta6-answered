GTA 6 Mods: What Rockstar’s New Rules Allow

Published Nov 14, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

Will GTA 6 have mods? Rockstar hasn’t announced mod support for GTA 6 as of November 16, 2026, and the game comes out on November 19, 2026, only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, where traditional mods don’t run. What Rockstar has done is rewrite its mod rules for all its games just before launch, and it has owned the team behind FiveM since 2023. So the honest answer is "not now, and if it happens, on Rockstar’s terms."

Here’s what each piece actually says.

Mods aren’t confirmed for GTA 6

Modding in GTA has always been a PC thing. GTA V’s huge mod scene, from silly car packs to roleplay servers, lives on PC. GTA 6 has no PC version. Rockstar hasn’t announced one, and on consoles you can’t install files the store didn’t sell you.

That means anything sold to you as a "GTA 6 mod" for PS5 or Xbox is either a fake, a scam, or a cheat tool that will get your account in trouble. Our PC answer covers the PC status, and our PC release post goes into what’s been said and what hasn’t.

GTA 6 mods news: Rockstar’s updated mod guidelines

In September 2026, a few weeks before launch, Rockstar updated its modding guidelines. They apply to Rockstar’s games in general, not just GTA 6, and they’re stricter than the free-for-all many PC players remember. Going by MP1st’s summary of the rules:

  • No rewriting the story. Mods can’t expand or change Rockstar’s official storylines, missions or characters.
  • No mixing games. You can’t bring assets from one Rockstar game into another, so no dropping GTA 6 cars into GTA V or the other way round.
  • No unofficial platforms. Mods can’t be used to run a game on hardware or platforms where Rockstar hasn’t released it. That rules out "GTA 6 on PC" ports.
  • Multiplayer only through Rockstar. Multiplayer mods have to run through Rockstar’s licensed Creator Platform or another license Rockstar grants. Cheat menus and anything that interferes with official online services are banned.
  • Non-commercial by default. No selling mods unless the Creator Platform allows it. No real-money gambling, loot boxes, currency sales or crypto.

Rockstar also makes clear the guidelines aren’t a license and it doesn’t endorse third-party mods. Read the actual rules on Rockstar’s site before you build anything.

Rockstar owns FiveM (Cfx.re)

In August 2023, Rockstar announced it had acquired Cfx.re, the team behind FiveM and RedM. Those are the platforms that run custom multiplayer and roleplay servers for GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 on PC. Rockstar said at the time that it had expanded its mod policy to cover the roleplay community and would work with Cfx.re to support it.

The new guidelines’ "Creator Platform" language fits that. My read: Rockstar would rather run community multiplayer than fight it. What it hasn’t said is whether GTA 6 will ever get the same treatment. Owning FiveM is a strong hint about Rockstar’s direction, not a GTA 6 announcement.

The Project ROME rumor

In September 2025, a post on X claimed Rockstar was working on "Project ROME," said to stand for Rockstar Online Modding Engine, a way for players to build their own content. Vice covered it as a rumor and pointed out the original claim tied it to GTA V, not GTA 6.

Rockstar has never confirmed Project ROME exists. Treat it as a rumor until Rockstar says otherwise. Our rumors page keeps the verdict current.

Console vs PC mods

This is where expectations go wrong, so here’s the plain version.

  1. On console, there are no mods. Not on PS5, not on Xbox. If Rockstar ever adds creator tools to GTA 6 on console, expect them to come built into the game through an official update, not as files you install.
  2. On PC, there’s nothing to mod yet. No PC version means no PC mods. When a PC version comes, if it does, the new rules will already be in place.
  3. Online is its own thing. Rockstar hasn’t announced a date for GTA 6 Online. Anything that touches online play will have to go through Rockstar’s platform under the new rules. Our online answer tracks that.

What to watch for

A few signs would tell you mods are coming: a PC version announcement, a Creator Platform update that names GTA 6, or creator tools showing up in a GTA 6 update. Until then, skip anything sold as a GTA 6 mod menu. Those are cheat tools, they break Rockstar’s rules, and the most likely result is a banned account, not a better game.
