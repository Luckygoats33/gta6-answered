GTA 6 Ultimate Edition Content: All 16 Extras, Listed

Published Nov 14, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

The GTA 6 Ultimate Edition content is 16 extra items on top of the full game: vehicles, weapons, clothing, shops, a few special locations and one side activity. Rockstar listed them when pre-orders opened in June 2026, and they unlock as you progress through Jason and Lucia’s story, all in the single-player game.

Ultimate costs $99.99 in the US against $79.99 for Standard, so you’re paying $20 for this list. Here’s every item, grouped so you can judge whether it’s worth it to you.

Vehicles in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition

Four of the 16 items put something new in your garage or on the water:

  1. ’95 Grotti Cheetah: a mid-90s sports car with a retro livery.
  2. ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy: an off-road buggy, stored at the Paradise Garage (see locations below).
  3. Jason’s Safehouse Vehicles: a camo-tinted Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest kayak.
  4. Shitzu Squalo: a pink and blue boat docked at Washington Beach, which comes with a crate of explosive weapons.

There’s also a Ganado Retro Build, a modkit of exclusive parts for Jason’s Vapid Ganado pickup. It upgrades a truck you’d have anyway rather than adding a new one.

Weapons

Two items:

  • Hawk and Little Morgan Revolvers: a matched pair of his and hers revolvers with Vice City styling, including palm tree grips, engraving and a scope.
  • Personalized Variants: engraved versions of Jason’s Girardi ES9 and Lucia’s Klose K17 pistols.

These are cosmetic and personal more than a different class of weapon, going by Rockstar’s descriptions. Rockstar hasn’t published stats for them.

Clothing and looks

Two items:

  • Vice City Style: outfits, tattoos and more for both characters.
  • Goodtime Gear: clothing and accessories inspired by Macca the Gator, a character from the game.

And several of the shops below are about looks too.

Shops and locations

This is where most of the Ultimate value sits. Rockstar marked the five shops as Ultimate Edition only:

  • Rideout Customs: a mod shop for interiors, rims and donk-style builds.
  • One-Eyed Willie’s: a mod shop in Lake Leonida for off-road builds and hand-painted artwork.
  • Sara’s Unisex Salon: hairstyles for both characters, facial hair for Jason, makeup and nails for Lucia.
  • Stock 305: a clothing store with exclusive streetwear.
  • Electric Fang Tattoo: more than 50 tattoos designed by the artist collective FAILE.

Plus one location with a function:

  • Paradise Garage in Watson Bay, which comes with the Dominator Buggy and has a weapon locker and a spot to fence stolen goods.

Activities

Two items here:

  • PTT YOUNGIN$ Illegal Goods Store: a gang compound you can raid for special items and contraband.
  • Classic Car Collection: a special commission for the collector Wyman, recovering and restoring abandoned classic cars.

Count it all up and you get the 16: four vehicle items, the modkit, two weapon items, two looks items, five shops, the illegal goods store and the car collection. (The Paradise Garage arrives as part of the Dominator Buggy item.)

What the Ultimate Edition doesn’t include

Just as important as the list:

  • No early access. Ultimate players start at the same local midnight on November 19, 2026 as everyone else.
  • No disc or boxed version. Ultimate is digital only. The boxed Standard copy holds a download code, and Standard owners can buy the Ultimate upgrade digitally.
  • No online content. Rockstar hasn’t announced a GTA 6 online mode, so none of this is sold as online gear.
  • No extra story missions in Rockstar’s list. The Classic Car Collection is a side commission, and the rest are items, shops and places.
  • Not the pre-order bonus. The Vintage Vice City Pack (’55 Vapid Stanier and garage, outfits and a weapon pattern) comes with digital purchases of either edition made before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store). It isn’t Ultimate-only.
  • Not the GTA+ month. That is a pre-order perk on the PlayStation and Microsoft stores, and it applies to GTA Online, not GTA 6.

Is the content worth $20?

If you care about customization, the five exclusive shops are the real draw, since they add whole categories of looks and car mods. If you mostly play for the story and drive whatever’s parked nearest, you can skip it, and if you change your mind later, Standard owners can upgrade to Ultimate at any time.
