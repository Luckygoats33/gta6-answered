GTA 6 on PS4: Why It Isn’t Coming and Your Upgrade Options

Published Nov 14, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

Can you play GTA 6 on PS4? No. Rockstar is releasing GTA 6 on November 19, 2026, for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and there’s no PS4 version, no PS4 disc, and no download that runs on a PS4. If you’re on a PS4, the only official way to play is a PS5 (or an Xbox Series X or S). Here’s why, and how to pick the right upgrade without overspending.

The short answer: no

Rockstar has listed two platforms since it dated the game: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That leaves out the PS4 and PS4 Pro completely: no version, no cloud streaming, nothing. GTA 6 has no Xbox One version either, but if you own it you can stream it to an Xbox One through Xbox Cloud Gaming. There’s no equivalent for PS4. It also leaves out PC and phones, at least for now.

The reason isn’t a secret. GTA 6 was built for current hardware. Rockstar’s target is 30 fps on every console, PS5 Pro included. The download alone is up to 212 GB. A 2013 console with a spinning hard drive isn’t in the picture.

You may see listings or videos claiming a "GTA 6 PS4 version." They’re fake. So are "PS4 patches" that promise to make it work. Don’t download anything, and don’t put your PSN login into a site that promises one. Our consoles page has the short version for sharing with friends who keep asking.

PS5 models compared

All three PS5 models run GTA 6. The differences are storage, the disc drive, and power:

  • Original PS5 (2020). 825 GB drive, with roughly 667 GB usable for games. Came in a disc version and a Digital Edition. GTA 6 takes close to a third of that space.
  • PS5 Slim (2023). A smaller body and a 1 TB drive. The disc drive can be added or detached depending on the model.
  • PS5 Pro (2024). A 2 TB drive and a stronger GPU. No disc drive in the box.

For GTA 6 specifically, the disc drive doesn’t matter. There’s no GTA 6 disc. The boxed copy holds a download code, so a Digital Edition plays it exactly the same. The disc page explains how that works.

PS5 Pro and the Enhanced label

The PlayStation Store listing for GTA 6 reportedly carries a PS5 Pro Enhanced label. Rockstar spelled out what that means on November 10, 2026: the Pro keeps the same 30 fps target as the other consoles, and adds PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows. Rockstar hasn’t disclosed rendering resolution for any console.

So the fair way to look at it: a Pro gives you a sharper, better-lit picture and the most storage, but not a higher frame rate. No 60 fps or performance mode has been announced. If that changes, it’ll be in Rockstar’s patch notes and the game’s settings menu, not in a store badge.

Storage on PS5

Whichever model you get, plan for space:

  • GTA 6 is up to 212 GB, and big games grow with updates.
  • You can move PS5 games you aren’t playing to a USB drive to free room. You can’t play PS5 games from USB, though. They have to be copied back first.
  • An M.2 SSD in the expansion slot adds space that can actually run PS5 games.

If you’re also bringing over your PS4 library, that’s a bonus: most PS4 games run on PS5, and PS4 games can be played from a USB drive. That’s a good use for the old external drive you already own.

Is it worth upgrading for GTA 6?

That depends on what you play besides GTA 6. A few honest points:

  • If GTA 6 is the reason, a standard PS5 or Slim is enough. That’s the console Rockstar captured its footage on.
  • The Pro is the most power and storage, and in GTA 6 that buys PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows, not more frames. It’s the same 30 fps target.
  • The Series S is the budget route if you don’t care about PlayStation. It runs GTA 6, but its drive is small, so check your space.
  • Don’t buy a PS4 copy of anything GTA 6 related. It doesn’t exist.

One more thing. There’s no PS4-to-PS5 upgrade for GTA 6, because there was never a PS4 version. And if you’re thinking about trading in your PS4, back up your saves for other games first. Most PS4 saves can be moved to a PS5 through cloud saves or a local transfer.

For the price of the game itself, see the price page. If you’ve just bought a PS5 for it, our earlier post on GTA 6 on PS5 covers editions, the download size and the PS5 Pro picture in more detail.
