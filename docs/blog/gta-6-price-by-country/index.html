GTA 6 Price by Country: UK, Canada, Australia, India and More

Published Nov 15, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

Here’s the GTA 6 price comparison in one line: $79.99 and $99.99 in the US, £69.99 and £89.99 in the UK, €79.99 and €99.99 in the eurozone, and noticeably cheaper in India. The US prices were announced by Take-Two and Rockstar, so they’re confirmed. The other regions are the Standard and Ultimate list prices as reported when pre-orders opened in June 2026. Stores run sales, so the price you see on your console may be lower, but it shouldn’t be higher.

All prices below are for the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions. There’s no PC version to compare; see our PC answer.

US prices: $79.99 and $99.99

Rockstar announced the GTA 6 price in the USA as $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate. US store prices don’t include sales tax, which gets added at checkout depending on your state. The $20 gap is the same as the Ultimate upgrade price listed on Xbox. Our price answer has the short version.

UK and Europe

  • UK: £69.99 Standard, £89.99 Ultimate.
  • Eurozone: €79.99 Standard, €99.99 Ultimate.

UK and EU prices include VAT, which is why £69.99 isn’t a straight currency conversion from $79.99. Shops sometimes sell the boxed Standard copy below list, so it’s worth comparing a retailer against the digital store.

Canada

  • Canada: C$109.99 Standard and C$139.99 Ultimate, as reported by iPhone in Canada when pre-orders went live.

Like the US, Canadian prices are before tax.

Australia and New Zealand

  • Australia: A$129.95 Standard, A$159.95 Ultimate, with GST included.
  • New Zealand: we couldn’t verify New Zealand list prices from a reliable source, so check the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store in your region.

JB Hi-Fi, EB Games, Amazon and Target were among the Australian retailers listed for pre-orders, alongside the PlayStation Store and Xbox Store.

India and Pakistan

  • India: ₹5,999 Standard and ₹7,499 Ultimate, per Esquire India.
  • Pakistan: we couldn’t confirm official store prices for Pakistan. Prices depend on which regional store your account uses, and we won’t guess.

India is one of the cheapest places to buy GTA 6 at list price. Khaleej Times worked out that ₹5,999 is about 233 dirhams, well below the UAE price.

Middle East and Africa

  • UAE: Khaleej Times reported the UAE store price as $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate, the same as the US list price, which it put at about 294 and 367 dirhams.
  • Other Middle Eastern and African countries: we haven’t verified those. Many smaller markets use stores priced in US dollars or euros, so check your own store.

Why prices differ by store

A few reasons, none of them a conspiracy:

  1. Taxes. UK, EU, Australian and Indian prices include tax. US and Canadian prices add it at checkout.
  2. Regional pricing. Publishers set prices per market based on what people there can pay. Khaleej Times put India’s lower price down to that kind of local pricing, rather than a straight currency conversion.
  3. Currency swings. Prices get set months ahead. Exchange rates move, list prices mostly don’t.
  4. Retailer discounts. Boxed copies at shops can go below list, especially around holidays.

Can you buy it cheaper from another country?

Not in a way we’d recommend. Your console account is tied to a region, and switching or buying in another store can break the store’s rules. PS5 boxed codes are also region locked: a code only redeems on a PlayStation account from the same region. Xbox codes aren’t region locked, but buying through grey-market key sites carries its own risks. Buy from your own store or a real retailer in your country.
