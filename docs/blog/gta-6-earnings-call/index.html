Take-Two Earnings Calls and GTA 6: What Each One Told Us

Published Nov 16, 2026.

Every GTA 6 earnings call from Take-Two, Rockstar’s parent company, has been watched like a trailer drop, because that’s where the big news kept landing: one delay announced alongside the results, several confirmations of the date, and the first hard numbers on what Take-Two expects the game to earn. Here’s each one that mattered, in order, with what was actually said. Take-Two’s fiscal year runs April to March, which is why "fiscal 2027" covers the November 2026 launch.

May 2025: the first delay lands first

The first delay didn’t wait for the call. In early May 2025, Rockstar announced GTA 6 was moving from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, shortly before Take-Two reported its results. That set the pattern: Rockstar announces, then Take-Two explains on the call. The new date held through the summer 2025 call.

November 2025: the second delay

On November 6, 2025, the game moved again, to Thursday, November 19, 2026. Rockstar posted the news, and it came with Take-Two’s quarterly results. Rockstar apologized for the wait and said it needed the extra months for polish.

On the call, CEO Strauss Zelnick said Take-Two remained excited and confident in Rockstar, and still expected record net bookings in fiscal 2027. He admitted moving a date is painful, but said the company has never regretted doing it in hindsight. Take-Two’s shares fell sharply after hours.

February 3, 2026: date confirmed, marketing in summer

This was the "is it slipping again?" call, and the answer was no. Zelnick said GTA 6 would launch November 19 and called it the lead event of fiscal 2027. He said Rockstar’s marketing would begin in the summer, and the company would be judicious about how it spent it.

On numbers, CFO Lainie Goldstein said detailed fiscal 2027 guidance would come with the May results.

May 2026: the $8 billion outlook

The fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 report kept the November 19 date and finally put a number on the year. Take-Two guided for fiscal 2027 net bookings of $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion, about 20% growth, plus operating cash flow of more than $1 billion. Zelnick called fiscal 2027 a breakout year and argued a big GTA launch would be good for the whole games industry.

Pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026, a month later.

August 7, 2026: holding steady

The first-quarter fiscal 2027 report on August 7 reaffirmed the November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S and reiterated the $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion net bookings outlook. Quarterly net bookings were $1.39 billion, slightly above Take-Two’s own guidance. Later that month, Rockstar released the Extended Look on Netflix.

November 9, 2026: the last call before launch

The second-quarter fiscal 2027 results came out on November 9, 2026, ten days before launch. Per Shacknews, revenue was $1.60 billion against expectations of $1.66 billion. The headline for players was what Zelnick said about GTA 6: pre-orders had exceeded the company’s expectations. Reports said he declined to raise the full-year outlook before the game was actually out, preferring not to celebrate early. Take-Two didn’t publish a pre-order total.

What the calls don’t tell you

Earnings calls are for investors. They’re useful for dates and big-picture numbers, but there are limits:

  • No budget. Take-Two has never disclosed what GTA 6 cost. Our earlier post on the GTA 6 budget covers where the estimates come from.
  • No gameplay news. Trailers, features and the Online date come from Rockstar, not from the call.
  • No GTA 6 Online date. As of November 16, 2026, Rockstar hasn’t given one. See the online page.
  • No PC date. Zelnick has said Rockstar starts on console. See the PC page.

The next GTA 6 update from a Rockstar earnings call

The next report covers October to December 2026, which means it’s the first one that includes GTA 6’s launch. Take-Two hasn’t announced the date as of November 16, 2026. For reference, the same report a year earlier came on February 3, 2026, and Take-Two usually announces the date about a month ahead on its investor relations site. If it has already happened by the time you read this, that’s where the official release and webcast live.

When it comes, the things worth listening for are simple: any sales figure Take-Two chooses to share, whether the full-year outlook changes, and anything about GTA 6 Online or a PC version. Treat everything else as commentary. We’ll put the confirmed parts on the news page.
