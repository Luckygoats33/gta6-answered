Is the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition Worth $20 More?

Published Nov 12, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

Is the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition worth it? For most people, it’s a nice-to-have, not a must. The $20 gets you 16 extra items in the single-player game: cars, boats, guns, outfits, shops and a couple of side activities. It doesn’t get you early access, a disc, or more story. And since Standard owners can upgrade later, there’s no penalty for waiting.

Here’s the long version, so you can decide with your own wallet.

What $20 buys you

In the US, Standard is $79.99 and Ultimate is $99.99, prices Take-Two and Rockstar confirmed. Rockstar describes the Ultimate Edition as premium vehicles, weapons, apparel and activities tied to Jason and Lucia’s story. The 16 items, as listed when pre-orders opened in June 2026:

  • Vehicles and boats: a ’95 Grotti Cheetah, a ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy kept at the Paradise Garage in Watson Bay, Jason’s Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest kayak, a retro modkit for Jason’s Vapid Ganado, and a Shitzu Squalo boat.
  • Weapons: the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, plus engraved pistols for each lead (a Girardi ES9 for Jason, a Klose K17 for Lucia).
  • Shops you can only use in Ultimate: Rideout Customs (a mod shop), One-Eyed Willie’s (an off-road mod shop in Lake Leonida), Sara’s Unisex Salon, the Stock 305 clothing store and the Electric Fang Tattoo shop, which has over 50 tattoos by the artist duo FAILE.
  • Looks: Vice City Style outfits and tattoos, and Goodtime Gear apparel.
  • Activities: the PTT YOUNGIN$ Illegal Goods Store gang compound, and a Classic Car Collection, a commission to restore abandoned classic cars.

MP1st reported that this content unlocks across the story rather than all at once.

What you don’t get

This is where people get tripped up, so it’s worth being blunt:

  • No early access. Ultimate unlocks at the same midnight as Standard.
  • No disc. Ultimate is digital only. The boxed Standard copy has a download code, not a disc, either.
  • No extra story missions announced. The main story is the same game.
  • Nothing for GTA 6 Online. Rockstar hasn’t announced Online yet, and the Ultimate list is all single-player content.

You can upgrade later

Standard owners can buy the Ultimate upgrade any time. It’s listed at $20 on Xbox, which makes the total the same $99.99 as buying Ultimate up front. On PlayStation, the upgrade price only shows once you own the Standard Edition, so check your own store. Our upgrade answer has the details.

That one fact does most of the work. You can play 20 hours of Standard, decide you want Rideout Customs, and pay the difference then. You lose nothing by waiting except whatever sale might come along.

One thing that won’t wait: the pre-order bonus. The Vintage Vice City Pack (’55 Vapid Stanier, a Shore Court garage, outfits and a tropical weapon pattern) is for digital orders placed before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store). If you buy before then, Standard or Ultimate, you’re inside that window. After that, it closes.

Who should buy Ultimate

  • You like to customize. Two extra mod shops, a salon, a tattoo parlor and two clothing stores add up to a lot of dressing up cars and people.
  • You collect vehicles. The Cheetah, the Dominator Buggy and the classic car side activity are aimed at you.
  • You’re going to play for months anyway. Spread over 100 hours, $20 is not much.
  • You want everything from the start and don’t want to think about it again.

Who should stick with Standard

  • You mostly care about the story. The story is the same.
  • You’re on a budget. $79.99 is already a lot for a game. Twenty dollars is a few weeks of a streaming service.
  • You’re not sure you’ll stick with it. Start Standard. Upgrade if you get hooked.
  • You bought the boxed copy. You already have Standard, and you can still buy the upgrade digitally later.

Our take

Ultimate is a fair add-on, not a scam and not a steal. The best GTA 6 edition for most people is Standard first, Ultimate later if you want it, because the upgrade price matches the gap. If you’re the type who spends an hour at the mod shop picking rims, just buy Ultimate and enjoy it.
