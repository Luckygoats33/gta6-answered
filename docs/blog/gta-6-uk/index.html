GTA 6 in the UK: Price, Release Time and Where to Buy

Published Nov 15, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

If you searched for where to pre-order GTA 6 in the UK, the pre-order window is still open: the game comes out on November 19, 2026, and pre-orders run until launch. The stores below are taking them now, at UK prices of £69.99 for the Standard Edition and £89.99 for the Ultimate Edition, as reported by TechRadar when UK pre-orders opened.

Here’s the UK picture in one place: when it unlocks, what it costs, where to buy, and what’s actually in the box.

Release time in the UK

GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time in every region, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. In the UK that means midnight GMT, the very start of Thursday, November 19, 2026.

For context, that puts UK players:

  • an hour behind Paris, Berlin and Madrid, which open at midnight CET (11pm UK time on November 18),
  • five hours ahead of New York,
  • and well behind New Zealand, which opens first at 11am UK time on November 18.

Pre-load opened on November 10. On PS5 that was midnight local time, and on Xbox it was midnight GMT, so UK Xbox players got the pre-load at the same moment as their PS5 friends. The full world table is on our launch times page.

GTA 6 UK price

The UK prices TechRadar reported from UK stores when pre-orders went live in June 2026:

  • Standard Edition: £69.99
  • Ultimate Edition: £89.99

That’s a £20 gap, the same shape as the US prices ($79.99 and $99.99). The Ultimate Edition adds 16 extra single-player items: vehicles, weapons, clothing, shops and activities. Standard owners can buy the Ultimate upgrade later from the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store, so you don’t have to decide up front.

Prices can change with sales and offers, so check the store page on the day you buy. Our price page has the current headline figures.

Where to buy GTA 6 in the UK

Your realistic options, digital first:

  1. PlayStation Store on PS5. Both editions, buy and download on the console or the app.
  2. Microsoft Store on Xbox Series X|S. Both editions.
  3. UK retailers for the boxed Standard Edition. TechRadar’s pre-order list included Amazon, ShopTo, The Game Collection, Smyths and Very, with Currys, Argos, EE and John Lewis listed for stock checks.

A few tips for picking:

  • Digital is the simplest. It’s tied to your account and you can download it again any time.
  • Boxed can make sense for gifts or if a retailer has a discount. Just know what you’re getting.
  • The Ultimate Edition is digital only, so you won’t find it in a box on any UK shelf.

Boxed copies in UK shops

The GTA 6 box doesn’t contain a disc. It holds a download code. Rockstar did this on purpose, and the boxes went on sale on November 12, 2026 so physical buyers could redeem early and pre-load.

What that means for you:

  • You still need to download the full game, up to 212 GB depending on your console. See the file size page.
  • The code is for one platform. A PS5 box won’t work on Xbox, or the other way around.
  • Codes follow the region of your PlayStation or Xbox account. Rockstar advises buying from a retailer in the same country as your account, so a UK account should buy from a UK shop.
  • Once a code is redeemed, it’s used. Don’t buy "pre-owned" codes or opened boxes.

More on why there’s no disc is on our disc page.

What about the pre-order bonus?

Rockstar’s bonus, the Vintage Vice City Pack, comes with digital pre-orders and purchases of either edition made before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store), and physical pre-orders include a code for it. If you order before launch, you should get it, but check the store listing to be sure. Be careful with listings that promise "pre-order bonus included" after launch, especially on marketplace sites.

Avoid dodgy UK deals

  • Key resellers selling cheap "GTA 6 codes" from other regions can leave you with a code that won’t redeem on a UK account.
  • Account sharing offers ("we’ll log in and buy it for you") are against store rules and an easy way to lose your account.
  • Fake stores pop up around every big launch. Stick to the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store and retailers you’d recognize on a high street.

Buy from the console store or a known UK shop, keep your receipt, and you’ll be in Vice City without any drama.
