Where to Buy GTA 6: Digital Stores, Boxed Copies and Deals

Published Nov 14, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

GTA 6 where to buy comes down to two choices: buy it digitally from the PlayStation Store or the Microsoft Store on your console, or buy the boxed Standard Edition from a retailer, which contains a download code instead of a disc. Both get you the same game on the same account. The difference is price, convenience, and whether you want the Ultimate Edition, which is digital only. GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, so until then every option below is a pre-order, and pre-load is already open.

Digital: PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store

This is the simplest route:

  • On PS5: open the PlayStation Store, search GTA 6, pick an edition, pay, download.
  • On Xbox Series X|S: open the Microsoft Store from the console, same steps.

Why go digital:

  • Ultimate Edition is only sold this way. If you want it, this is your store.
  • No code to type, no trip to a shop. It’s on your account the moment you pay.
  • Upgrades are easy. If you buy Standard and change your mind, the Ultimate upgrade is sold in the same store. On PlayStation, the upgrade price only shows once you own the Standard Edition. The upgrade page has more.

Watch for: digital store prices are usually list price. Sales do happen, but the console stores don’t tend to discount a brand-new Rockstar game quickly.

Boxed copies at retailers

Rockstar put boxed copies of the Standard Edition in stores from November 12, 2026, a week before launch. You’ll find them wherever console games are sold: the big electronics chains, the big-box stores, dedicated game shops and online retailers. Make sure you pick the right console. A PS5 box gives you a PS5 code, an Xbox box gives you an Xbox code, and they don’t cross over.

Why buy boxed:

  • Retailers discount. Physical retail is where sales, coupons, gift card promotions and store credit tend to show up first.
  • It’s giftable. A box under a tree is more satisfying than a digital gift card, even if it’s a code inside.
  • You can use gift cards or store credit you already have at that retailer.

Why the box has a code, not a disc

This catches people out. The boxed GTA 6 has no disc. Inside is a download code that you redeem on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store. After that, it downloads like a digital copy and lives on your account.

What that means for you:

  • It works on a Digital Edition PS5 or a console with no disc drive.
  • You can’t resell it like a normal disc once the code is used, because the game is tied to your account.
  • The download is huge. Up to 212 GB, so plan for time and space.

The disc page explains this in a little more detail.

Price differences

The US list prices, confirmed by Take-Two and Rockstar, are $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. Retailers can sell the boxed Standard for less if they choose to, so it’s worth comparing a few before you buy. The Ultimate Edition stays at store price unless the console store runs its own sale. Our price page keeps the basic numbers.

A tip: if a deal looks too good, check who’s selling. A third-party seller on a marketplace site is not the same as the retailer itself.

The pre-order bonus

Rockstar’s pre-order bonus is the Vintage Vice City Pack: a ’55 Vapid Stanier with a Shore Court garage, outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and a weapon pattern. Digital buyers get it if they purchase before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store), and according to The Sixth Axis, physical pre-orders included it while stocks lasted. That window is open for digital until launch. If you’re buying a box now, check the listing or the box itself before you assume it’s included.

Where not to buy

  • Key resellers and grey market sites. Console copies come from the console stores or a code in a retail box. A "cheap GTA 6 key" from a random site is a gamble at best.
  • "Shared account" offers. Someone selling access to their account isn’t selling you the game, and it breaks store rules.
  • Other regions’ stores. Buying from a foreign store to save money can break store rules and leave you with a copy on the wrong account.
  • Anything PC or mobile. There is no PC or mobile version. Those listings are scams.
