Every GTA 6 Weapon Rockstar Confirmed Before Launch

Published Nov 13, 2026. Facts checked Nov 16, 2026.

The GTA 6 new guns Rockstar has actually named before launch are a short list: the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, an engraved Girardi ES9 pistol for Jason and an engraved Klose K17 pistol for Lucia, all from the Ultimate Edition. Beyond that, Rockstar has shown plenty of shooting in trailers and the Extended Look, but it has never published a full weapon list. The full arsenal will only be known once the game is out on November 19, 2026, so this page sticks to what is official before then.

GTA 6 weapons confirmed by name

These are the only guns Rockstar listed by name in its pre-launch edition details:

  • Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers. An Ultimate Edition item.
  • Girardi ES9. A personalized, engraved pistol for Jason, part of the Ultimate Edition’s "Personalized Variants."
  • Klose K17. Lucia’s engraved pistol, from the same Ultimate set.

Gun names in Rockstar games are usually fictional brands with real-world inspirations, the way "Vapid" stands in for a real carmaker. Don’t expect a Glock to be called a Glock.

There’s one more pre-launch weapon item, though it’s cosmetic: the Vintage Vice City Pack, the pre-order bonus for digital purchases before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store), includes a tropical weapon pattern inspired by Tommy Vercetti’s palm tree shirt. The same pack’s Shore Court garage comes with a weapon locker.

Weapons seen in the trailers

Trailer 1 in December 2023 and Trailer 2 in May 2025 were cut like movie trailers rather than gameplay demos. They showed Jason and Lucia with handguns during robberies and a few bigger guns in action scenes, but nothing labeled. Fans spent months freeze-framing to identify models. That was guesswork, and we’re not repeating it as fact.

Weapons in the Extended Look

The Extended Look, which premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026, before going up on YouTube, was actual gameplay captured on a PS5. Engadget’s breakdown of what it showed:

  • Pistols. Jason fires one from behind cover during a raid.
  • Shotguns. Jason swaps to one mid-fight, and Lucia reloads one one-handed in a later scene.
  • An automatic rifle. Jason fires out of a car’s back window during a chase, taking on helicopters and police cars.
  • Bullet time. Jason uses a slow-motion aiming ability at a gala shootout. GTA V gave a similar power only to Michael.
  • Quick weapon swapping in the middle of combat.
  • Melee. Lucia, unarmed, fights back with her shoe in one scene. Very Leonida.

Outlets also tried to name specific guns from the footage. Rockstar didn’t caption any of them, so those names stay in the "identified by fans" pile.

Ultimate Edition weapons: worth it?

If you’re buying Ultimate mainly for guns, know what you’re getting: one pair of revolvers and two personalized pistols, alongside 14 other items that are mostly cars, shops and outfits. Standard owners can buy the upgrade later; the upgrade is listed at $20 on Xbox. See our upgrade answer and the Standard vs Ultimate answer before deciding.

What’s still unconfirmed

Rockstar hasn’t said any of the following ahead of launch, so we’re not claiming it:

  • A full GTA 6 weapons list. No official count or roster.
  • Weapon wheel details. Some outlets described a redesigned wheel from the Extended Look. Rockstar didn’t explain it.
  • Carry limits. Fans guessed at how many guns you can hold at once from the footage. Not official.
  • Weapon customization. Attachments, paint and tints were common in GTA V. Rockstar hasn’t detailed GTA 6’s system beyond the weapon pattern and the engraved pistols.
  • Online weapons. GTA 6 launches single-player, and Rockstar hasn’t announced Online; see our online answer.

Where to find the rest

Once people are playing on November 19, the real list will be written by players. If a site claims to have the "complete" list, check when it was written: anything from before November 19 is a guess. For official changes after launch, Rockstar’s patch notes and Newswire are the places to watch. And for everything confirmed beyond weapons, the facts page is the short version.
