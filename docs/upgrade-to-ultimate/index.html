Can I upgrade GTA 6 to Ultimate later?

Yes. Standard owners can buy the upgrade any time.

Buy it from the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store after the game is on your console.

Xbox lists the upgrade at $20. On PlayStation the price only shows once you own the Standard Edition.
