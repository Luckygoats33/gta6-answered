How big is the GTA 6 map?

About twice the size of GTA V.

Reported by press. Rockstar hasn’t confirmed this on its own pages.

Rockstar compares the state of Leonida to the maps of GTA IV, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 put together.

It has six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Rockstar hasn’t published a full map.
