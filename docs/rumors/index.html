GTA 6 rumors, checked

Each rumor gets a plain verdict and the source behind it. Updated as Rockstar confirms things.

  1. GTA 6 has been delayed again

    False

    Rockstar’s support page still lists November 19 at midnight local time. Rockstar Support

  2. GTA 6 comes on a disc

    False

    The boxed copy holds a download code, not a disc. Rockstar Newswire

  3. You can play GTA 6 on Netflix

    False

    Netflix showed a gameplay video. The game is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Netflix Tudum

  4. GTA 6 is on Android or iPhone

    False

    Not on mobile. “Mobile downloads” are scams. Rockstar Support

  5. New Zealand players get it first

    True

    Every region goes live at its own midnight, and New Zealand hits midnight first. Rockstar Support

  6. Switching your Xbox region gets you in early

    Unproven

    Reported as a trick, but nobody has confirmed it works for GTA 6. Windows Central

  7. GTA 6 Online launches in late 2027

    Reported

    From a Bloomberg report. Rockstar hasn’t given a date. Vice

  8. GTA 6 is on Game Pass day one

    Reported false

    Reported as not coming to Game Pass at launch. GameSpot

  9. A PC version arrives in early 2027

    Unconfirmed

    Outlets are guessing. Rockstar hasn’t announced PC. GGRecon

  10. Rockstar is launching a mod tool called Project ROME

    Unconfirmed

    From a fan account. No announcement from Rockstar. TweakTown
