Your GTA 6 download time depends almost entirely on your internet speed. At 100 Mbps the full 212 GB takes a little under five hours in a perfect world, and closer to six or seven in the real one. At 25 Mbps you are looking at most of a day, so start it before bed and let the console work.

GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar said the download is up to 212 GB depending on your console, and that's the only size figure Rockstar has given. The math below uses that maximum, so your console may finish a bit sooner.

## The GTA 6 download size, in hours

Internet plans are sold in megabits per second. Files are measured in gigabytes. One gigabyte is eight gigabits, so 212 GB is about 1,696 gigabits. Divide that by your speed and you get the best case:

- **25 Mbps:** about 19 hours
- **50 Mbps:** about 9 hours 25 minutes
- **100 Mbps:** about 4 hours 45 minutes
- **300 Mbps:** about 1 hour 35 minutes
- **500 Mbps:** about 57 minutes
- **1 Gbps (1,000 Mbps):** about 28 minutes

Those numbers assume your console pulls your plan's full speed for the whole download. It won't. Treat them as the floor, not the promise.

## Why your real download time runs longer

Every big launch I have watched goes the same way: the speed test says one thing, the progress bar says another. A few reasons stack up.

- **Your plan's number is a ceiling.** Internet providers advertise the top speed. Evenings, when the whole street is streaming, are slower.
- **Wi-Fi loses a lot.** A console two rooms away from the router can easily get half of what a wired connection gets.
- **Store servers get busy.** PlayStation Network and Xbox both serve millions of people on a launch this size. Delivery networks are built for it, but launch week is still the heaviest traffic they see.
- **Patches stack on top.** Rockstar hasn't published a separate day-one patch size. If one lands, it adds to the total. Check Rockstar's patch notes and your console's download queue for the current figure.

A fair rule of thumb: add 25 to 50 percent to the best-case time if you are on Wi-Fi or downloading in the evening. So the 100 Mbps household should plan on six or seven hours, not four and a half.

Rockstar said up to 212 GB, depending on the device, and it didn't break that down per console. What matters more is the space you actually have. ## How to make the download go faster

None of this is magic, but each one helps.

1. **Plug in a network cable.** If the router is close enough, this is the single biggest improvement for most homes.
2. **Pause everything else.** Other game downloads, 4K streaming on the TV and cloud backups on a laptop all share the same pipe.
3. **Let it run in rest or sleep mode.** On PS5, turn on "Stay Connected to the Internet" in the power saving settings. On Xbox, use the Sleep power option so downloads keep going with the screen off.
4. **Don't keep restarting it.** Pausing and resuming over and over rarely helps and sometimes slows things down.
5. **Download overnight or early morning.** Your neighbors are asleep, and the network is quieter.

## The GTA 6 install size after the download

Downloading and installing happen together on PS5 and Xbox. When the bar hits 100 percent there may still be a short "installing" or "copying" step, so don't panic if it sits there for a few minutes. If you pre-load before launch, you've already done the heavy lifting. Our [pre-load page](https://callunc.com/pre-load/) covers how that works.

Leave some room on top of the game itself. Updates almost always make big games bigger over time, and a full drive is the most common reason an update refuses to install.

## If it seems stuck

Give it time first. A download that shows the same percentage for a while is often still moving underneath. If it hasn't moved for an hour, check the console's network test, restart the router, and then restart the download from the queue. If the store itself is having trouble, Rockstar, PlayStation and Xbox each post service status pages, and that's the place to look before you start unplugging things.
