You can't do a GTA 6 Xbox pre order anymore, because the game is out. It launched on Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026, so the Microsoft Store now sells it as a normal purchase that downloads right away. The steps are almost the same as pre-ordering was: open the store, pick Standard or Ultimate, buy, and make room for a download of up to 212 GB.

## Step 1: Open the Microsoft Store

You can buy on the console or on the web:

1. **On your Xbox:** press the Xbox button, open the Store, and search for Grand Theft Auto VI.
2. **On the web:** go to the Microsoft Store site in a browser, sign in with the same Microsoft account you use on your Xbox, and search there. You can send the download to your console remotely, as long as it's set up to accept remote installs.

Check that the publisher reads Rockstar Games before you pay.

## Step 2: Pick Standard or Ultimate

There are two editions, at these US prices, confirmed by Take-Two:

- **Standard Edition, $79.99:** the full game.
- **Ultimate Edition, $99.99:** the game plus 16 single-player extras, including the '95 Grotti Cheetah, a Dominator buggy, custom revolvers, outfits and tattoos, and several Ultimate-only shops like Rideout Customs and Electric Fang Tattoo.

The story, map and missions are the same in both. Ultimate is digital only. If you're on the fence, buy Standard. You can upgrade later, and our [Standard vs Ultimate page](https://callunc.com/standard-vs-ultimate/) lists every extra.

What you won't get now is the pre-order bonus. The Vintage Vice City Pack came with digital purchases made before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store). If the store page lists any current bonus, it'll say so on the listing.

## Step 3: Check your space first

Rockstar said the download was up to 212 GB, depending on the console. Patches tend to grow that over time.

- **Series X:** fine for most people, though you may need to uninstall a couple of big games.
- **Series S:** about 364 GB usable, so GTA 6 fills most of the drive. Clear space before you buy, not halfway through the download.
- **More room:** Xbox storage expansion cards can run Series X|S games. A standard USB drive can store them but can't run them.

Check under Settings, then System, then Storage devices. More detail on our [file size page](https://callunc.com/file-size/).

## How pre-load timing worked on Xbox

For anyone curious, here's what you missed. Xbox pre-load opened at midnight GMT on November 10, 2026, all at once worldwide, which was 4pm PT and 7pm ET on November 9 in the US. PS5 opened region by region at local midnight instead. Either way, the game unlocked at midnight local time on November 19.

Pre-orders did well. Take-Two said before launch that they were tracking far above its expectations, and Xbox claimed a record for its own store, without giving numbers.

## Step 4: Upgrade to Ultimate later if you want

If you buy Standard and change your mind, the Ultimate upgrade is sold separately in the Microsoft Store. It was listed at $20, as reported by Forbes, which puts you at the same total as buying Ultimate outright. Our [upgrade page](https://callunc.com/upgrade-to-ultimate/) walks through it.

## Can't fit it? Cloud is an option

Rockstar confirmed GTA 6 streams through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One, using Series S graphics settings. You still have to own the game, and you need cloud gaming access through a Game Pass plan that includes it or a paid cloud option. It isn't on Game Pass as a game you can download for free with a subscription. See our [Game Pass page](https://callunc.com/game-pass/).

Cloud play depends on your connection, and Rockstar warned it can perform worse than a local install. It's a decent stopgap on a full Series S, or the only option on an Xbox One.

## Where not to buy it

Stick to the Microsoft Store, Xbox gift cards, or the boxed Standard copy from a known retailer, which contains a download code rather than a disc. Skip cheap "GTA 6 Xbox keys" from grey-market sites and anyone offering free codes. Region-switch tricks to get a cheaper price can break store rules, so they aren't worth the risk to your account.
