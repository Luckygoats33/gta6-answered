How much storage is needed for GTA 6? Rockstar says up to 212 GB, depending on your console, and you should plan for more than that once updates arrive. On a PS5 or Xbox Series X that's a big chunk of the drive. On a Series S it's more than half of everything you have. The good news: clearing space is quick if you do it in the right order.

## How much space you actually need

Start with Rockstar's number, up to 212 GB, and add a buffer. Two reasons:

- **Updates.** Games this size tend to grow after release, and an update needs free space to download and unpack before it installs.
- **Everything else.** Your console's own system updates, screenshots and video clips all live on the same drive.

I'd want around 250 GB free before starting, and more if you can. That's not a Rockstar figure, just a cushion so you aren't deleting things every time a patch drops. Our [file size page](https://callunc.com/file-size/) has the short answer per console.

## Check your free space

- **PS5:** Settings, Storage. You'll see used and free space for console storage and any extra drives.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** Settings, System, Storage devices. Or open My games and apps and look at the bar at the top of the Manage screen.

Write the number down. It saves you guessing later.

## Clearing space on PS5

1. **Delete what you're done with.** Settings, Storage, Console Storage, Games and Apps. Sort by size and tick off the big ones you finished.
2. **Delete parts of games.** Some games let you remove only certain pieces, like a multiplayer mode you never touch, through a Customize option. Worth a look on big shooters.
3. **Move, don't delete.** If you have a USB drive, you can move PS5 games to it from the Game Library: highlight the game, press Options, choose Move Games and Apps. You can't play PS5 games from USB, but you can copy them back later without downloading again.
4. **Clear the capture gallery.** Long video clips add up faster than you'd think.
5. **Add an M.2 SSD.** If you're always short on room, a compatible M.2 SSD in the expansion slot can hold and play PS5 games, GTA 6 included.

Anything you delete can be downloaded again from your library. Your saves are a separate thing, but if you care about them, check that cloud saves are on before you start deleting.

## Clearing space on Xbox

1. **Uninstall the big stuff.** My games and apps, Full library, then Manage on each game. Many games list their install size right there.
2. **Uninstall add-ons you don't use.** Under Manage game and add-ons, some games let you remove extra content separately.
3. **Use a USB drive as a parking spot.** Xbox Series X|S games can be stored on a USB drive but can't be played from it. They have to come back to the internal drive or an official expansion card first. Older Xbox One games can run straight from USB, so moving those off the internal drive is a free win.
4. **Consider an expansion card.** The Seagate and Western Digital cards that plug into the back are the only external storage that can play Series X|S games like GTA 6.

## Series S tips

The Series S has a 512 GB drive, and the usable space for games has been widely reported at about 364 GB. Put GTA 6 at up to 212 GB next to that and you've got roughly 150 GB left for everything else. In practice that means GTA 6 plus one or two other big games, and not much else.

What works for Series S owners:

- **Keep GTA 6 and one other main game installed.** Park the rest on a USB drive.
- **Move older Xbox One titles to USB** and play them from there.
- **Check before an update.** If a patch won't download, it's almost always space.

If you're still weighing which console to play on, the [consoles page](https://callunc.com/consoles/) covers what GTA 6 runs on.

## Leave room for updates

This is the step people skip. GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026, and Rockstar will publish its own patch notes for any updates. You won't always know the size in advance, and a full drive means the update sits in your queue and the game may not launch until it's installed. Keeping a margin free saves you a late-night scramble. Our earlier post on the [GTA 6 day one patch and updates](https://callunc.com/blog/gta-6-day-one-patch/) covers turning on auto-updates on both consoles.

One warning while you're cleaning house: if you see "GTA 6 storage booster" apps or websites promising to shrink the game, ignore them. There's no such thing. The size is the size.
