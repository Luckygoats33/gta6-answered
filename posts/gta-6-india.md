GTA 6 in India costs ₹5,999 for the Standard Edition and ₹7,499 for the Ultimate Edition on the PlayStation Store, as reported by Business Today before launch. It came out on November 19, 2026, at 12:00 am IST, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. There's no PC version and nothing on phones, so any "GTA 6 Android download" you see is a scam.

## Release time in IST

Rockstar's rule was midnight local time everywhere, and India's store listings showed 12:00 am IST on Thursday, November 19, 2026. In UTC that's 6:30 pm on November 18. Players in New Zealand, Australia and Japan started a few hours earlier because their midnight came first. Players in Europe and the US started later.

That's history now. If you buy a copy now, it downloads and plays as soon as the install finishes. The full world schedule is on our [launch times page](https://callunc.com/launch-times/).

## Price on PS Store India

Business Today reported these prices from the PlayStation Store India listing before launch:

- **Standard Edition:** ₹5,999
- **Ultimate Edition:** ₹7,499
- **Ultimate upgrade for Standard owners:** ₹1,500

Other Indian outlets reported the same prices on the Xbox Store, but check the listing on your own account before you pay, since store prices can change. For comparison, the US prices are $79.99 and $99.99.

Ultimate adds 16 single-player extras: a '95 Grotti Cheetah, custom revolvers, outfits and tattoos, extra vehicles and several Ultimate-only shops. The story and map are the same in both editions. Our [Standard vs Ultimate page](https://callunc.com/standard-vs-ultimate/) lists them all.

## The GTA+ month: check the deadline

This one matters if you pre-ordered. The Indian store listing included one month of GTA+ with digital pre-orders made before November 20, 2026. Business Today reported that the month has to be claimed by March 31, 2027, and that GTA+ renews as a paid subscription afterward unless you cancel before the next payment. If you pre-ordered and haven't claimed it, do it before the deadline, and set a reminder to cancel if you don't want to keep paying.

The other pre-order bonus, the Vintage Vice City Pack, came with digital purchases made before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store). A copy bought now won't include either one.

## How to buy GTA 6 in India

1. **PS5:** open the PlayStation Store on your console or in the PlayStation App, search Grand Theft Auto VI, pick an edition and pay. PlayStation Store gift cards sold in India work too.
2. **Xbox Series X|S:** open the Microsoft Store, search the game and buy. Xbox gift cards work as well.
3. **Boxed copy:** the physical Standard Edition holds a download code, not a disc. It works on a PS5 Digital Edition too. See our [disc page](https://callunc.com/disc/).

Buy from the official stores or a known retailer. Skip grey-market "cheap keys" and region tricks that break store rules. A banned account costs more than you save.

## PS5 vs Xbox in India

Same game on both, with a 30 frames per second target on every console. The differences:

- **PS5:** DualSense haptics, trigger feedback and police radio through the controller speaker.
- **PS5 Pro:** adds PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows for a sharper image.
- **Xbox Series S:** the cheapest way in, but about 364 GB of usable storage, and the download was up to 212 GB. You'll need to clear most of the drive.

If you're buying a console mainly for GTA 6, pick the one your friends use. If Rockstar adds an online mode later, that will matter more than small graphics differences. Rockstar and PlayStation both pushed the line that the game plays best on PS5, which is marketing, but the controller features are real. ## Avoiding mobile scams

Searches for "GTA 6 mobile download" and "GTA 6 APK" are common in India, and almost every result leads to a scam. The facts:

- GTA 6 is not on Android or iPhone. Rockstar lists PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only.
- There's no "lite" version, "beta APK" or "India special edition."
- Pages asking you to complete surveys, install apps or log in with your Google, PSN or Xbox account to "unlock" GTA 6 are phishing.

If a video promises GTA 6 on your phone, it's either a different game with a GTA 6 thumbnail or a link to malware. Our [mobile page](https://callunc.com/mobile/) covers how the fakes usually look.
