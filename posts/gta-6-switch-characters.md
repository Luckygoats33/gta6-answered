Yes, you can switch characters in GTA 6 between its two protagonists, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. But it isn't a free button you can press any time you like. Rockstar has said switching depends on the mission: some let you swap freely, some swap for you at set moments, and some keep you locked to one character.

GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026. Everything below is what Rockstar and reputable outlets have described ahead of launch. How it feels in the game is something you'll find out with a controller in hand.

## GTA 6 two protagonists: the basics

GTA 6 is built around Jason and Lucia, a criminal couple in the state of Leonida. The Dazed preview, as reported by PC Gamer, describes Lucia as fresh out of prison and Jason as an ex-military drug runner.

Both are playable, both are yours, and the game moves between them. That's the core difference from most GTA games, which put you in one person's shoes for the whole story. Our [characters post](https://callunc.com/blog/gta-6-characters/) covers who they are in more detail.

## How GTA 6 character switching works in missions

The clearest explanation came from Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson in an interview with Famitsu, translated by GamesRadar+ and reported by PC Gamer. In short:

- **Some missions let you switch as much as you want.** Nelson pointed to the attack at the end of the Extended Look as one where you can swap freely between the two.
- **Some missions switch for you.** In the bank robbery escape from the Extended Look, you shoot at the police as Jason from the back seat, and the game then puts you in the driver's seat as Lucia.
- **Some sections lock you to one character.** Nelson said Rockstar sometimes wants you to experience a scene from a specific perspective, "like GTA 5, so we limit switching."

He gave a behind-the-scenes example, too. An early mission once let you swap between them, but the team decided Jason should lead it because of his military background.

Engadget's breakdown of the Extended Look fits the same picture. It spotted the action passing from Jason to Lucia during a car chase, and a scene where the couple are offered a job and Lucia leaves Jason to take it, suggesting some story moments decide for you.

## Can you play as Lucia whenever you want?

Outside missions, it sounds like yes. PC Gamer noted that the Dazed preview had already described swapping freely in the open world. Dazed said you can play as a couple or go off and cause trouble independently, and described cruising the highway as Jason, then switching to Lucia in the passenger seat to use her in-game phone.

So if your question is "can I play GTA 6 as Lucia?", the answer is yes, a lot. You just won't control which of them leads every story mission.

## How it compares to GTA V's switching

GTA V had three protagonists, Michael, Franklin and Trevor, and a switch wheel that let you jump between them in free roam. The camera would zoom out over the map and drop into whatever that character was doing, sometimes mid-argument, sometimes somewhere embarrassing. In missions, switching was often scripted around heists and set pieces.

GTA 6 keeps the spirit, with some changes:

- **Two characters instead of three,** and they're a couple, so they're often together rather than across the map.
- **Switching in the same car.** Going from driver to shooter inside one vehicle, as in the Extended Look, is a tighter kind of swap than GTA V's map-wide jumps.
- **The zoom-out is still there.** In the Extended Look, the camera pulls away from Lucia after a base jump and drops down to Jason miles away, a move GTA V players will recognize.
- **Story comes first.** Nelson's comments make clear that Rockstar decides when switching serves the scene.

## What isn't confirmed

Keep these in the "not announced" pile:

- **Co-op.** Two protagonists doesn't mean two players. Rockstar described GTA 6 as a single-player experience. Our [co-op post](https://callunc.com/blog/gta-6-co-op/) explains.
- **Exact switch controls.** Rockstar hasn't published the button layout.
- **A third playable character.** None has been announced.
- **Online characters.** There's no GTA 6 online mode announced yet. See the [online page](https://callunc.com/online/).

## The short version

- Two playable protagonists: Jason and Lucia.
- Free switching in the open world, as described in the Dazed preview.
- In missions, Rockstar decides: free swaps in some, automatic swaps in others, one character only in a few.
- It builds on GTA V's system, but with the couple often side by side instead of miles apart.

If you liked GTA V's switching, there's plenty here to like. Just let the story drive when it wants to.
