The GTA 6 first release date was never a day. It was a window: 2025, announced with the first trailer in December 2023, then narrowed to fall 2025. The first actual date Rockstar gave was May 26, 2026, and that one moved too. The game finally came out on November 19, 2026, the date Rockstar set in November 2025 and held for a full year.

So the answer to "how many release dates did GTA 6 have" is two real dates and one window. Here's the whole run, in order.

## Before there was a date

- **February 4, 2022.** Rockstar confirmed for the first time that active development on the next Grand Theft Auto was well underway. No name, no window.
- **September 2022.** Early development footage leaked online. Rockstar acknowledged it. This site doesn't use or describe leaked material, and the leak didn't come with any release date from Rockstar.
- **December 4, 2023.** The first trailer was scheduled for December 5, but it leaked shortly before release, so Rockstar put it out early, on the evening of December 4. It named the game Grand Theft Auto VI, set it in Vice City, and gave a 2025 window for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Earlier windows and delays

The 2025 window got more specific before it broke.

- **Early 2025.** Take-Two said the game was launching in fall of calendar 2025.
- **May 2, 2025: the first delay.** Rockstar announced the game was moving to **May 26, 2026**, its first real date. It apologized and said the extra time was needed to deliver the quality players expect.
- **May 6, 2025.** Four days later, Trailer 2 dropped as a surprise, which took some of the sting out.
- **November 6, 2025: the second delay.** Rockstar announced on X that GTA 6 would release on **Thursday, November 19, 2026**. It apologized again and said the extra months would let the team finish the game with the polish players expect. Take-Two's CEO Strauss Zelnick confirmed the date in the company's financial results.

That's the full list of GTA 6 release dates: fall 2025 (a window), May 26, 2026, and November 19, 2026.

## GTA 6 release date confirmed, again and again

After November 2025, the date didn't move, and Take-Two kept saying so on its earnings calls.

- **May 2026.** On Take-Two's earnings call, Zelnick reiterated November 19. His own short version: "reiterating November 19 as a launch day today is probably a positive."
- **June 24, 2026.** Pre-orders opened, with prices of $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate.
- **August 2026.** Take-Two's first-quarter results again listed November 19, 2026 for GTA VI and kept the company's full-year forecast, which was built around the game. Zelnick called pre-orders unprecedented.
- **August 27, 2026.** "Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look" premiered on Netflix, with the November 19 date attached. Our [Netflix answer](https://callunc.com/netflix/) explains that one.

Every confirmation after the second delay pointed at the same day, which is unusual for a game with two delays behind it. By the fall, the questions people were asking had changed from "will it slip again" to "what time does it unlock where I live," which is about as good a sign as a launch date gets.

## Launch week timeline

The last stretch, as Rockstar laid it out in early November 2026:

1. **November 10.** Digital pre-load opened: midnight local time on PS5, and midnight GMT on Xbox Series X|S (4 p.m. Pacific on November 9).
2. **November 12.** Boxed Standard copies, with a download code inside, reached stores.
3. **November 18, 11:00 UTC.** New Zealand hit midnight first and unlocked the game.
4. **November 19.** Everyone else followed at their own local midnight. Our [release time answer](https://callunc.com/release-time/) shows how that rolled around the world.

## How the delays compare to Rockstar's history

Rockstar delaying a big game isn't new. Red Dead Redemption 2 also slipped from its first announced window before shipping in October 2018. What stood out with GTA 6 was the scale: more than four and a half years from Rockstar's first development confirmation to launch, and almost three years from the first trailer.
