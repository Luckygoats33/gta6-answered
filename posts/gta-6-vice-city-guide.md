Welcome to Vice City, take two. In GTA 6, Vice City is the big metropolis of the state of Leonida, Rockstar's version of Miami, and the first place most players will spend serious time. It's one of six regions Rockstar named before the game came out on November 19, 2026, alongside the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.

If you played the 2002 original, the names will ring a bell. The city around them is a very different place.

## Vice City's place in the GTA 6 Vice City map

Leonida is a fictional Florida, and Vice City is its Miami. The rest of the state is built around it:

- **Leonida Keys** to the south, a chain of islands for boat parties and dive bars.
- **Grassrivers**, the wetlands, which is Rockstar's Everglades.
- **Port Gellhorn**, a run-down former vacation town.
- **Ambrosia**, an industrial region with a sugar refinery and a biker gang in charge.
- **Mount Kalaga National Park**, the wilderness at the northern edge.

Rockstar is reported to have said the whole state is roughly twice the size of GTA V's map, but it didn't publish a full map before launch. Our [map size page](https://callunc.com/map-size/) has the details, and the [Leonida guide](https://callunc.com/blog/gta-6-leonida-guide/) covers the other five regions.

## Districts and places seen so far

Rockstar's own descriptions, plus the edition and pre-order announcements and the Extended Look, give us a decent list of named places in and around the city. This is what was public before release, not a complete list:

- **Ocean Beach:** white sand and pastel art deco hotels. The postcard part of town.
- **Little Cuba:** a compact, busy neighborhood that Rockstar called out by name.
- **VC Port:** pitched by Rockstar as the cruise ship capital of the world.
- **Tisha-Wocka flea market:** named on Rockstar's site as a city landmark.
- **Washington Beach:** where the Ultimate Edition's Shitzu Squalo boat is docked.
- **Watson Bay:** home of the Paradise Garage from the Ultimate Edition.
- **Stockyard:** where the Ultimate Edition's Stock 305 clothing store and Electric Fang Tattoo shop are.
- **Shore Court:** the location of the pre-order bonus garage, near Ocean Beach.
- **The Effluvia:** a rooftop bar in downtown Vice City, seen in the Extended Look.

Note that some of those spots (the Ultimate shops and the bonus garage) belong to specific editions. The neighborhoods themselves are part of everyone's game.

## Beaches and nightlife

Vice City has always been about two things: the beach by day and the neon by night. Rockstar leaned into both. Its descriptions talk up Ocean Beach's sand and hotels, and the Extended Look spends time in clubs, bars and rooftop lounges. Rockstar's senior vice president of narrative told Dazed (as reported by PC Gamer) that the team was interested in Miami's music scene, from house parties to high-end South Beach clubs.

The flip side, which GamesRadar noted from the official material, is traffic. Vice City is full of suspended freeways and narrow, crowded streets, which makes driving slower and police escapes harder. Breaking line of sight may matter more than raw speed.

## Vice City then and now

The original GTA Vice City came out in 2002 on PS2 and was set in 1986, with Tommy Vercetti climbing the city's drug trade. It was a pastiche of 80s Miami movies and TV: neon, synth, linen suits.

GTA 6's Vice City is set in the present day, and Rockstar described it as a return to modern Vice City. Some neighborhood names carry over, like Ocean Beach and Little Cuba (and Washington Beach is a familiar one too). The city's scale and detail are in another league, and the satire has moved from cocaine-era excess to social media, influencers and the Florida Man headline.

Rockstar did nod to the old game. The Vintage Vice City Pack pre-order bonus included a weapon pattern based on Tommy's famous shirt. Our [GTA 6 map post](https://callunc.com/blog/gta-6-map/) covers how the city fits the wider state.

## What to explore first

There's no wrong order in an open world, but some easy starts based on what Rockstar showed:

1. **Ocean Beach at night.** The skyline and neon are what the trailers sold. Drive the strip once just to look.
2. **Little Cuba on foot.** Dense neighborhoods are better walked than driven, and you'll pick up the street layout faster.
3. **VC Port.** Worth a look for the cruise ships alone.
4. **Out to the water.** Boats are a big part of Leonida, and the coast is the fastest way to see how the city connects to the Keys.

What you'll actually find in each place is something to learn in the game, not from a pre-release list. Rockstar often hides its best details where it didn't point the camera.
