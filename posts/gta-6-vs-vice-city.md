GTA 6 vs Vice City is less a rematch than a homecoming. Grand Theft Auto: Vice City came out in October 2002 on the PlayStation 2 and was set in a neon-soaked 1986. GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026, and brings Vice City back as the biggest city in a whole state, Leonida, set in the present day with two leads instead of one. Same name on the map. Very different place.

## Vice City then and now

The 2002 game was Rockstar's love letter to Miami Vice and Scarface. You played Tommy Vercetti, a mobster fresh out of 15 years in prison, sent south by his boss Sonny Forelli to run a drug deal that goes wrong in the first few minutes. The rest of the game is Tommy taking over the city. The mood was pastel suits, synth radio and a skyline that seemed lit by a sunset that never ended.

GTA 6's Vice City is a modern city. The trailers show phones in every hand, livestreams, nightclubs and beach crowds, and a lot of jokes at the expense of internet culture. Rockstar's own tagline calls it the darkest side of the sunniest place in America. The neon is still there, but it now shares the frame with vertical video.

## Leonida beyond the city

This is the biggest change. In 2002, Vice City was the whole game: two main islands and some smaller ones. In GTA 6, Vice City is one of six regions in the state of Leonida, Rockstar's version of Florida:

- **Vice City**, the city itself.
- **The Leonida Keys**, where Jason worked for drug runners.
- **Grassrivers**, swamp country that looks a lot like the Everglades.
- **Port Gellhorn.**
- **Ambrosia.**
- **Mount Kalaga National Park.**

So the GTA 6 Vice City comparison isn't city against city. It's a city against a state with a city in it.

## Two protagonists instead of one

Tommy was a lone operator. GTA 6 has Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, partners who, per Rockstar, have to rely on each other when an easy score goes bad. Lucia is the first woman to lead a modern GTA story, and her backstory even ties to the wider series: Rockstar's profile says her mother dreamed of a better life back in Liberty City.

The supporting cast reflects the new Vice City too. Instead of 1980s crime lords, Rockstar profiled a music label owner, an aspiring rap mogul, a viral rap duo and a smuggler from the Keys' old days. That last one, Brian Heder, is basically a leftover from Tommy's era who never left.

## What returns from the 2002 game

Rockstar leaned into the history, especially in the pre-launch extras:

- **The name and the beach.** Ocean Beach, a district in the 2002 game, shows up again in GTA 6's official material.
- **A Tommy nod.** The Vintage Vice City Pack, the bonus for digital purchases before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store), includes a tropical weapon pattern inspired by Tommy Vercetti's palm tree shirt, plus a '55 Vapid Stanier and a garage in Shore Court near Ocean Beach.
- **The look.** The Ultimate Edition's "Vice City Style" outfits and tattoos lean on the same retro pastel palette.
- **Boats.** The 2002 game made boats a big deal, and GTA 6 has kayaks, speedboats and a whole Keys region to use them in.

What doesn't return, as far as Rockstar has said, is Tommy himself. He isn't in any official GTA 6 material.

## Map size compared

Rockstar hasn't published square miles for either game. What it has said is that GTA 6's world is about twice the size of GTA V's, and Rockstar head of development Rob Nelson told the YouTuber TGG that Vice City alone is twice the size of GTA V's Los Santos. The 2002 Vice City map was much smaller than either. It was big for a PS2, and it fits in a corner of what Leonida offers. Our [map size answer](https://callunc.com/map-size/) has the details.

## What it means if you loved the original

If you played Vice City as a kid, GTA 6 will feel familiar in spirit and foreign in detail. The city has the same swagger, the same beaches and some deliberate winks, but the scale, the two leads and the modern setting make it a new game, not a remake. If you want the 2002 version, that's a different purchase: GTA 6 is PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only (see our [consoles answer](https://callunc.com/consoles/)), and it doesn't include the old game.
