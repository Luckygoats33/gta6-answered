There is no GTA 6 in phone form. Rockstar released Grand Theft Auto VI on November 19, 2026, on two platforms only, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it hasn't announced a mobile version. So every "GTA 6 Android download," "GTA 6 iOS beta" or "GTA 6 mobile APK" you see is fake. Some are just ad farms. Some are malware. None of them are the game.

## GTA 6 is not on phones

Rockstar's official GTA VI page lists PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. That's it. No Android, no iPhone, no tablet. Our [mobile answer](https://callunc.com/mobile/) says the same thing in two lines.

One real thing to not confuse with the fakes: Xbox Cloud Gaming. GTA 6 has no Xbox One version, but if you own it you can stream it to an Xbox One through Xbox Cloud Gaming, Microsoft's official service. That's legit. Rockstar has only confirmed cloud streaming to Xbox consoles, so a "GTA 6 cloud" app or site that isn't Microsoft's own is not that.

It's worth knowing why the fakes are believable. Rockstar did put older GTA games on phones years after their console releases, and there's a real appetite for a pocket version. Scammers lean on that. A game that's up to 212 GB on a console isn't something a phone is going to run, and Rockstar hasn't said a word about trying.

## What the fake apps look like

Security researchers at NordVPN and others have been tracking GTA 6 fakes since well before launch. The patterns repeat:

- **A "GTA 6 Beta" or "GTA 6 Mobile" Android app** with Rockstar branding and a slick intro video. NordVPN found one that contained no game at all, just full-screen ads and links to subscription scams.
- **A fake "download extra data" step.** The app shows a loading screen, then asks you to fetch more files from a link. That link is where the real damage happens.
- **Fake "private beta" APK and EXE files** promoted in Discord servers, YouTube descriptions and forum posts. Gizmodo reported these installing a trojan.
- **"Beta key" or "early access" sites** asking you to pay for a subscription, fill in a survey or install software to "unlock" your key.
- **Fake Rockstar login pages** built to harvest your Rockstar Games Social Club details.
- **YouTube videos** titled something like "GTA 6 Mobile Download (Working)," with a link in the description. The comments are usually bots.

## What they're really after

When you strip away the Vice City sunset, these are ordinary scams:

- **Ad money.** The harmless-ish version just shows you ads until you give up.
- **Subscriptions.** Some route you to pages that sign you up for recurring charges.
- **Your accounts.** Phishing pages grab Rockstar, PlayStation, Xbox or email logins, which get resold or used for fraud.
- **Your phone itself.** Malware variants reported by Gizmodo can steal saved passwords and banking logins, and some Android versions read your SMS messages, which can defeat two-factor codes. NordVPN noted one fake app's domain had a history of spreading banking trojans and ransomware.

The common thread is urgency. "Limited beta slots." "Download before it's removed." Real Rockstar releases don't work like that.

## How to tell in five seconds

- If it says GTA 6 on Android or iPhone, it's fake.
- If it asks for an APK, an "OBB," or a file from outside the official app store, it's fake.
- If it offers a "GTA 6 Xbox One download" or install file, it's fake. Xbox One owners stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming, they don't download it.
- If it asks for your login on a page you reached from a link, close it.
- If it wants a payment, a survey or an app install to "unlock" a key, it's fake.
- If a video promises a "working" mobile version, it's lying for views.

## How to report them

Reporting takes a minute and helps the next person:

1. **Google Play:** on the app's page, open the three-dot menu and choose Flag as inappropriate.
2. **Apple App Store:** use Report a Problem from your purchase history if you downloaded something.
3. **YouTube:** use the Report option on the video, under spam or misleading content.
4. **In the US:** report fraud to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov, especially if you lost money.

If you already installed something, uninstall it, run a reputable security scan, change the passwords for any account you typed in, and turn on two-factor authentication. If a card was charged, call your bank.

## Where to actually play

The real game is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, from the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store or a boxed copy with a download code. There's no PC version either; see our [PC answer](https://callunc.com/pc/).

If you want GTA 6 on a phone screen, the legit route is streaming a copy you own from your own console. Rockstar said the game fully supports Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox. Sony's PS Remote Play app and Xbox's remote play feature stream games from your console to your phone over your home connection. The game still runs on the console. Your phone is just the screen.
