Rockstar never published a full vehicle list before launch, so the only GTA 6 cars confirmed by name come from Rockstar's own edition descriptions: the '95 Grotti Cheetah, Jason's Vapid Ganado pickup, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, a Dinka Enduro motorcycle, a Shitzu Squalo boat and a Crest kayak. Everything else people list was spotted in trailers and gameplay footage, and the real-world matches are car fans' educated reads, not Rockstar statements. Here's what each source actually shows.

## Cars Rockstar named before launch

When Rockstar laid out the Ultimate Edition in June 2026, it named several vehicles outright. Some are Ultimate-only. Others are cars from the main game with Ultimate extras attached:

- **'95 Grotti Cheetah:** a mid-1990s sports car with a minimalist, retro-futuristic livery. Ultimate Edition.
- **Vapid Ganado:** Jason's pickup. It's in every copy. Ultimate adds the Ganado Retro Build modkit.
- **'67 Vapid Dominator Buggy:** an off-road buggy for Mount Kalaga, stored at the Paradise Garage in Watson Bay. Ultimate Edition.
- **Dinka Enduro:** an army-fatigue motorcycle at Jason's safehouse. Ultimate Edition.
- **Crest Kayak:** also at the safehouse. Ultimate Edition.
- **Shitzu Squalo:** a pink-and-blue boat docked at Washington Beach. Ultimate Edition.

Ultimate also includes the Classic Car Collection, a commission from a collector named Wyman to recover abandoned classic and project cars. Rockstar didn't list which cars that covers. Two Ultimate-only mod shops, Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's, add interiors, rims, donk styling and off-road parts. ## Cars seen in the trailers

The first two trailers were cut like movies, so cars show up in quick shots. Car sites went through them frame by frame. From Trailer 2 in May 2025, PakWheels picked out:

- A red muscle car that looks like a Chevrolet Chevelle, which Jason drives during heists. It also appeared in Trailer 1.
- A big off-road pickup styled like a Ford F-150 Raptor.
- Police cruisers that closely copy the Ford Taurus Police Interceptor and the Ford Crown Victoria.
- Traffic in wealthy areas resembling a Mercedes C63 and a Lamborghini Gallardo.

Those are lookalikes. GTA has always used fictional brands like Vapid, Grotti and Dinka, with designs that borrow heavily from real cars without using real names.

## Cars in the Extended Look

The 27-minute Extended Look in August 2026 showed far more driving. Jalopnik went through it shot by shot. A few cars carried in-game names:

- **Sentinel:** a red convertible that Jalopnik compares to the E30 BMW 3 Series.
- **Schafter:** a black sedan that looks like the W212 Mercedes-Benz E-Class, plus a newer Schafter at a car meet.

The rest were matched by look only. Jalopnik's reads included a police cruiser that looks like a Dodge Charger, a Rolls-Royce Cullinan-style SUV in an airport convoy, a Chiron-like hypercar, and street racers resembling a Nissan Skyline GT-R R32, a Porsche Cayman and Ferrari and Lamborghini exotics. Regular traffic included lookalikes of the Ram 1500, Toyota RAV4, Chevrolet Suburban and a C7 Corvette.

Engadget's breakdown added scenes of torching a car and swapping into a van to lose the police, car theft and fan boat rides through the swamps.

## Real-world inspirations, and why they're guesses

A Vapid pickup with a familiar grille is clearly built to evoke a Ford truck. But Rockstar doesn't confirm real-world matches, and the in-game version usually mixes parts from several cars. When a list says "the GTA 6 version of the Dodge Viper," read it as "a car that looks a lot like one."

The same goes for the giant "GTA 6 cars list with pictures" posts. Some count every blurry vehicle in a trailer. Some pad the list with cars from GTA Online on the assumption they'll return. Neither is confirmation.

## Boats, bikes and aircraft

Leonida is a coastal state, and the footage shows it. Beyond the Squalo and the kayak, the official videos showed jet skis, fan boats in the Grassrivers, speedboats and yachts. Motorcycles turn up in traffic and chases, and police helicopters appear during wanted levels. Rockstar didn't publish a list of flyable aircraft before launch.

## Now that the game is out

GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026, so the real list is in the game itself. If you want to know whether a specific car exists, the most reliable sources are the game's own garages and mod shops, and anything Rockstar posts on the Newswire. Be wary of "secret car" videos that promise a location for something nobody has actually found.
