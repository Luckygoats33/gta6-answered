The best place for official GTA 6 screenshots is Rockstar's own GTA VI site, in two spots: the Launch Gallery, posted two weeks before release, and the Media & Artwork section, where Rockstar lets you download official screenshots, videos and other artwork. Everything else you find online is either a copy of those, a compressed repost, or something that isn't from Rockstar at all. GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026, so there's also a flood of player screenshots now. This post is about the official set.

## Official GTA 6 screenshots, release by release

Rockstar didn't drop everything at once. It added screenshots in batches around its big moments:

1. **May 2025, after Trailer 2.** Rockstar released about 70 screenshots shortly after the second trailer. VGC noticed the image file names labeled locations (Vice City, Ambrosia, Grassrivers, Leonida Keys, Mount Kalaga National Park, Port Gellhorn) and characters like Jason Duval, Lucia Caminos, Cal Hampton and Boobie Ike. That's where a lot of early region knowledge came from.
2. **September 2026, after the Extended Look.** Push Square reported a new batch on Rockstar's site the week after the Netflix gameplay video, captured on PS5 like the video itself.
3. **November 5, 2026, the Launch Gallery.** Rockstar announced it on X and posted it at rockstargames.com/VI/launch-gallery, describing it as a look at Vice City and beyond. Outlets counted nearly 40 new images. The page itself signs off with "See you in Leonida on November 19."

If you want the "gta 6 new screenshots" from closest to launch, the Launch Gallery is the one.

## GTA 6 cover art

Rockstar revealed the official cover art on June 18, 2026, the same day it announced pre-orders would open on June 25. It follows the series tradition: a collage of panels rather than one big image. Shacknews described it as showing Jason and Lucia, an alligator, Boobie Ike, and scenes from Vice City and around it. Rockstar's post linked to downloadable artwork, which is why the cover is easy to find in good quality.

If it reminds you of the 2002 Vice City box, you're not alone. Several outlets made the same comparison.

## GTA 6 artwork of the characters

For character art and descriptions, go to the "Only in Leonida" section of Rockstar's GTA VI site. It introduces the main cast with portraits and short profiles:

- **Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos,** the two leads.
- **Cal Hampton,** Jason's paranoid friend.
- **Boobie Ike and Dre'Quan Priest,** Vice City's music and money crowd.
- **The Real Dimez,** the rap duo signed to their label.
- **Raul Bautista and Brian Heder,** the older criminals in the orbit of the story.

Those profiles are also the cleanest official source for who's who, if you're trying to avoid story spoilers from elsewhere.

## Where to find them in high quality

A few tips so you get the real files, not a blurry copy:

- **Go to Rockstar directly.** Use the Media & Artwork section on the GTA VI site to download images. Rockstar presents it as the place to download and share official media.
- **Skip "HD pack" downloads from random sites.** They're often recompressed, and some bundle junk files. You never need to install anything to view a screenshot.
- **Check the source before you believe it.** Images that aren't on Rockstar's site or a reputable outlet may be fan art, AI edits or old leak material. The 2022 leak footage is still floating around and has nothing to do with the finished game's look.

## Using the screenshots

Setting an official screenshot as your phone or desktop wallpaper is what Rockstar's share section is for. Selling prints, putting the art on merch or using it in ads is a different matter: the images belong to Rockstar and Take-Two, so read Rockstar's terms before using them for anything commercial.

## Your own screenshots

Now that the game is out, you can take your own with your console's capture button: the Create button on a PS5 controller or the Share button on an Xbox Series controller. Whether GTA 6 has its own photo mode or in-game camera features, and how they work, is something to check in the game and in Rockstar's patch notes rather than in pre-launch guesses.
