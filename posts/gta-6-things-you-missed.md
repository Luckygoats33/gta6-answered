Most of the GTA 6 things you missed in the Extended Look aren't hidden easter eggs. They're small systems Rockstar showed for a second and never explained: a police search circle, a stream chat, a gym membership. "Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look" premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026, as 27 minutes of in-game footage captured on PS5. The game came out on November 19, 2026, but the video is still the best map of what Rockstar chose to show before launch.

If you want the full walkthrough of the video itself, start with our [Extended Look post](https://callunc.com/blog/gta-6-extended-look/). This one is the second pass: the stuff in the corners.

## GTA 6 things we missed in the background

The loud parts were the shootout and the car chases. The quiet parts were more interesting.

- **Jason raiding Lucia's fridge.** Early on, Jason is in Lucia's apartment taking food from the fridge. It's a throwaway beat, but it sets the tone: the two of them share space, not just missions.
- **A stream chat on screen.** An influencer livestreams from Lucia's car, and a chat scrolls on the right side of the screen. Rockstar didn't explain it. Dexerto's recap describes an in-game social platform where content can go viral.
- **Backyard life.** Basketball outside Jason's house and Jason watching backyard wrestling. Not missions, just the neighborhood living around you.
- **A gym with a membership.** Lucia works out at a gym, and Dexerto's recap notes she needed a membership. VGC couldn't tell whether that scene was gameplay or a cutscene, so don't read too much into it.

## Character moments

The video is a story piece, not a feature list, and a few moments tell you more about the leads than any trailer.

- **The deal that goes wrong.** The opening is a drug deal that collapses into a shootout, then a run from the police. It's the Netflix page's pitch in miniature: an easy score that turns into something bigger.
- **The rooftop talk.** Jason and Lucia discuss business and their relationship at a rooftop bar. The pair is the point of the game, and Rockstar gave that scene room.
- **Raul Bautista shows up.** The seasoned bank robber from Rockstar's character bios hires the pair as getaway drivers. If you remember his name from the website, this was the first look at him in action.
- **Lucia at the gun range.** Her shooting is timed, which hints at a scored activity rather than a cutscene.

## Map hints

The video doesn't show a full map, but it gives you a sense of range.

- **Water everywhere.** Fan boats on a river, kayaks on open water, diving and jumping into the sea. Grassrivers and the Keys weren't just backdrops.
- **Height.** Lucia parachutes off a building in Vice City, which VGC singled out for how far you could see.
- **Racing locations.** Dirt bike races, plus street and track racing in Dexerto's list, suggest the map's open spaces get used for more than driving between missions.
- **Leisure stops.** A zoo, a gun range, a gym, an Ammu-Nation and a store the pair robs. Small places, but places you can walk into.

## Systems hinted at

These are the bits that matter most once you're playing, and the ones Rockstar explained least.

1. **The wanted system works on description.** VGC noticed the map shows a circle where police are searching instead of individual police icons. Changing masks, hats or other clothes before a crime lowers your wanted level. Dexerto adds that you can stash clothing and weapons in cars to change your look mid-search.
2. **Witnesses and cameras.** Characters are identified by their clothing, including on security cameras, and shop owners fight back during robberies.
3. **Two-person crimes.** One shot has one character in a store while the other waits as getaway driver. VGC reports that missions can be played as Jason or Lucia, together or separately.
4. **Character switching.** It's in the footage. VGC said it looks similar to GTA V's; other outlets weren't sure. Play it and judge for yourself.
5. **Tire shots.** Jason disables a police car by shooting its tires. Classic, but nice to see it confirmed.
