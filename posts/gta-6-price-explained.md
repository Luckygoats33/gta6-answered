For years the GTA 6 expected price was a guessing game, with people betting on anything from $70 to $100 for the base game. The answer turned out to be $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition in the US, confirmed by Take-Two and Rockstar. GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026, at those prices, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

That's more than most new games, and people noticed. Here's what the two numbers actually mean.

## The two prices

There are two editions of the game, and only two.

- **Standard Edition, $79.99.** The full game. You can buy it digitally, or as a boxed copy that holds a download code instead of a disc.
- **Ultimate Edition, $99.99.** Standard plus a set of extra in-game items. Digital only.

Standard owners can buy the Ultimate upgrade later, so nobody is locked out of the extras by choosing the cheaper edition first. Our [upgrade page](https://callunc.com/upgrade-to-ultimate/) covers how that works on each console.

## GTA 6 is more expensive than most new games

For the last few years, $69.99 has been the usual price of a big new console game in the US. GTA 6's $79.99 is a $10 step above that, and the Ultimate sits $30 above it.

Why? Rockstar and Take-Two haven't laid out a public breakdown of the price, so anyone who tells you the exact reason is guessing. A few things are fair to say without guessing:

- GTA 6 has been in development for a long time. Rockstar confirmed active development in early 2022, and the game shipped almost three years after the first trailer.
- It's a single game expected to sell for years, not a yearly series.
- There's no Game Pass or PlayStation Plus catalog release at launch to undercut it. Reports from before launch said it wasn't coming to Game Pass on day one, and Rockstar never announced otherwise.

Whether that justifies $79.99 is your call. What's certain is that the price wasn't a surprise by launch. It had been public since June 2026.

## GTA 6 price confirmed: what the extra $20 buys

The Ultimate Edition's extras are all single-player content. Rockstar's list, as reported when the editions were revealed, includes:

- Extra vehicles, including a '95 Grotti Cheetah, a '67 Vapid Dominator buggy and a classic car collection with four Ultimate-only cars
- Jason's safehouse vehicles: a Dinka Enduro motorcycle, a Crest kayak and a modified Vapid Ganado pickup
- A boat, the Shitzu Squalo, docked at Washington Beach
- Two vehicle mod shops, a clothing store, a salon, a tattoo parlor and other shops and properties
- Personalized sidearms for Jason and Lucia, plus a revolver found at Ammu-Nation during the story
- Vice City outfits, tattoos and styles for both characters

That's a lot of stuff, and some of it is places you can go, not just skins. But none of it is new story missions, and none of it is GTA 6 Online, which Rockstar hasn't announced a date for. If you're a "do every side activity" player, Ultimate gives you more to do. If you play the story and move on, Standard is the full game. Our [Standard vs Ultimate page](https://callunc.com/standard-vs-ultimate/) has the short version, and our [Ultimate worth it post](https://callunc.com/blog/gta-6-ultimate-worth-it/) goes further.

## GTA 6 why is it so expensive outside the US?

Prices in other countries are set per region and include local taxes, so they don't convert neatly from the US price. Check the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store in your own region for the real number, and be wary of anyone quoting a "cheaper region" trick. Buying through another country's store can break the store's rules and cause trouble with your account.

## Is there a cheaper way to play?

Not a big one, and certainly not a free one. Here's what's real:

1. **Buy Standard, upgrade later if you want.** You spend $79.99 now and only pay the difference if you find you want the extras.
2. **Watch for store gift card deals.** Retailers sometimes sell PlayStation or Xbox gift cards with a small discount or bonus. That's legitimate.
3. **Wait for a sale.** Nobody knows when GTA 6 will first be discounted. Rockstar and the stores haven't said. If you can wait, you'll eventually pay less.
4. **Don't count on a subscription.** GTA 6 wasn't on Game Pass or a PlayStation Plus catalog at launch. Our [Game Pass answer](https://callunc.com/game-pass/) stays current.

What isn't real: "free GTA 6" downloads, cheap PC keys (there's no PC version), and mobile versions. Those are scams. The only way to play is to buy it for PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. If you own the Xbox version, you can also stream it to an Xbox One through Xbox Cloud Gaming.
