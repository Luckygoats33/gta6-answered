There is no GTA 6 release date for PC. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version at all as of November 16, 2026. GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and that's the whole platform list. Anything you've read with a specific PC month attached, including the popular "early 2027," is a guess. Our [PC page](https://callunc.com/pc/) has the one-line answer. This is the longer one.

## The short answer: no PC version announced

Here's what the official record actually says:

- **Rockstar lists two platforms:** PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **Take-Two's CEO has framed it as console first.** In a Bloomberg interview in May 2026, reported by Wccftech, Strauss Zelnick said Rockstar has historically gone to console first and that you're judged by serving your core audience first. He didn't announce a PC version or a date.
- **Nobody official has said "never."** Console first is not the same as console only, and Rockstar's history points the other way.

So the honest status of a "gta 6 pc version" is: likely someday, unannounced today.

## How Rockstar handled PC for GTA V and RDR2

The last two big Rockstar games both reached PC, both well after console:

- **GTA V** came out on PS3 and Xbox 360 in September 2013. The PC version arrived on April 14, 2015, about 19 months later. It also slipped along the way: Rockstar first set a January 2015 date, then moved it to March and finally April, saying it needed more time for testing and polish.
- **Red Dead Redemption 2** came out on consoles in October 2018. Rockstar announced the PC version in October 2019, and it launched on November 5, 2019, about a year after the console release.

Two patterns stand out. The gap has been roughly one to two years, and in the RDR2 case Rockstar announced the PC date only about a month before it shipped. That means a PC version could be announced with little warning. It also means nothing in that history points to a specific month for GTA 6.

## Why "early 2027" is a guess

The "gta 6 pc release early 2027" idea comes from outlets and fans doing the math on past gaps, not from Rockstar or Take-Two. Our [rumors page](https://callunc.com/rumors/) lists it as unconfirmed. If GTA 6 followed GTA V's gap, a PC version would land around mid-2028. If it followed RDR2's, late 2027. Neither is a prediction. They're just the range history suggests, and Rockstar has never promised to repeat either.

When someone gives you an exact date, ask for the link to Rockstar's Newswire. That's where every Rockstar PC announcement has appeared.

## Can you play on PC another way?

Not natively. There's one legitimate option, and one that people assume works but doesn't yet:

- **PS Remote Play.** The PlayStation Store lists GTA 6 as supporting Remote Play. If you own a PS5 and the game, Sony's Remote Play app lets you stream it from your own console to a PC or other device on your network. It's still your PS5 doing the work.
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming, but not on PC.** Rockstar and Xbox said that if you own GTA 6 you can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, at Series S graphics settings. It does not stream to PC at launch, so this isn't a PC route yet. Check Xbox's own cloud gaming pages if that changes.

What doesn't work: "gta 6 xbox to pc" tricks, emulators or "PC download" sites. There is no PC build to download, so any "GTA 6 PC download" is a scam or malware. Don't install it, and never type your PlayStation, Xbox or Rockstar login into a site promising one.

## What to watch for

If you're waiting for the PC version, these are the signs that actually mean something:

1. **A Rockstar Newswire post** announcing the PC version and a date. That's how GTA V and RDR2 PC were announced.
2. **An updated platforms list** on Rockstar Support.
3. **Store listings on Steam or the Rockstar Games Launcher** that Rockstar itself links to.
4. **Take-Two earnings calls,** where the company sometimes discusses its release plans.

Until one of those happens, the answer to "gta 6 when on pc" is simple: not announced. If you can't wait, a PS5 or Xbox Series S is the realistic path. Our [consoles page](https://callunc.com/consoles/) covers which machines run it.
