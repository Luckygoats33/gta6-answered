Giving GTA 6 as a gift works differently on each console. On Xbox you can buy the game itself as a gift in the Microsoft Store and send it to someone's email or gamertag. On PlayStation you can't send a specific game, so the usual gift is a PlayStation Store gift card for at least the price of the edition they want, or the boxed Standard copy, which holds a download code. Before any of that, find out which console they own.

## Step one: confirm the console

GTA 6 runs on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It isn't on PS4, PC, Switch or phones. A PlayStation purchase won't work on Xbox, and the other way around. GTA 6 has no Xbox One version, but if they own it they can stream it to an Xbox One through Xbox Cloud Gaming, as long as they have cloud gaming access. Two more checks save a lot of grief:

- **Storage.** The download is up to 212 GB. A Series S has about 364 GB of usable space, so the game takes most of it.
- **Do they already own it?** Plenty of people have pre-ordered it. If they have the Standard Edition, the Ultimate upgrade may be the better gift. More on that below.

## How to gift GTA 6 on Xbox

The Microsoft Store has a "Buy as gift" option for many digital games. Microsoft has offered gifting through the store since 2017. The steps:

1. Open the Microsoft Store on your Xbox, or the store's website in a browser.
2. Search for Grand Theft Auto VI and pick the edition.
3. Choose **Buy as gift** instead of the normal buy button.
4. On a console, pick a friend's gamertag. On the web, enter the email address tied to their Microsoft account.
5. Pay. They get a code by email, and an Xbox notification if you picked a gamertag.

Two rules to know. Microsoft's gifting rules say a gift can only be redeemed in the country or region where you bought it, so don't send a US gift to a friend in the UK. And Microsoft has historically excluded pre-orders from gifting. That matters for GTA 6 until it comes out on November 19, so the gift option may not show up before then. If the gift option doesn't appear for an edition, an Xbox gift card for the right amount works too.

## How to gift GTA 6 on PS5

PlayStation doesn't let you send a specific game to someone's account. What you can give is a PlayStation Store gift card. Sony says they work on digital games, add-ons and subscriptions, and they can be bought from retailers or sent digitally by email with a design and a message.

1. Buy a card for at least $79.99 for Standard or $99.99 for Ultimate (US prices). Sales tax may push the total higher, so a little extra helps.
2. Buy a card for the same country as their PlayStation account. Cards are sold per region.
3. They redeem the 12-digit code on their PS5, in the PlayStation App or on the web, then buy the game with the credit.

It's a little less magical than unwrapping the game itself, but they can't end up with the wrong edition.

## The boxed copy is also a download code

The physical Standard Edition, in stores since November 12, comes with a download code, not a disc. That makes it a perfectly good gift for either console as long as you buy the right version. It works on a PS5 Digital Edition too, since there's no disc to read. It's Standard only, because Ultimate is digital only. Our [disc page](https://callunc.com/disc/) explains how the code works.

## GTA 6 Christmas gifts for people who already have it

If they already own Standard, the Ultimate upgrade adds the same 16 extra items as the Ultimate Edition. Xbox listed the upgrade at $20, as reported by Forbes. On PlayStation the price only shows to Standard owners. There's no way to send the upgrade directly on PlayStation, so a gift card covers it.

One thing a December gift won't include: the Vintage Vice City Pack. Rockstar has tied it to digital purchases made before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store), so a gift bought after that won't come with it. Check the store page for current bonuses before you pay.

## Gift card and code scams to skip

Holiday season brings out the fakes. Skip anything that offers:

- "Free GTA 6 codes" for following an account, filling out a survey or sharing a post.
- Cheap keys from sites that aren't the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store or a known retailer. Some resell codes bought with stolen cards, and those can be revoked.
- A PC or mobile copy. Neither exists.

Buy from the console store or a known retailer, keep the receipt, and hand over the code yourself.
