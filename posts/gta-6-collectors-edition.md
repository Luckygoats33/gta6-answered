There is no GTA 6 collector's edition of the game itself. Rockstar sells two editions, Standard and Ultimate, and neither comes in a fancy box. What Rockstar did sell is a $399.99 merchandise box called the Goodtime State, Vice City Collection, and the catch is in the fine print: as Push Square reported, it doesn't include the game. GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026, and as of November 16, 2026, Rockstar hadn't announced any other edition.

If you've seen "GTA 6 Collector's Edition" listings, here's how to make sense of them.

## The editions Rockstar actually sells

- **Standard Edition, $79.99.** Digital, or boxed. The box holds a download code, not a disc.
- **Ultimate Edition, $99.99.** Digital only. Standard plus extra single-player vehicles, weapons, outfits, shops and locations.

That's the whole list. There's no Deluxe, no Gold, no Special Edition. Standard owners can buy the Ultimate upgrade later, which is the only way to "step up" an edition. Our [Standard vs Ultimate page](https://callunc.com/standard-vs-ultimate/) has the short comparison, and our [disc page](https://callunc.com/disc/) explains the code in the box.

## The $399.99 collector's box

Rockstar unveiled the Goodtime State, Vice City Collection in September 2026, priced at $399.99 in the US and £349.99 in the UK, sold through the Rockstar Store in limited quantities with a November 19 release. It's themed around Macca the Gator, a fictional Leonida TV show character. As reported at the reveal, it holds 11 items:

1. A Macca the Gator figure with a twist-open base
2. Oakley Frogskins sunglasses with a GTA VI logo
3. A Leonida Keys crossbody bag
4. A New Era snapback hat
5. A Macca the Gator magnetic mirror
6. A Chunkee the Manatee shot glass
7. A Vice City swizzle spoon
8. A Vice City keychain
9. A set of nine Leonida Keys enamel pins in a tin
10. A pack of nine vinyl stickers
11. A double-sided poster with Macca art on one side and a Leonida map on the other

No game, no code, no in-game items mentioned. If you want the game, you buy it separately. That's why so many people searched "GTA 6 collector's edition" and came away confused: the product exists, but it's merch, not an edition.

## Is this normal for Rockstar?

Not exactly. Rockstar sold real collector's editions before. GTA IV had one with an art book and soundtrack, and GTA V's came with a map, steelbook and a money bag, as Insider Gaming recounted. Red Dead Redemption 2 is the closer match: its collector's box was sold without the game. GTA 6 follows the RDR2 approach.

Whether a boxed premium edition ever appears for GTA 6 is anyone's guess. Rockstar hasn't announced one, and Insider Gaming noted that GTA 6's editions were announced without one, which broke Rockstar's old pattern. Don't buy anything on the promise of a "future collector's edition."

## GTA 6 steelbook and special edition questions

- **Steelbook:** Rockstar hasn't announced an official GTA 6 steelbook. Some retailers in some countries offer steelbooks as their own pre-order or store bonuses on big games. If one shows up, check that it's from a real retailer and that the game code is included. A steelbook with no code is just a case.
- **Special edition:** There isn't one. Rockstar has two editions.
- **The cheap poster:** Rockstar said it would offer the cover art and a Leonida map as free digital downloads on release day, plus a double-sided physical poster from the Rockstar Store shortly after launch for $0.99, or free with any Rockstar Store purchase. If a poster is what you wanted from a collector's box, that's the cheap route.

## Fake listings and how to spot them

Big launches attract fake "collector's editions," and GTA 6 had them before launch. Before buying:

1. **Check the seller.** The collector's box was sold through the Rockstar Store. A third-party listing at a markup may be a resale, and a listing far below $399.99 is a red flag.
2. **Read what's included.** A real GTA 6 edition names the platform (PS5 or Xbox Series X|S). A box that says "PC" or "Switch" is fake.
3. **Ignore placeholder prices.** Before launch, a European retailer listing reportedly showed a GTA 6 Collector's Edition at €199.99. Rockstar never announced it. Placeholder listings aren't products.
4. **Watch for "bundles" from marketplaces.** A boxed Standard copy plus some unofficial merch isn't a collector's edition, however it's labeled.
