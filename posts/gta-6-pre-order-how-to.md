If you're searching for GTA 6 how to pre order, you're a little late, in the best way: the game came out on November 19, 2026, so there's nothing left to pre-order. You just buy it. On PS5 that's the PlayStation Store, on Xbox it's the Microsoft Store, and a boxed copy with a download code works on either. The one thing you can't get anymore is the pre-order bonus.

Here's how the pre-order worked, what changed at launch, and the steps to buy and download it now.

## How GTA 6 pre-orders worked

Rockstar opened pre-orders on June 25, 2026, at midnight local time, on the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Store and at retailers. Pre-ordering did three things:

- **Locked in the Vintage Vice City Pack**, the bonus for digital pre-orders and purchases made before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store): a '55 Vapid Stanier with a garage in Shore Court, outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and a tropical weapon pattern inspired by Tommy Vercetti's shirt.
- **Added a free month of GTA+** for digital pre-orders on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store. That membership auto-renews at the regular price unless you cancel it, which is worth checking if you pre-ordered and forgot.
- **Let you pre-load.** Pre-loading opened November 10 so the game was installed before launch.

None of that gave early access. Everyone unlocked at their local midnight.

## Buying GTA 6 on PS5 now

1. Open the PlayStation Store on your PS5 (or the PlayStation App on your phone).
2. Search for Grand Theft Auto VI.
3. Pick Standard ($79.99 in the US) or Ultimate ($99.99).
4. Buy it, then select Download. It goes straight to your library.

If you bought a boxed copy, open the PlayStation Store, use the three-dot menu, choose Redeem Code and enter the code from the box. PS5 codes only work on a PlayStation account from the same region as the code, so check the box matches your account before you open it.

## Buying GTA 6 on Xbox now

1. Open the Microsoft Store on your Xbox Series X|S, or the Xbox app on your phone.
2. Search for Grand Theft Auto VI and choose your edition.
3. Buy it and install. If you bought on your phone, you can send the install to your console remotely.

For a boxed copy, use Redeem in the Microsoft Store or redeem.microsoft.com. Xbox codes aren't region locked, so a box from another country works fine.

## What about PC?

People ask GTA 6 how to pre order on PC, and the answer is simple: you can't. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version as of November 16, 2026. Any site taking PC pre-orders or offering a PC key is a scam. Our [PC answer](https://callunc.com/pc/) has more.

## Choosing your edition

The difference is $20 and 16 extra items in the single-player game, including the '95 Grotti Cheetah, a pair of revolvers, engraved pistols for Jason and Lucia, extra mod shops, a salon, a tattoo parlor and some clothing. Ultimate is digital only.

You don't have to decide now. Standard owners can buy the Ultimate upgrade any time. It's listed at $20 on Xbox, the same as the price gap, and on PlayStation the upgrade price shows only to Standard owners. Start with Standard if you're unsure. ## Claiming the pre-order bonus

If you did pre-order, the Vintage Vice City Pack should be in your game already. If it isn't showing up, the usual fixes are:

- Make sure you're signed into the same PlayStation or Xbox account you bought with.
- Check that the game is fully updated.
- Look in the places Rockstar said the items live: the Shore Court garage for the Stanier, and your wardrobe for the outfits.
- If it's still missing, contact Rockstar Support with your purchase details.

If you're buying now, the bonus isn't included. Rockstar hasn't announced a way to buy the Vintage Vice City Pack separately. Don't trust anyone selling "pre-order bonus codes" online.
