If you're searching for GTA 6 how to pre order, you're just in time: the game comes out on November 19, 2026, and pre-orders are open until launch. On PS5 that's the PlayStation Store, on Xbox it's the Microsoft Store, and a boxed copy with a download code works on either. The one thing you can't get after launch is the pre-order bonus.

Here's how the pre-order works, what changes at launch, and the steps to buy and pre-load it now.

## How GTA 6 pre-orders work

Rockstar opened pre-orders on June 25, 2026, at midnight local time, on the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Store and at retailers. Pre-ordering does three things:

- **Locks in the Vintage Vice City Pack**, the bonus for digital pre-orders and purchases made before launch (around November 19 to 20, depending on the store): a '55 Vapid Stanier with a garage in Shore Court, outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and a tropical weapon pattern inspired by Tommy Vercetti's shirt.
- **Adds a free month of GTA+** for digital pre-orders on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store. That membership auto-renews at the regular price unless you cancel it, which is worth checking if you pre-ordered and forgot.
- **Lets you pre-load.** Pre-loading opened November 10 so the game is installed before launch.

None of that gives early access. Everyone unlocks at their local midnight on November 19.

## Pre-ordering GTA 6 on PS5 now

1. Open the PlayStation Store on your PS5 (or the PlayStation App on your phone).
2. Search for Grand Theft Auto VI.
3. Pick Standard ($79.99 in the US) or Ultimate ($99.99).
4. Pre-order it, then select Download to pre-load. It goes straight to your library.

If you bought a boxed copy, open the PlayStation Store, use the three-dot menu, choose Redeem Code and enter the code from the box. PS5 codes only work on a PlayStation account from the same region as the code, so check the box matches your account before you open it.

## Pre-ordering GTA 6 on Xbox now

1. Open the Microsoft Store on your Xbox Series X|S, or the Xbox app on your phone.
2. Search for Grand Theft Auto VI and choose your edition.
3. Pre-order it and start the pre-load. If you bought on your phone, you can send the install to your console remotely.

For a boxed copy, use Redeem in the Microsoft Store or redeem.microsoft.com. Xbox codes aren't region locked, so a box from another country works fine.

## What about PC?

People ask GTA 6 how to pre order on PC, and the answer is simple: you can't. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version as of November 16, 2026. Any site taking PC pre-orders or offering a PC key is a scam. Our [PC answer](https://callunc.com/pc/) has more.

## Choosing your edition

The difference is $20 and 16 extra items in the single-player game, including the '95 Grotti Cheetah, a pair of revolvers, engraved pistols for Jason and Lucia, extra mod shops, a salon, a tattoo parlor and some clothing. Ultimate is digital only.

You don't have to decide now. Standard owners can buy the Ultimate upgrade any time. It's listed at $20 on Xbox, the same as the price gap, and on PlayStation the upgrade price shows only to Standard owners. Start with Standard if you're unsure. ## Claiming the pre-order bonus

If you pre-order, the Vintage Vice City Pack should be in your game at launch. If it isn't showing up on launch day, the usual fixes are:

- Make sure you're signed into the same PlayStation or Xbox account you bought with.
- Check that the game is fully updated.
- Look in the places Rockstar said the items live: the Shore Court garage for the Stanier, and your wardrobe for the outfits.
- If it's still missing, contact Rockstar Support with your purchase details.

If you buy after launch, the bonus isn't included. Rockstar hasn't announced a way to buy the Vintage Vice City Pack separately. Don't trust anyone selling "pre-order bonus codes" online.
