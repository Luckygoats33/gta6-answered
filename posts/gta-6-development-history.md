How many years of development went into GTA 6? By Rockstar's own account, the game has been in development since 2015, and most of the team moved onto it after Red Dead Redemption 2 shipped in 2018. It comes out on November 19, 2026. So the honest answer is about eleven years from first work to launch, with roughly eight of those in full production. And it arrives thirteen years after GTA V, which is the gap most people actually feel.

## When development started

Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North, told the French outlet Trois Couleurs in 2026 that the game had been in development since 2015. In the same interview, he said the majority of the team only really got rolling on it after Red Dead Redemption 2 released in 2018.

That matches how Rockstar works. A small group plans and prototypes while the big team finishes the current game, then everyone piles on. So when people ask "GTA 6, how many years to make," both "eleven" and "eight" are fair, depending on whether you count the early planning.

Before Nelson's comments, there were plenty of guesses floating around, some putting the start as early as 2014. Rockstar never confirmed those, and Nelson's 2015 date is the one on the record.

## The announcement

For years, Rockstar said almost nothing. GTA V came out in 2013, got re-released on two console generations, and GTA Online kept printing money. The silence became a meme.

Then, on February 4, 2022, at the end of a blog post mostly about GTA V's next-gen versions, Rockstar said development on the next Grand Theft Auto was "well underway." That was the whole announcement. No name, no date, no setting.

In September 2022, a hacker stole and posted early development footage. It was one of the biggest leaks in the industry. We don't cover the footage, but it shaped everything after: Rockstar stayed even more guarded.

## The trailers

- **Trailer 1, December 2023.** Planned for December 5, it leaked a day early and Rockstar put it out on December 4. It named Grand Theft Auto VI, showed Lucia and Vice City, and promised 2025. It broke YouTube's 24-hour record for a non-music video. Our [Trailer 1 explainer](https://callunc.com/blog/gta-6-trailer-1/) has the full story.
- **Trailer 2, May 2025.** Introduced Jason properly, plus official profiles for the supporting cast.
- **The Extended Look, August 2026.** A long gameplay video that premiered on Netflix before YouTube. No, you can't play it on Netflix; see our [Netflix answer](https://callunc.com/netflix/).

## Delays and the final date

The 2025 window didn't survive:

1. **May 2025.** Rockstar delayed the game to May 26, 2026.
2. **November 6, 2025.** Rockstar moved it again, to Thursday, November 19, 2026. It apologized for the longer wait and said the extra months would let the team finish the game with the polish players expect.

Bloomberg's Jason Schreier reported that people he spoke to at Rockstar had never seen fall 2025 as a realistic window, and that management wanted to avoid the heavy crunch Rockstar was criticized for in the past. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said the company had believed in the May date but needed more time.

After that, the date has held. Rockstar announced prices on June 24, 2026 ($79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate), opened pre-orders the next day, opened pre-loading on November 10, and put boxed copies with download codes in stores on November 12.

## Rockstar's team size

Rockstar hasn't published a headcount for GTA 6. Rockstar North in Edinburgh, the studio behind most mainline GTA games, is where Rob Nelson works, and he's been the public face of development. You'll see figures quoted online for Rockstar's total staff, but those count the whole company, not one project, so we're not repeating them as a GTA 6 number.

## Launch

GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026, at midnight local time, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, single-player at launch. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or a date for GTA 6 Online as of November 16, 2026. The [facts page](https://callunc.com/facts/) keeps the confirmed details in one place.

## Why it took so long

Rockstar hasn't given one reason, so this is our read of the public timeline. Red Dead Redemption 2 kept most of the company busy until 2018. GTA Online stayed a huge live game the whole time. The 2022 leak didn't help. And Rockstar has a long habit of not shipping until it's ready, which the two delays confirmed. Put together, you get thirteen years between numbered games. If you were wondering how long GTA 6 has been in development, the short answer is: long enough that people who played GTA V in high school are buying GTA 6 with a mortgage.
