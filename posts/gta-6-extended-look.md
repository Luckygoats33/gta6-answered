If you're looking for the GTA 6 gameplay trailer, the one you want is "Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look." It's the long gameplay video Rockstar released on August 27, 2026, premiering on Netflix before it went up on Rockstar's YouTube channel later the same day. Netflix lists it at 27 minutes and says it was made entirely of in-game footage captured on PS5. Trailers 1 and 2 were mostly story and mood. The Extended Look is the one that actually shows you how the game plays.

GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026, so you'll soon be able to compare the video with the real thing. Until then it's worth watching if you haven't, because it's the clearest official walkthrough of the game's systems.

## What the Extended Look is

Think of it as a guided demo rather than a trailer. Instead of quick cuts set to music, it follows gameplay for long stretches, with missions, driving and open-world activities played out at length.

A few details people get wrong:

- **It's a video, not a playable game.** You can't play GTA 6 on Netflix. The game is coming to PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Our [Netflix page](https://callunc.com/netflix/) explains the confusion.
- **It was captured on PS5.** VGC reported the footage came from a base PS5, not a PS5 Pro or a PC.
- **Netflix added subtitles.** Tudum said the video streamed with subtitles in 13 languages.

## Where to watch it

You have two official options:

1. **Netflix**, where it premiered as a TV-MA special.
2. **Rockstar's YouTube channel and the official GTA VI site**, which lists An Extended Look next to the Launch Trailer, Trailer 2 and Trailer 1.

Skip re-uploads with clickbait titles. They're usually cropped, compressed, or padded with someone talking over it.

## Jason and Lucia on screen

The video puts both leads front and center and shows how the two-protagonist setup works in practice. From VGC's rundown of what was shown:

- **It opens with a drug deal going bad,** which sets the tone fast.
- **Everyday moments get screen time.** Jason walks through Lucia's apartment and grabs food from the fridge.
- **Switching between them is quick,** in a way that looks similar to GTA V's character swaps.
- **Missions can be played as Jason or Lucia,** on your own or with the two working together.
- **Robberies split the roles.** One character hits a store while the other waits in the getaway car.

## Open world details

This is the part most players rewatch. The video showed:

- **Social media inside the game.** Lucia drives while an influencer streams from her car, with the stream's chat on screen.
- **Scale and draw distance.** Lucia parachutes off a building, and the camera shows how far the city stretches.
- **Wetlands and water.** A fan boat on Everglades-style marshland and kayaks on open water.
- **Side activities.** Working out and dirt bike races both appear.
- **A reworked wanted system.** VGC reported that a circle on the map now marks where police are searching, instead of individual police markers on the minimap. Changing masks, hats and clothes before a crime can bring your wanted level down.

If you want the bigger picture of the setting, our [map size page](https://callunc.com/map-size/) covers what Rockstar has said about Leonida's size and regions.

## What the Extended Look didn't show

Just as useful is what wasn't in it:

- **No GTA 6 Online.** The video is about the single-player game. Rockstar hasn't announced an Online date. See our [online page](https://callunc.com/online/).
- **No PC version.** Everything was PS5 footage, and Rockstar hasn't announced PC.
- **No frame rates or graphics modes.** Nothing in the video tells you how the game runs on each console. Rockstar covered that separately on November 10: its target is 30 fps on every console, PS5 Pro included, and the Pro adds PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows.
- **No full map.** You see slices of Leonida, not a map screen of the whole state.

Treat anything claiming to be "the full gameplay list" from the video with some suspicion. Fan breakdowns often mix in guesses and leaks. Stick to what's on screen, and from November 19, to what you see in your own playthrough.

## Why it mattered

Take-Two pointed ahead to the Extended Look in its August 7, 2026 earnings report, the same report where it reconfirmed the November 19 date. It is Rockstar's main pre-launch showcase, and for many players it went a long way toward answering whether the game will play as well as the trailers look. If you only watch one official GTA 6 video, make it this one.
