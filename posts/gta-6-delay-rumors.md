Will GTA 6 be delayed again? That question has an answer now: no. GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026, at midnight local time in each region, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, exactly as Rockstar had said since November 2025. Every "third delay" rumor in 2026 turned out to be wrong. There were two real delays, both announced by Rockstar itself, and both happened before 2026 started.

This post walks through how the date held, the signs that it would, and why delay rumors kept spreading anyway. Useful the next time someone tells you GTA 6 Online "will get delayed again."

## The two real delays

It helps to remember what a real Rockstar delay looks like, because neither one was a surprise leak:

1. **Fall 2025 to May 26, 2026.** GTA 6 was first announced for 2025. In 2025, Rockstar moved it to May 26, 2026.
2. **May 26, 2026 to November 19, 2026.** On November 6, 2025, Rockstar posted that the game would now release on Thursday, November 19, 2026. VGC reported the statement apologized for the longer wait and said the extra months would let the team finish the game with the polish players expect.

Both came straight from Rockstar's own channels, in plain language, with a new date attached. That's the pattern. A real delay doesn't arrive as a blurry screenshot from an anonymous account.

## What Rockstar's support page said

Through 2026, Rockstar Support kept a page listing regional pre-load and release times for both consoles, and it never moved off November 19 at midnight local time. Rockstar's GTA VI site said the same, with pre-load news in November and the release date front and center. Our [launch times page](https://callunc.com/launch-times/) keeps the region-by-region table.

If you want one rule for future rumors: when the official support page and the official site haven't changed, the date hasn't changed.

## Take-Two's August 2026 confirmation

Take-Two is a public company, so it can't be casual about its biggest release. It reconfirmed the November 19 date on its earnings calls during 2026, including the August 7, 2026 report, where it said the game wasn't delayed and pointed to the Extended Look gameplay video coming on August 27. CEO Strauss Zelnick called the pre-order levels "unprecedented and astonishing," while noting nobody knew yet how that would turn into sales.

A publisher planning its whole fiscal year around a date has every reason to warn investors early if that date is slipping. It didn't.

## Boxed copies were already in stores

The final tell came in November:

- **Pre-load opened November 10,** so digital buyers had the game on their consoles more than a week early.
- **Boxed copies hit stores November 12,** with a download code inside. See our [disc page](https://callunc.com/disc/).

Once the game is downloading to millions of consoles and the boxes are on shelves, a delay isn't really on the table. At that point, the only question left was what time your region hit midnight.

## Where delay rumors come from

The rumors didn't come from nowhere. They came from the same few places every time:

- **Fake screenshots** of "leaked" Rockstar emails or store pages.
- **Misread store dates.** A store page showing November 18 in one region, because of time zones, looked like a date change to people who didn't check.
- **Old news recycled.** Articles about the 2025 delays kept getting reshared as if they were new.
- **Engagement bait.** "GTA 6 delayed AGAIN?" with a question mark gets clicks whether it's true or not.
- **Prediction-market chatter.** Odds on a delay got passed around as if they were information. They're bets, not news.

## What "delayed again" means now

With the game out, the delay talk has moved to things that don't have dates yet:

- **GTA 6 Online.** Bloomberg reported that multiplayer is expected in 2027, per Vice's summary. Rockstar hasn't announced a date, so it can't be "delayed" in any official sense. If it arrives later than the report suggested, that's a report being wrong, not Rockstar missing a promise.
- **A PC version.** Rockstar hasn't announced one, so there's no date to slip.
- **Patches and updates.** Check Rockstar's patch notes rather than social posts about what's "coming."

Same rule as before: if Rockstar didn't announce a date, nobody can tell you it was delayed. And if Rockstar does announce one, a delay will come from Rockstar too.
