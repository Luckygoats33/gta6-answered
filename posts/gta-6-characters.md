The GTA 6 characters Rockstar has introduced before launch come down to two leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, plus a supporting cast of about a dozen named people on Rockstar's official "Only in Leonida" pages. Those profiles went up with Trailer 2 in May 2025. Everything below comes from that official material, so there are no story spoilers past what Rockstar chose to show.

Once you're playing, you'll know more than this page does. Until then, this is who you'll be spending time with.

## The GTA 6 main characters

### Jason Duval

Rockstar's bio paints Jason as a guy who wants an easy life and keeps not getting one. He grew up around grifters and crooks, joined the Army after a rough adolescence, then ended up in the Leonida Keys working for local drug runners. When we meet him, he's living rent-free in a property owned by a smuggler named Brian, in exchange for helping with shakedowns. He's the steady, practical half of the pair, and his bio notes he has bigger plans for himself and his friend Cal.

### Lucia Caminos

Lucia is the first woman to lead a modern GTA story, something fans asked about for years. Her father taught her to fight as a kid. She ended up in the Leonida Penitentiary for fighting for her family, and got out, as Rockstar puts it, by luck. She wants the good life her mother has dreamed about since their days in Liberty City, and she sees Jason as a possible way to get it. She's the opening shot of the first trailer, which tells you where Rockstar's head was.

Rockstar's main page sums the pair up simply: partners who know the deck is stacked against them and have to rely on each other when an easy score goes wrong.

## The supporting cast

These are the other people with official profiles, or named inside those profiles.

- **Cal Hampton.** Jason's friend and an associate of Brian's. A homebody who listens in on Coast Guard radio and, per Rockstar, keeps a lot of private browser tabs open. Probably the comic relief, possibly the conspiracy guy.
- **Brian Heder.** A drug runner left over from the Keys' old smuggling days. He runs product through his boat yard and is Jason's landlord, of a sort.
- **Lori.** Brian's third wife. She helps run the boat yard and, according to the bio, keeps the sangria coming.
- **Boobie Ike.** A Vice City legend who turned his street money into a legitimate empire: real estate, a strip club and a recording studio.
- **Dre'Quan Priest.** A young, aspiring music mogul who used to book acts at Boobie's club. He and Boobie are partners in Only Raw Records.
- **Real Dimez.** A rap duo, Bae-Luxe and Roxy, friends since high school with a big social media following. An early hit with a local rapper named DWNPLY made their name, and they're now signed to Only Raw.
- **Raul Bautista.** A veteran bank robber who recruits people for big, risky jobs. Rockstar's bio says his recklessness makes every score more dangerous for his crew, which sounds like a promise.

## What the trailers and Extended Look added

Trailer 1 in December 2023 was mostly Lucia, with Jason as a masked figure behind her. Trailer 2 in May 2025 gave Jason his name and screen time and introduced most of the cast above. The Extended Look on Netflix in August 2026 showed both leads in actual gameplay: robbing stores, getting into shootouts, and Jason using a slow-motion aiming ability. Outlets picked up more names from dialogue in that video, but Rockstar didn't publish new official bios for them, so we're leaving them off this list.

## Who voices the characters?

Rockstar hasn't published an official voice cast for GTA 6 as of November 16, 2026. Rockstar traditionally credits actors with the game rather than announcing them beforehand. You'll see guesses and fan "confirmations" online. Treat them as rumors until the credits roll.

## Can you switch between Jason and Lucia?

GTA V let you swap between three leads. Rockstar has shown both Jason and Lucia playable in GTA 6 footage, and the game's marketing is built around the two of them. How and when the game lets you switch is something you'll learn by playing; Rockstar hasn't spelled it out.
