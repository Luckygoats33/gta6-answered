GTA 6 languages come down to one rule: the voices are English only, and the text is translated. The Xbox Store lists 13 supported languages for the game, and outlets that checked the store listings in June 2026 reported that every language other than English gets translated menus and subtitles, not dubbed voices. That's how Rockstar has done it for years, and GTA 6, which comes out on November 19, 2026, keeps the tradition.

So if you were hoping to hear Jason and Lucia in Spanish, Portuguese or Hindi, that's not happening. If you just want to read along in your language, there's a good chance you're covered.

Why no dubs? Rockstar hasn't explained it for GTA 6. Its last two big games, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2, also shipped with English voices and translated text, and a game packed with radio shows, side characters and street chatter would be a huge job to re-record.

## The reported list of GTA 6 languages

The Xbox Store page says "13 supported languages" but doesn't spell them out on the page. When the store listings went up in late June 2026, Brazilian site Einerd and Polish site PPE.pl both reported the same 13:

1. English (full audio, menus and subtitles)
2. French
3. Italian
4. German
5. Spanish (Spain)
6. Spanish (Mexico)
7. Portuguese (Brazil)
8. Polish
9. Russian
10. Japanese
11. Korean
12. Chinese (Simplified)
13. Chinese (Traditional)

For everything after English on that list, the reports say you get the interface, menus and subtitles in that language, with English voices. Spanish site Vandal reported the same thing from the Microsoft Store listing: text in several languages, audio in English only.

Treat the list as reported from store listings rather than an official Rockstar announcement. If your language matters, check the "languages" section on the store page for your own region before you buy.

## GTA 6 Spanish: two versions, no dub

There's good news and bad news for "gta 6 spanish" searches. The good news: both European Spanish and Latin American (Mexico) Spanish are on the reported list, so the text matches the Spanish you actually speak. The bad news: there's no Spanish voice track. Vandal's headline put it plainly, saying GTA 6 keeps the tradition of English-only voices.

## GTA 6 Hindi and other missing languages

Hindi isn't on the reported list, as text or audio. Neither are Arabic or Turkish. If you play in one of those languages, expect to play with English menus and subtitles.

Rockstar hasn't announced any language additions for GTA 6 as of November 16, 2026. Don't count on it until Rockstar's patch notes say so. And be wary of downloads promising a "Hindi patch" or fan dub for console. There's no legitimate way to add a fan translation to the console version, and those links are often malware or scams.

## GTA 6 subtitles

Subtitles matter more than usual in a GTA game, because so much of the story and the radio chatter happens while you're driving. A few tips:

- **Turn subtitles on early.** Check the game's settings menu for subtitle and text options as soon as you start.
- **Match the console language.** If the game doesn't show the language you want, check your console's system language. On PS5 that's under Settings, System, Language. On Xbox it's under Settings, System, Language and location. Some console games take their text language from the system setting, so it's worth checking.
- **The radio is still English.** Expect songs, talk shows and ads in English no matter what you choose.

## Don't mix up the game with the Extended Look video

One source of confusion: Netflix said its stream of the Extended Look gameplay video had subtitles in 13 languages, including English, German, Spanish, Japanese and Korean. That was the video's subtitles on Netflix, not the game's language list. The numbers happen to match, which didn't help.

## The short version

- **Audio:** English only.
- **Text and subtitles:** 13 languages reported, including two kinds of Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Korean and both forms of Chinese.
- **Not included:** Hindi, Arabic and Turkish, among others.
