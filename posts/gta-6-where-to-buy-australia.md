If you're in Australia, GTA 6 at JB Hi-Fi is one of the easiest options: the boxed Standard Edition for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, which holds a download code rather than a disc. You can also buy it from Big W, EB Games, Amazon Australia, or straight from the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store. The game came out on November 19, 2026. Here's how the options compare and what the Australian prices looked like.

## Australian prices

The recommended retail prices reported when pre-orders opened in June 2026:

- **Standard Edition:** A$129.95
- **Ultimate Edition:** A$159.95, digital only

Those are recommended prices. Retailers set their own prices on the boxed Standard Edition, which is where you'll find any discount. Prices change, so check the listing on the day. Our [price page](https://callunc.com/price/) has the US figures if you're comparing.

## Digital stores

Buying through the PlayStation Store on PS5 or the Microsoft Store on Xbox is the simplest route:

- It's the only way to get the **Ultimate Edition**.
- The game lands on your account immediately, so no code to redeem.
- If you start on Standard, you can buy the **Ultimate upgrade** from the same store later.

The catch is price. Retailers compete on the boxed copy, so that's where small discounts tend to show up.

## JB Hi-Fi

JB Hi-Fi took pre-orders for the boxed Standard Edition on PS5 and Xbox Series X. When Man of Many checked in June 2026, JB listed it at A$129, a touch under RRP. Check the current price before you assume it's still the same.

A few practical notes for buying GTA 6 at JB Hi-Fi:

- **Pick the right console.** The PS5 and Xbox boxes have different codes.
- **Keep the receipt.** If the code has a problem, the store will want it.

## Big W

Big W sells console games and accessories too, and many people search for GTA 6 at Big W for both Xbox and PS5. We couldn't verify a specific Big W price for GTA 6, so check its site or catalogue. The same rules apply: it's the boxed Standard Edition with a code, and you need the version for your console. Big W doesn't sell the Ultimate Edition, because nobody sells a boxed Ultimate.

If you're after the GTA 6 controllers at Big W: Sony made two limited-edition GTA 6 DualSense controllers for November 19, sold through PlayStation Direct where available and through other retailers. Sony didn't publish an Australian price in its announcement, and we couldn't confirm which local stores stock them. Any DualSense works with the game. The special ones are just for looks.

## EB Games and Amazon Australia

- **EB Games** listed the Standard Edition at A$129.95 for pre-order, with a A$10 deposit.
- **Amazon Australia** took physical pre-orders with home delivery.

Both sell the same code-in-a-box Standard Edition as everyone else.

## Boxed copies have a code, not a disc

This is the thing to understand before you buy anywhere. The boxed GTA 6 has no disc. It holds a download code you redeem on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store. Boxed copies went on sale from November 12, 2026.

What that means:

- It works on a **Digital Edition PS5** or any console without a disc drive.
- Once the code is redeemed, the game is tied to your account, so you **can't trade it in** like a disc.
- The download is **up to 212 GB**, so on Australian internet, give yourself time.

The [disc page](https://callunc.com/disc/) explains more.

## When it unlocked in Australia

Each region unlocked at its own midnight on November 19. In Sydney, on daylight saving time, that was 13:00 UTC on November 18, so Australia was among the first in the world, behind New Zealand. Other states unlocked at their own local midnight. The full table is on the [launch times page](https://callunc.com/launch-times/).

## A word on bargain sellers

Avoid "cheap GTA 6 keys" from overseas resellers and account sharing offers. Console copies come from the console stores or a code in a retail box. And there's no PC or mobile version, so any listing for one is a scam.
