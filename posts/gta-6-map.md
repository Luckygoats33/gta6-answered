The GTA 6 map is the state of Leonida, Rockstar's fictional take on Florida, with Vice City as its big city. Rockstar has named six regions and says the whole thing is roughly twice the size of GTA V's map. GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026, and Rockstar said it would post a free digital map of Leonida on release day, so you don't have to rely on fan sketches.

Here's what Rockstar actually told us about the place, before and around launch.

## The state of Leonida

Leonida is a state, not a city. That's the biggest change from GTA V, which was one city, Los Santos, plus Blaine County. Vice City is the headline location, but most of the map is everything around it: swamps, islands, beach towns, farm country and a national park.

Rockstar's marketing calls Leonida the sunniest place in America, with a dark side. The setting is a parody, so expect familiar Florida shapes and jokes, not a one-to-one copy. Outlets have pointed out that famous real places like the big theme parks and the space coast probably won't have stand-ins, but Rockstar hasn't gone through a list.

## The six regions on the GTA 6 map

Rockstar introduced the regions on the game's website in May 2025. Paraphrasing Rockstar's own descriptions:

1. **Vice City.** The metropolis, and Rockstar's picture of flashy American ambition and money chasing. It's GTA's Miami, last seen in 2006's Vice City Stories.
2. **Leonida Keys.** A string of tropical islands with retirees, boaters and sea turtles, and, Rockstar warns, some of the most dangerous waters in America.
3. **Grassrivers.** A swampy wilderness Rockstar says holds predators worse than alligators. It plays the role of the Everglades.
4. **Port Gellhorn.** A faded vacation town with empty shops and parking lots full of the debris of hard living.
5. **Ambrosia.** Called the heart of Leonida: traditional values, factories and a local biker gang.
6. **Mount Kalaga National Park.** Lush backcountry with hunting, fishing and off-road trails.

## How big the map is

Rockstar's line, repeated by development head Aaron Garbut in interviews before launch, is that Leonida is roughly twice the size of GTA V's map. Not exactly double, roughly. Rockstar also said the total landmass comes close to the playable areas of GTA IV, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 put together.

Two cautions:

- **There's no official square-mile figure.** Any exact area you see comes from outlets and fans doing math, not from Rockstar.
- **Size isn't the same as content.** Garbut told Game Informer the team obsessed over every part of the world, and Rockstar's narrative lead Rupert Humphries said the story can't take you everywhere. So there's exploring left after the credits.

## What Rockstar hasn't shown

Before launch, Rockstar didn't publish a labeled map of its own or detail every town and interior. Some questions it left open:

- Exactly where each region's borders sit
- How many interiors you can enter
- Whether the map grows later with updates or [GTA 6 Online](https://callunc.com/online/), which Rockstar hasn't dated

If you see a "full leaked GTA 6 map" from before launch, treat it as fan work or worse. The 2022 leak happened, but this site doesn't use leaked material, and old leak-based maps were drawn years before the game was finished.

## GTA 6 map layout: where to get a real one

Now that the game is out, you have three honest options.

- **The in-game map.** It's the source of truth, and it fills in as you explore.
- **Rockstar's free digital map.** Rockstar said it would offer a printable cover art poster and a Leonida map as free downloads from its website on release day, plus a double-sided physical poster from the Rockstar Store shortly after launch.
- **Fan interactive maps.** These usually show up within weeks of a big release, built from players' own exploring. They're useful for collectibles, but check that a site isn't asking you to download anything or log in with your console account. A map is a web page, not an app.
