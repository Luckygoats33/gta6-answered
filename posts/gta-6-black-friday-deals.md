Don't expect a big GTA 6 Black Friday discount. The game came out on November 19, 2026, and Black Friday lands on November 27, eight days later. Brand-new games of this size almost never get real price cuts in their first weeks, and as of November 16 no major US retailer had announced a Black Friday price on the game itself. What you can find are side deals: rewards, gift card bonuses and console promotions.

So the useful question isn't "where is the cheapest GTA 6," it's "what counts as a good deal, and what's a trap." Here's how to tell.

## The prices you're comparing against

In the US, the Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99. Those prices were announced by Take-Two and Rockstar, so they're confirmed, not just reported. Anything you see should be measured against those two.

A few things worth knowing before you shop:

- The boxed Standard copy holds a download code, not a disc. You still download the full game. Our [disc page](https://callunc.com/disc/) explains it.
- Ultimate is digital only. If a store lists a "physical Ultimate," read the listing twice.
- Standard owners can buy the Ultimate upgrade later, so you don't have to decide now. See [upgrade to Ultimate](https://callunc.com/upgrade-to-ultimate/).

## What GTA 6 Black Friday deals look like

The realistic deals this year fall into a few buckets.

- **Retailer rewards instead of discounts.** Before launch, IBTimes UK reported a Best Buy offer that gave a $50 certificate to members who pre-ordered GTA 6 and bought an eligible console during its Black Friday sale. The certificate came after both purchases and didn't lower the checkout price. That offer was tied to pre-orders, so it's a good example of the shape of these deals rather than one you can still use.
- **Console bundles and console discounts.** If you need hardware anyway, the console is where Black Friday money usually is. Compare the bundle price to the console's sale price plus $79.99 for the game. Sometimes the bundle wins, sometimes it's just packaging.
- **Discounted store gift cards.** Some retailers sell PlayStation Store or Xbox gift cards at a small discount or with a bonus around Black Friday. Buying one from a real retailer and using it on the digital game is a legitimate way to shave a few dollars.
- **Membership coupons.** Store memberships, student and military programs sometimes take a percentage off games. Read the exclusions, because new releases are often left out.

## GTA 6 best deals: how to judge one

Run any offer through these four checks.

1. **Is it the real game?** "GTA 6 bundle," "GTA 6 collection" and "GTA 6 pack" listings sometimes turn out to be merchandise, a guide book, or an older GTA. Look for the platform (PS5 or Xbox Series X|S) and the edition name.
2. **Is it the right platform?** GTA 6 has no native version for PC, PS4, Xbox One or phones. Xbox One owners who buy it can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming, but that's the official Xbox service, not a separate "Xbox One edition" for sale. A "PC key" for GTA 6 is fake, full stop, because Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version.
3. **What's the real saving?** A $10 gift card with a $79.99 game is a 12.5 percent saving. A "free" accessory you'd never buy is worth nothing.
4. **Who's selling it?** Sold and shipped by the retailer is safe. A third-party marketplace seller with a new account and a price far below everyone else is not.

## What to skip

This is the part that costs people money every big launch.

- **Grey-market key sites.** Resold codes for console games can be region locked, already used, or bought with stolen cards and later revoked. If the account gets flagged, the "deal" is gone and so is your money.
- **"GTA 6 mobile" or "GTA 6 PC download" ads.** These are scams. The game is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Our [mobile page](https://callunc.com/mobile/) covers how to spot them.
- **Account sharing offers.** Sites selling "shared" console accounts with GTA 6 on them break the store rules and can get you locked out.
- **Too-good resale boxes.** Sealed boxed copies sold online for well under retail are a red flag, since the code inside may have been redeemed.

## GTA 6 discount: worth waiting?

If you were going to buy it anyway and you're eager to play, waiting for a big discount isn't much of a plan. Nobody can tell you when the first real price cut will come, and Rockstar and the stores haven't said. A gift card bonus or a retailer reward is about the best you can count on this Black Friday, and if a store does cut the price, it'll be obvious against $79.99.

If you're on the fence, there's no harm in waiting either. The game isn't going anywhere, and you'll have more information later: Rockstar's patch notes, what it says about GTA 6 Online, and any edition changes. Our [price answer](https://callunc.com/price/) stays current if anything moves.

The cheapest GTA 6 is still the one you only buy once. Buy from a store you trust, on the platform you play, and ignore anything that sounds like a secret.
