No, you don't need GTA 6 PS Plus or an Xbox subscription to play the game. GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026, as a single-player game, and the story mode doesn't sit behind a membership. You buy the game, you play it. Where a subscription usually comes in is online multiplayer, and Rockstar hasn't announced a date for GTA 6 Online.

Here's the longer version, with the current plan names so you don't buy the wrong thing.

## Do you need PS Plus for GTA 6 single-player?

No. PlayStation Plus is Sony's membership for online multiplayer, monthly games, cloud saves and a game catalog. Single-player games don't need it, and nothing Rockstar or Sony published says GTA 6's story is an exception.

You will want an internet connection at times, at least to download the game and its updates, since the boxed copy is a download code rather than a disc. But being online and having PS Plus aren't the same thing. Our [disc page](https://callunc.com/disc/) covers the code in the box.

## GTA 6 Xbox Live: what's it called now?

"Xbox Live Gold" is gone. Microsoft folded online multiplayer into Game Pass plans, and the names changed again in 2025. On Xbox's own plan page, the plans that list online console multiplayer are:

- **Game Pass Essential**
- **Game Pass Premium**
- **Game Pass Ultimate**

PC Game Pass doesn't list console online multiplayer, so it won't help on an Xbox.

As with PlayStation, none of this is needed for GTA 6's single-player story on Xbox Series X|S.

## Is GTA 6 on Game Pass or PS Plus?

Not at launch. Reports say GTA 6 isn't coming to Game Pass on day one, and Rockstar hasn't announced otherwise. It isn't in the PlayStation Plus catalog either. Having Game Pass Ultimate or PS Plus Premium doesn't get you the game for free. Our [Game Pass answer](https://callunc.com/game-pass/) stays current if that changes.

So subscriptions don't save you money on GTA 6 itself. You pay $79.99 for Standard or $99.99 for Ultimate (US prices), with or without a membership.

## GTA 6 online subscription: what to expect

This is the part nobody can answer for certain yet.

- **Rockstar hasn't announced GTA 6 Online.** Rockstar described the launch game as a single-player experience and hasn't given a date or window for multiplayer.
- **2027 is a report, not a promise.** A Bloomberg report pointed to 2027 for online, and Vice covered it as tentative. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.
- **Console online play normally needs a membership.** Sony's page says online multiplayer is included with all PlayStation Plus memberships. Xbox lists it under Essential, Premium and Ultimate. Unless Rockstar and the platform holders do something unusual, a future GTA 6 Online on console would most likely follow the same rule.

That last point is the pattern, not an announcement. Some games are exceptions, especially free-to-play ones. Until Rockstar says how GTA 6 Online will be sold and accessed, don't buy a subscription in advance just for it. Our [online answer](https://callunc.com/online/) tracks any news.

## Which PS Plus tier would you need?

If GTA 6 Online follows the usual rule, the cheapest tier is enough. On Sony's current lineup that's:

1. **PlayStation Plus Essential:** online multiplayer, monthly games, cloud saves
2. **PlayStation Plus Extra:** Essential plus a catalog of games
3. **PlayStation Plus Premium:** Extra plus classics, streaming and trials

The higher tiers add more games to play, not better access to online. Prices change and vary by region, so check Sony's page in your country.

## Common mix-ups

A few questions come up a lot:

- **"My PS Plus ran out, will my GTA 6 save disappear?"** Your local save stays on your console. What PS Plus adds is cloud save backup, so if you rely on that, keep an eye on it.
- **"Do I need a subscription to download updates?"** No. Game patches download without a membership.
- **"Someone's selling a shared account with GTA 6 and PS Plus."** Skip it. Account sharing breaks store rules and can get the account locked.

Bottom line for most people: buy the game, skip the subscription unless you already use it for other games, and wait for Rockstar to say how GTA 6 Online works before paying for anything extra.
