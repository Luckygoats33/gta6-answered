Leonida is the fictional state GTA 6 takes place in, and the GTA 6 Leonida map is Rockstar's version of Florida. Vice City is its big city, but it's only one of six regions, with islands, wetlands, a struggling coastal town, industrial country and a national park spread across the rest of the state. Rockstar's own tagline calls it "the darkest side of the sunniest place in America," which is about as Florida as a sentence gets.

GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026, so you can explore it yourself now. This guide sticks to what Rockstar said and showed before launch, so you know which parts came from the source and which came from fan maps.

## What Leonida is

In older GTA games, the setting was a city (Vice City, Liberty City) or a city plus countryside (San Andreas, Los Santos and Blaine County). GTA 6 goes up a level: the setting is a whole state, and the story moves across it. The Xbox Store description puts it plainly: Jason and Lucia get caught in a criminal conspiracy that stretches across Leonida after a job goes wrong.

How big is it? Rockstar's Aaron Garbut told Game Informer, as reported by VGC, that Leonida is Rockstar's biggest world by some distance, about twice the size of GTA V's map. Rockstar hasn't published an official full map with exact measurements. Our [map size page](https://callunc.com/map-size/) has the short version.

## The six regions of the GTA 6 Leonida map

Rockstar's region names showed up on its official site and in the file names of its screenshots. VGC listed them when the second trailer's screenshots came out in May 2025:

1. **Vice City.** The neon center of everything, and Rockstar's take on Miami.
2. **Leonida Keys.** The island chain south of the mainland. Rockstar's character page says Jason has been working for local drug runners in the Keys.
3. **Grassrivers.** The wetlands, Leonida's answer to the Everglades. Think fan boats and the alligator on the cover art.
4. **Port Gellhorn.** A coastal town that has seen better days.
5. **Ambrosia.** The industrial side of the state.
6. **Mount Kalaga National Park.** The wilder, natural side of Leonida.

Rockstar also mentions a few smaller places on its site, like the Leonida Penitentiary, where Lucia served her time.

## Vice City

Vice City is where most of the city life happens: nightlife, music, money and the people chasing all three. Rockstar's "Only in Leonida" page introduces characters tied to the city, like Boobie Ike, who has a hand in real estate, a strip club and a record label, and Dre'Quan Priest, the music executive who signed the Real Dimez. If you played the 2002 game, the name is familiar. This is a modern version, built around social media and influencers as much as pastel suits.

How Leonida and Vice City relate is simple: Vice City is a city inside Leonida, the way Miami is a city inside Florida. "Vice City, USA" is how Rockstar's store copy describes the setting.

## Outside the city: Keys, swamps and mountains

The rest of Leonida is where GTA 6 differs most from GTA V:

- **The Keys** bring boats, bars and smuggling. Rockstar's character notes say Jason lives rent-free at a boat yard run by an older smuggler named Brian, in exchange for helping with shakedowns.
- **Grassrivers** is the swamp country. The Extended Look gameplay video showed a fan boat on Everglades-style marshland, the most "only in Florida" scenery in the game.
- **Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia** fill in the working, rough-edged parts of the state, the places tourists drive past.
- **Mount Kalaga** is the national park, the closest thing Leonida has to GTA V's backcountry, minus the desert.

Rockstar also told Game Informer, per VGC, that the weather system is more localized, so one part of the state can be clear while another gets hammered by a storm.

## Leonida's real-life inspiration

Rockstar never names Florida on its own pages, but it doesn't need to. VGC's write-up of the Game Informer preview ties each region to a real counterpart: Vice City to Miami, the Keys to the Florida Keys, Grassrivers to the Everglades, Port Gellhorn to Florida's fading tourist towns, Ambrosia to the state's industrial areas, and Mount Kalaga to the natural landscapes near the Florida-Georgia border.

A word on the extras people search for, like a Leonida flag, state seal or license plate: plenty of designs float around online, and most are fan-made. If you want the real thing, check Rockstar's official Media & Artwork section on the GTA VI site, or look closely in the game itself.
