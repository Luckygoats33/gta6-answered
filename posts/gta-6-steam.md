GTA 6 is not on Steam. Rockstar released it on November 19, 2026, for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and as of November 16, 2026, it hadn't announced a PC version at all. No PC version means no Steam page, no Epic Games Store listing and no Steam Deck support. If you see any of those, it's fake. Here's what's actually known, and what Rockstar's history suggests about the wait.

## Why GTA 6 isn't on PC yet

Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, has been open that this is deliberate. In a Bloomberg interview in May 2026, CEO Strauss Zelnick said Rockstar always starts on console, because console players are the core audience for a release like this and need to be served first. He didn't give a PC date, and he didn't rule PC out.

That's the official position: console first, PC not announced. Anything more specific, like "PC in early 2027" or "PC in 2028," is an outlet or a fan guessing. Our [PC page](https://callunc.com/pc/) is updated if that changes.

## How long GTA V and RDR2 took to reach PC

Rockstar's track record is the best guide we have. It has a long habit of launching on consoles and bringing PC later:

- **GTA IV** came out on consoles in April 2008 and reached PC in December 2008.
- **GTA V** launched on PS3 and Xbox 360 in September 2013, came to PS4 and Xbox One in late 2014, and hit PC in April 2015. That's about a year and a half after the original release.
- **Red Dead Redemption 2** launched on PS4 and Xbox One in October 2018. The PC version arrived in November 2019, first on the Rockstar Games Launcher and Epic, then on Steam in December 2019. Roughly a year.

So the pattern says a PC version is likely at some point, and the gap has been anywhere from about eight months to a year and a half. But a pattern isn't an announcement. Rockstar could do it differently this time, and it hasn't said anything.

## GTA 6 on Steam Deck

Not possible. The Steam Deck plays PC games, and there's no PC version of GTA 6. When one does come, whether it runs well on handheld PC hardware is a separate question. Rob Nelson of Rockstar North said before launch that the team rebuilt the vehicle physics from scratch for current consoles, and Rockstar's target is 30 fps on every console, PS5 Pro included. That gives you a sense of how demanding it is.

If you want to play GTA 6 on a handheld, there is no official route yet. PlayStation's Remote Play can stream from a PS5 you own to other devices, but that's streaming your console, not running the game on the device. Xbox Cloud Gaming doesn't help here either: owners can stream GTA 6 to Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, but it does not stream to PC at launch.

## GTA 6 on Epic Games or the Rockstar launcher

Same answer as Steam: no PC version, so no listing. When Rockstar brought RDR2 to PC, it sold the game first through its own Rockstar Games Launcher and Epic, with Steam a month behind. Rockstar hasn't said how a GTA 6 PC release would be sold, if and when it happens.

## Watch out for fake Steam pages and PC downloads

This is the part that actually costs people money. Since there's no PC version, every "GTA 6 PC download," "Steam key," "repack" or "early access demo" is a scam.

Malwarebytes documented one in August 2026: fake sites dressed up as Rockstar, copying the real Extended Look promotion, with a "Play Now" button. The download was a file of about 1 MB, which no modern game could fit in, and it was an infostealer that grabs saved passwords and session cookies from your browsers.

How to stay safe:

- **No PC version exists.** Anything claiming otherwise is fake by definition.
- **Check the file size.** A game this big is hundreds of gigabytes, not a few megabytes.
- **Don't buy "keys" from resellers.** There are no GTA 6 PC keys to sell.
- **If you ran something,** scan your PC, then change your important passwords from a different device and sign out of all sessions on your accounts. Session cookies can let an attacker in even after a password change.

## What PC players can do now

If you're PC-only, your options are waiting, or picking up a console. GTA 6 runs on the Xbox Series S, the entry-level current Xbox, and on every PS5 model. The [consoles page](https://callunc.com/consoles/) covers which ones.

If you're waiting, keep an eye on Rockstar's Newswire, not on YouTube thumbnails. A PC announcement would come from Rockstar or Take-Two first.
